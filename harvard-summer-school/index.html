费用包含什么？What is included?

住宿项目包括学费、住宿、餐饮及项目费；Pre-College 还包括两周意外与疾病保险。线上／走读的标价已含 $200 项目费，不需重复加收。$75 申请费不退。Residential prices include tuition, housing, meals and the program fee. Pre-College also includes two weeks of accident and sickness insurance. Online/commuting prices already include the $200 program fee. The $75 application fee is nonrefundable.

另外预算：书本、学习用品、交通、个人开销及适用的保险或签证费用。走读学生自行安排校外住宿和交通。Budget separately for books, supplies, travel, personal spending and any applicable insurance or visa costs. Commuters arrange their own off-campus accommodation and transportation.

有没有助学金？Is financial aid available?

有数量有限、按经济需要评估的奖学金，通常覆盖部分费用。官网资格包括美国公民、永久居民或 DACA 身份；一般国际学生不符合该校内资助资格。需在项目申请中同步申请资助并按时提交材料。Limited need-based scholarships are available and usually cover only part of the cost. Eligibility includes U.S. citizens, permanent residents and students with DACA status; other international students generally do not qualify for this institutional aid. Request aid within the program application and meet its deadlines.

怎样选，更适合孩子？Choose for the student

先选学习目标，再比较预算和时间投入。Start with the learning goal, then compare budget and time commitment.

Pre-College

只有两周，想体验校园住宿和大学课堂：优先了解 Pre-College。项目无大学学分，仍需认真参与课堂与活动。With two weeks available and an interest in campus living, explore Pre-College. It is noncredit, while still requiring serious participation in classes and activities.

SSP Online

希望在家修读大学课程并获得成绩／学分：优先比较 7 周线上项目。第一次可考虑一门基础匹配的 4 学分课。To study a college course from home for grades and credit, compare the 7-week online option. A first-time student might start with one 4-credit course that fits their preparation.

SSP Residential

希望住校并修学分：4 周适合集中读一门课；7 周适合安排更完整的学术体验与 8 学分课程。For campus living plus credit, four weeks allows a focused single-course experience; seven weeks provides a longer academic stay with an 8-credit course load.

夏校对大学录取和学分有什么意义？What does it mean for college admission and credit?

参加夏校不保证哈佛本科录取。SSP 提供大学课程字母成绩，可申请成绩单；学分能否转入未来大学由接收学校决定。Pre-College 不给大学学分，采用完成要求与否的评价。Summer-school participation does not guarantee admission to Harvard College. SSP provides letter-graded college coursework and transcripts on request; the receiving institution decides whether credits transfer. Pre-College is noncredit and evaluates whether requirements were met.
