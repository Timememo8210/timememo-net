先看这四点Four essentials

可以同时报美术与写作Art and writing can both be entered

13 岁及以上、7–12 年级；同一个账号可提交不同作品。Ages 13+, grades 7–12; enter different works through the same account.

不要求写长篇A novel is not required

短篇、个人随笔、诗歌均可。写作作品集仅限毕业年级。Short stories, personal essays and poems qualify. Portfolios are for graduating seniors only.

主要使用英文Primarily in English

下方 word count 指英文单词数，不是中文字数。诗歌按行数计算。Word limits count words, not Chinese characters. Poetry is measured in lines.

提交方法与时间How and when to enter

  1. 登录现有学生账号 → Add Work → 选择写作类别。Sign in to the existing student account → Add Work → choose a writing category.
  2. 在作品文本框输入或粘贴正文；不以 PDF、图片或外部链接替代正文。检查段落、诗歌换行及词数。Type or paste the manuscript into the work field. Do not substitute a PDF, image or link. Check paragraphs, line breaks and word count.
  3. 正文、标题页和页眉不写作者、学校等身份信息。非虚构中的普通人物使用化名；公众／历史人物例外。Remove identifying information from the manuscript, title page and headers. Use pseudonyms for private individuals in nonfiction; public and historical figures are exceptions.
  4. 填写作品来源／引用；同一作品只报一个类别。保留提纲、初稿和修改记录，最终作品必须由学生独立创作。Declare sources and citations; enter each work in only one category. Keep outlines, drafts and revisions. The final work must be the student’s own.
  5. 核查家长同意状态，完成提交及每件 15 美元费用；经济困难可申请费用豁免。Check guardian consent and complete submission with the $15 fee per work, or request a fee waiver for financial need.

评委看什么What judges evaluate

官方三项标准：原创性、技巧、个人声音或视角。匿名评审；地区 Gold Key 作品进入全国评审。The three official criteria are originality, skill and personal voice or vision. Judging is anonymous; regional Gold Key works advance to national judging.

阅读范文时，可看：细节是否具体，结构是否推进情节，语言是否符合人物，以及结尾如何回应开头。这些是阅读建议，不是官方评分权重。When reading examples, examine concrete details, narrative movement, character-appropriate language and how the ending responds to the opening. These are study suggestions, not official scoring weights.

“思乡”主题的准备方向Preparing a homesickness piece

优先考虑个人随笔／回忆录：选一件真实小事，例如一次与家人的通话、一顿饭、一件带回来的旧物，写场景、变化和自己的理解。可以先以 800–1,500 英文词为练习目标；这是准备建议，官方范围仍为 500–3,000 词。Consider a personal essay or memoir: start with a true incident, such as a family call, a meal or an object brought from home. Develop a scene, a change and your own interpretation. An 800–1,500-word draft is a suggested practice target; the official range remains 500–3,000 words.

如果更擅长虚构，就写短篇小说。保留每轮修改；参考获奖作品的技巧，由学生自己决定人物、情节与语言。写作提交的是完整作品，不是美术作品的设计说明。If fiction is a better fit, write a short story. Save revisions, study techniques in winning work, and let the student choose characters, plot and language. A writing entry is the work itself, not an artist’s statement.
