准备方向Creative direction

建议从学生能持续观察、独立表达的具体经历出发；题材与画面处理要有联系。以下为准备建议，不是官方选题要求。Start with experiences the student can observe and express independently, and connect the subject to visual decisions. These are preparation suggestions, not official topic requirements.

方向Direction具体练习Practical study对应案例Examples
家庭与生活细节Family & daily life

记录一顿饭、房间变化、长辈的手或兄弟姐妹互动；先自摄参考图。Observe a meal, a changing room, a relative’s hands or sibling interactions; take original reference photos.

Scarlett Cai / Jada McAliley
运动与伙伴Sports & peers

画训练前后、等待、协作和疲惫等具体瞬间；练习动作、空间和光线。Study waiting, teamwork or fatigue around practice; develop gesture, space and lighting.

Peize Wang
幽默与想象Humor & imagination

把熟悉的课堂或日常情境转成夸张叙事；练习构图与色彩节奏。Transform a familiar classroom or daily scene into an exaggerated narrative; study composition and color rhythm.

Jasmine Li
自然与材料Nature & materials

观察树皮、水流、侵蚀或植物生长；用纹理、结构和材料表现变化。Observe bark, water, erosion or plant growth; use texture, structure and materials to express change.

Lia Musser / Luke Haring

行动顺序：选 2–3 个具体题材 → 每个画 3 张构图草稿 → 选 1 个完成作品 → 保存修改过程 → 按官方规格拍摄。研究获奖作品的观察和表达方法，避免复制其构图、符号或个人故事。Sequence: choose 2–3 specific subjects → make 3 composition sketches for each → finish one work → retain revisions → photograph to the official specifications. Study observation and expression; do not copy compositions, symbols or personal stories.
