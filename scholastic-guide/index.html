仅检查像素数量；不上传图片或尺寸数据。清晰度、DPI、格式和文件大小需另行核对。Checks pixels only; no image or dimension data is uploaded. Check sharpness, DPI, format and file size separately.

像素／DPI／MB，是三件不同的事。Pixels, DPI and MB mean different things.

像素决定图像细节数量；DPI 表示打印密度；MB 是文件占用大小。官网建议图片约 3–5 MB，这不是明确的硬性上传上限。改 DPI 数字或把小图拉大，都不能找回原本缺失的细节。Pixels describe image detail; DPI describes print density; MB is file size. The FAQ suggests roughly 3–5 MB, not a stated hard upload cap. Changing DPI metadata or enlarging a small image cannot recover missing detail.

05

作品拍摄要求Artwork photography

01

用后置相机，保留原图Use the rear camera; keep the original

选择高分辨率。发文件时选原图，不用截图。Use high resolution. Share the original file, not a screenshot.

02

正对作品，避免透视歪斜Keep the camera parallel

让作品尽量占满画面，保留完整边缘。Fill the frame while retaining all edges of the artwork.

03

均匀柔光，避免反光Use even, diffuse light

避开强烈阴影。非作品组成部分的玻璃画框先移开。Avoid harsh shadows; remove reflective frames that are not part of the work.

04

裁背景，不拉伸画面Crop backgrounds; do not stretch

按原作品比例裁剪，之后再核对像素。Preserve the artwork’s aspect ratio and recheck dimensions after cropping.

05

忠实呈现，少做修饰Keep the image faithful

不要美化滤镜。扫描平面作品也可以。Avoid stylized filters. Scanning is also suitable for flat work.

06

留好过程与原件Keep the process and original

保留草稿、过程照或图层。实物不要在未获通知前寄出。Keep sketches, process photos or layers. Wait for instructions before shipping an original.

[2]

07

创作过程与原创性核验Process records & originality review

绘画／素描通常提交 1 张成品图。过程照片非统一必交，但官方建议保留；涉嫌抄袭或 AI 违规时可能索取。Painting / Drawing usually require one final image. Process photos are not universally required, but the organizer recommends keeping them and may request them when plagiarism or AI concerns arise.[1]

提交材料Submission

  • 成品：按所选类别提交；设计、装置等类别可能需要多角度或设计资料。Final work: follow category rules; design and installation categories may need multiple views or design documentation.

  • 过程：没有统一的全程录像或本人出镜要求；勿自行把草稿拼进单张成品图。Process: no universal requirement for full-length video or the student to appear on camera. Keep sketches separate from a single final image.

  • 来源：如实填写 Work Sources；不能冒用他人作品或提交临摹作品。Sources: complete Work Sources honestly; do not submit another person’s work or copied artwork.

  • AI：可用于允许的构思辅助；不得提交 AI 生成的最终作品，并须如实披露使用情况。AI: permitted process assistance may include brainstorming; AI-generated final work is ineligible. Disclose use honestly.

    [5]

核验方式Verification

  • 反向图片搜索：核查艺术作品与网上已有图片的相似性。Reverse image search checks art against images online.

  • 评委与赛区：标记、搜索并上报疑似违规作品。Jurors and regional programs flag, search for and report suspected violations.

  • 全国奖候选：复核版权及 AI 相关问题。National Medal candidates receive copyright review, including AI screening.

  • 调查：比较原作与疑似来源，查看引用和评委意见；必要时索取补充材料。最终决定由工作人员作出，不仅凭自动检测。Investigation: compare works and sources, review citations and juror notes, and request more information when needed. Staff make final decisions, not automated tools alone.

核验可能漏检；获奖后发现违规仍可调查、撤奖。Checks can miss violations; later discoveries may lead to investigation and revocation.

建议保存的材料Recommended records

以下为准备建议，不是官方统一提交清单或照片数量要求。These are preparation suggestions, not a universal submission list or required photo count.

材料Record保存内容What to keep
草稿Sketches构思小稿、底稿、修改稿。Concept sketches, underdrawings and revisions.
过程照片Process photos建议记录 4–6 个阶段：起稿、铺色、刻画、修改、完成。Suggested: 4–6 stages, from initial sketch through color, detail, revision and completion.
参考来源References自摄原图；其他资料的作者、链接和使用说明。Original reference photos; creators, links and usage notes for other sources.
原件／源文件Original / source files实体作品；数字作品的图层、原始文件和已有版本记录。Physical artwork; layered digital files and available version history.
