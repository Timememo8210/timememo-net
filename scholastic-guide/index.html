先了解比赛，再看当前进度The competition, and where things stand

这是提交原创艺术或写作作品的评奖活动，不是考场考试。基本资格为提交时满 13 岁、就读 7–12 年级，并在美国及符合条件的属地／军事基地或加拿大（魁北克除外）居住及就学。This is an original art and writing competition, not an exam. Entrants must be 13 or older at entry, in grades 7–12, and live and attend school in the United States and eligible territories or military bases, or Canada excluding Quebec.

作品说明，要写多少？How long should the statement be?

先分清官方要求和准备建议。字数指英文单词数。Separate official requirements from drafting advice. Counts refer to English words.

先写一份短草稿Start with a short draft

由孩子用自己的话写，选定类别后再调整。不是全体参赛作品的强制标准，也不保证每个类别都有说明栏。Let the teen draft in their own words and adapt after choosing a category. This is not a universal requirement or a promise that every category has a statement field.

01 / WHY

为什么想做这件作品？Why this work?

写真实的观察、经历或问题。Describe a real observation, experience or question.

02 / HOW

做了哪些设计选择？Which choices did you make?

颜色、构图、材料、结构，为什么这样安排？Why these colors, composition, materials or structure?

03 / MEANING

希望观众感受到什么？What should the viewer notice?

具体描述希望传达的感受或想法。Name the feeling or idea you hope to communicate.

标题、材料、作品实际尺寸和参考来源单独记录。说明不是英文写作参赛正文，不套用小说／散文的字数限制。Record title, materials, physical dimensions and sources separately. A work statement is not a writing-category entry; fiction and essay word limits do not apply.

04

照片到底要多大？How many pixels do you need?

最实用的一条：裁剪完成后的图片，两个边都至少 2700 像素。The practical rule: after cropping, both dimensions must be at least 2,700 pixels.

9 in×300 dpi=2700 px

这是由官方“打印短边至少 9 英寸、300 dpi”换算的像素门槛，不是原画必须有 9 英寸。This is calculated from the official 9-inch minimum printed shorter side at 300 dpi, not a minimum physical size for the original artwork.

只检查像素数量，不判断清晰度、dpi 元数据、文件大小、格式或创作合规。没有上传照片，也不向服务器发送尺寸。Checks pixel count only, not sharpness, DPI metadata, file size, format or originality. No photo upload; dimensions are not sent to a server.

像素／DPI／MB，是三件不同的事。Pixels, DPI and MB mean different things.

像素决定图像细节数量；DPI 表示打印密度；MB 是文件占用大小。官网建议图片约 3–5 MB，这不是明确的硬性上传上限。改 DPI 数字或把小图拉大，都不能找回原本缺失的细节。Pixels describe image detail; DPI describes print density; MB is file size. The FAQ suggests roughly 3–5 MB, not a stated hard upload cap. Changing DPI metadata or enlarging a small image cannot recover missing detail.

05

拍照时，照着这六步做Six steps to photograph the work

01

用后置相机，保留原图Use the rear camera; keep the original

选择高分辨率。发文件时选原图，不用截图。Use high resolution. Share the original file, not a screenshot.

02

正对作品，避免透视歪斜Keep the camera parallel

让作品尽量占满画面，保留完整边缘。Fill the frame while retaining all edges of the artwork.

03

均匀柔光，避免反光Use even, diffuse light

避开强烈阴影。非作品组成部分的玻璃画框先移开。Avoid harsh shadows; remove reflective frames that are not part of the work.

04

裁背景，不拉伸画面Crop backgrounds; do not stretch

按原作品比例裁剪，之后再核对像素。Preserve the artwork’s aspect ratio and recheck dimensions after cropping.

05

忠实呈现，少做修饰Keep the image faithful

不要美化滤镜。扫描平面作品也可以。Avoid stylized filters. Scanning is also suitable for flat work.

06

留好过程与原件Keep the process and original

保留草稿、过程照或图层。实物不要在未获通知前寄出。Keep sketches, process photos or layers. Wait for instructions before shipping an original.

[2]

07

把原创过程也保存下来Keep a record of the original process

做自己的作品Make it your own

从自己的想法出发，不能抄袭；诚实注明参考来源。不能把同一件作品重复投到多个类别。Start with your own ideas, avoid plagiarism and disclose sources. Do not enter one piece in multiple categories.

AI 辅助 ≠ AI 成品AI assistance ≠ an AI-made final work

官方允许构思等过程辅助，不接受 AI 生成的最终作品；提交时如实披露使用情况。The rules allow process assistance such as brainstorming, but not an AI-generated final work. Disclose use honestly.

旧作品先核对资格Check older works before reuse

并非必须新近创作。曾参加过该比赛的作品有重投限制；保留过程记录与此前参赛信息。Work need not be newly created. Previously entered work has re-entry restrictions; retain process records and prior entry details.
