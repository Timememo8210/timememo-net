AMY · MONEY SKILLS · 2026

自己的账户，
一步步建立信用。Your own money.
A thoughtful start to credit.

想自己管理钱，是个很好的开始。现在能做的事很明确：与家长共同开户，自己管理日常用钱；信用记录另外规划。

Wanting to manage your money is a good start. The practical plan is simple: open a joint bank account with a parent for everyday money, and handle credit building separately.

官方规则复核 / Official rules checked: 2026-11-02 · 美国 / United States

01 / THE ANSWERS

先回答两个问题Two clear answers

ACCOUNT / 账户

13 岁可以有自己使用的账户。

Yes to an account you use yourself.

在本页比较的 Bank of America 和 Chase 产品中，你需要与家长共同持有。可以有自己的借记卡和登录，但不是你一个人独立持有。 [1] [2]

For the Bank of America and Chase products below, a parent must co-own the account at age 13. You can have your own debit card and sign-in, but you are not the sole owner. [1] [2]

CREDIT / 信用

现有运通附属卡，现在不报送信用。

The Amex additional card does not report yet.

运通在附属持卡人 18 岁起才报送征信信息。现在可练习用卡，但不能当作正在累积已报送的信用记录；18 岁以后可以有建信帮助。 [3]

Amex starts credit-bureau reporting for additional card members at 18. The card can teach spending habits now, but it is not currently building a reported credit history. It may help with credit after 18. [3]

记住：自己的登录 ≠ 单独拥有账户。普通借记卡消费 ≠ 积累信用。Credit Score 是信用评分，不是消费奖励积分。 [6]

Remember: your own sign-in does not mean sole ownership. Ordinary debit-card spending does not build credit. A credit score is different from reward points. [6]

02 / OUR PROPOSAL

接下来，就做这三步Three next steps

  1. 先选一个共同银行账户

    Choose one joint bank account

    优先选家里已有、方便使用的银行：BOA SafeBalance 或 Chase High School Checking。家长陪同预约网点，确认 Amy 是共同持有人，并核对证件、费用、自己的登录和借记卡。BOA 的 SafeBalance for Family Banking 是家长独有账户，名称相近，别混淆。 [1] [2]

    Prefer the bank the family already uses: BOA SafeBalance or Chase High School Checking. Visit a branch together; confirm Amy is a co-owner, required ID, fees, her sign-in and debit card. BOA's similarly named SafeBalance for Family Banking is parent-owned, a different arrangement. [1] [2]

  2. 信用目标单独核实

    Check the credit-building route separately

    若希望 18 岁前提前准备，向 Capital One 核对具体卡是否报送 13 岁授权用户、报送哪些机构、开户日期如何记录、是否收费。优先查家长已有卡；确认后再决定是否添加。只在按时还款、低余额的账户上考虑这一步。 [5]

    If early credit building matters, ask Capital One about the specific card: reporting for a 13-year-old, which bureaus, the reported opening date, and fees. Check an existing parent card first, then decide whether to add Amy. Consider only an account with on-time payments and low balances. [5]

  3. 再决定运通附属卡去留

    Then decide whether to keep the Amex card

    如果唯一目的是现在积累信用，无需为这个目的保留。先核对是否有未结交易、附属卡费用及其他用途，再由家长决定是否取消 Amy 的附属卡。18 岁后仍可重新评估建信需要。 [3]

    If its only purpose is building credit right now, there is no need to keep it for that purpose. Check pending charges, additional-card fees and other uses before a parent decides whether to remove Amy's additional card. Reassess credit needs at 18. [3]

以上为建议，尚未开户、添加授权用户或取消任何卡。银行开户与信用卡申请是两件事。

This is a proposal. No account has been opened, no authorized user added, and no card canceled. Bank-account opening and credit-card applications are separate.

产品规则对照Compare the product rules

Bank of America
Advantage SafeBalance Banking

13 岁与家长共同持有；自己的借记卡、网银登录。16 岁起可申请单独持有，不是 15 岁。普通使用不积累信用。 [1] [6]

Joint with a parent at 13, with your own debit card and online access. Sole ownership is available from 16, not 15, subject to approval. Ordinary use does not build credit. [1] [6]

Chase
High School Checking

13～17 岁与家长共同开户，关联家长符合条件的 Chase checking。自己的借记卡及网上银行；普通使用不积累信用。 [2] [6]

For ages 13–17, co-owned with a parent and linked to the parent's eligible Chase checking account. Includes a debit card and online banking. Ordinary use does not build credit. [2] [6]

Amex / Chase
信用卡附属用户Credit-card authorized user

运通等到 18 岁报送；Chase 官方也明确不报送未成年附属用户的信用历史。因此换成 Chase 附属信用卡不能解决现在的建信目标。 [3] [4]

Amex waits until 18 to report. Chase also says it does not report minors' authorized-user credit history. Switching to a Chase authorized-user credit card would not meet the current credit-building goal. [3] [4]

Capital One
信用卡附属用户，待具体核对Credit-card authorized user: investigate

官方表示添加孩子无最低年龄要求，会报送授权用户活动。良好使用可能帮助建立信用；仍属于家长信用账户，主卡人负责还款，不保证立即有分数。 [5]

Capital One says there is no minimum age for adding a child and it reports authorized-user activity. Responsible use may help establish credit. It remains the parent's credit account; the primary holder pays. An immediate score is not guaranteed. [5]

还有三点需要知道Three useful details

  • 没有“所有银行统一到 15 岁就独立”的规则。本页结论限于列出的产品；如果 Amy 看到另一款具体产品，可以按其官方条款再核对。

    There is no universal “independent at 15” rule. These conclusions apply to the named products. If Amy has another specific product in mind, check its official terms.

  • 银行报送记录，不等于现在就能查到评分。Experian 表示不展示未满 18 岁的普通信用报告；此前已报送的记录可在 18 岁后显示。也不能仅凭运通卡上的 Member Since，推定会获得五年征信历史。 [7] [3]

    Reporting does not mean a score is visible today. Experian says ordinary credit reports are not displayed for consumers under 18; previously reported history can appear at 18. Do not assume Amex's “Member Since” guarantees five years of reported history. [7] [3]

  • 13 岁不能申请通常意义上自己作为主卡人的信用卡；18～20 岁仍有独立还款能力或合资格共同签署要求。无需为了评分多消费、欠款或付利息。 [4] [8] [9]

    At 13, you cannot get a standard primary credit card of your own. Ages 18–20 still face independent ability-to-pay or qualifying co-signer requirements. You do not need to spend more, carry debt or pay interest to build credit. [4] [8] [9]
