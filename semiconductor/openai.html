OpenAI Jalapeño 推理芯片：从模型公司到系统级芯片共同设计者Jalapeño: from model company to system-level silicon co-designer

2026 年 6 月，OpenAI 公布首款自研“Intelligence Processor” Jalapeño：由 OpenAI 定义和设计、Broadcom 完成硅实现与网络、Celestica 集成板卡和机架，台积电负责制造。工程样片已运行 GPT‑5.3‑Codex‑Spark，部署目标为 2026 年底。In June 2026 OpenAI unveiled Jalapeño, its first custom “Intelligence Processor”: OpenAI defines and designs it, Broadcom implements the silicon and networking, Celestica integrates boards and racks, and TSMC manufactures it. Engineering samples are running GPT‑5.3‑Codex‑Spark, with deployment targeted by end-2026.

核心结论Bottom line

Jalapeño 已越过“计划”阶段，但尚未越过量产与规模部署验证。Jalapeño has moved beyond “plan,” but not yet beyond volume-production and scaled-deployment proof.

OpenAI 已确认样片在目标频率与功耗下运行真实模型，并给出 2026 年底部署目标；但尚未披露芯片面积、晶体管数、HBM 数量、精度、吞吐、延迟、单芯片功耗、良率或总产量。公开资料只能确认方向和产业链，不能据此建立完整性能/成本模型。OpenAI confirms that samples run real models at target frequency and power and targets deployment by end-2026. It has not disclosed die size, transistor count, HBM count, precision, throughput, latency, per-chip power, yield or total units. Public evidence establishes direction and partners, not a full performance/cost model.

Jalapeño

为 OpenAI 推理负载“从白纸开始”设计Designed from a blank sheet for OpenAI inference workloads

OpenAI 把它称为第一款“Intelligence Processor”，定位是大型语言模型推理，而不是对外销售的通用 GPU。设计重点是减少数据搬运，让计算、内存和网络在机架尺度更平衡。OpenAI calls it its first “Intelligence Processor,” aimed at large-language-model inference rather than sale as a general-purpose GPU. The design prioritizes less data movement and a better rack-scale balance of compute, memory and networking.

10GW 是系统部署能力计划，不是芯片 TDP 相除就能得到的精确芯片数量。实际数量取决于加速器功耗、每机架配置、冗余、利用率、网络和数据中心基础设施。10GW is a system-deployment capacity plan, not an exact chip count obtained by dividing by chip TDP. Units depend on accelerator power, rack configuration, redundancy, utilization, networking and data-center infrastructure.

制造难题Manufacturing challenges

真正的瓶颈是“计算裸片 + HBM + 封装 + 网络 + 电力”的同步爬坡The real bottleneck is synchronized ramp of compute die, HBM, package, network and power

1 · 先进节点良率Advanced-node yield

大面积 AI 裸片对随机缺陷敏感；冗余单元、版图分区、频率分档和 DFT 决定可售良率。OpenAI 未披露裸片大小，因此不能计算单片成本。Large AI dies are sensitive to random defects; redundancy, floorplanning, binning and DFT determine sellable yield. OpenAI has not disclosed die size, so per-chip cost cannot be calculated.

2 · HBM 容量与带宽capacity & bandwidth

推理的权重、KV cache 和批处理对容量/带宽提出不同要求。HBM 堆栈数、代际和已知良品裸片供应会影响封装良率与交付。Weights, KV cache and batching place different demands on capacity and bandwidth. HBM stack count, generation and known-good-die supply affect package yield and delivery.

3 · 2.5D/3D 先进封装advanced packaging

中介层/桥接、基板、热设计和封装测试必须与晶圆同步。即使计算裸片充足，封装产能也可能限制整机出货。Interposers/bridges, substrates, thermals and package test must ramp with wafers. Packaging can cap system shipments even when compute dies are available.

4 · 网络与机架Network & rack

推理系统需要高带宽 Ethernet/SerDes、交换芯片、光模块、供电和液冷。OpenAI/Broadcom 强调减少数据搬运，说明系统互连是架构核心。Inference systems need high-bandwidth Ethernet/SerDes, switches, optics, power and liquid cooling. OpenAI/Broadcom’s focus on data movement makes interconnect architectural, not peripheral.

5 · 软件成熟度Software maturity

编译器、算子、调度、容错和模型量化决定实际利用率。样片跑通一个模型与生产环境稳定支持模型组合之间仍有距离。Compilers, kernels, scheduling, fault tolerance and quantization determine utilization. Running one model on samples is not the same as stable production support for a model fleet.

6 · 迭代节奏Cadence

模型每几个月变化，而芯片从架构到量产通常跨年。可编程性、内存余量和多代兼容性决定自研芯片是否会过早固化。Models evolve in months while chips take years from architecture to volume. Programmability, memory headroom and multi-generation compatibility determine obsolescence risk.
