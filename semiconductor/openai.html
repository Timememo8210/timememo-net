更新至Updated 2026-07-25工程样片Engineering samples首次部署目标First deployment target · 2026 EOY

老板先看：五条结论Executive brief: five conclusions

  1. Jalapeño 已有工程样片并运行 GPT‑5.3‑Codex‑Spark，但尚未量产；OpenAI 未公开 tokens/s、延迟、TDP、成本、裸片面积或良率。Jalapeño has engineering samples running GPT‑5.3‑Codex‑Spark but is not in volume production. OpenAI has not disclosed tokens/s, latency, TDP, cost, die area or yield.
  2. TSMC 制造和 3nm/HBM 来自 Reuters；OpenAI 官宣没有确认具体节点、HBM 代际/数量或封装。Jalapeño 使用 hybrid bonding 没有公开证据。TSMC manufacturing and 3nm/HBM come from Reuters. OpenAI has not confirmed the exact node, HBM generation/count or package. There is no public evidence that Jalapeño uses hybrid bonding.
  3. 第一代制造平台已关闭，不应以“换节点”评估；优先窗口是 2027–29 后续代、I/O/network tile、base die 和先进封装。Gen1’s manufacturing platform is closed and should not be evaluated as a node switch. Priority windows are 2027–29 follow-on generations, I/O/network tiles, base die and advanced packaging.
  4. MI455X 等具体竞争标杆已升至 2nm+3nm、HBM4、20TB/s 以上和 3D hybrid-bonded compute dies；这不是所有后续产品的硬性需求，但必须用真实 workload 解释为何可以低于该基准。Specific benchmarks such as MI455X have moved to 2nm+3nm, HBM4, more than 20TB/s and 3D hybrid-bonded compute dies. This is not mandatory for every follow-on product, but any lower target must be justified on real workloads.
  5. 结论：当前代不追；2026 H2 起推动后续代 PDK/IP/封装共研。14A + 18A‑PT/HBI 的技术匹配度高，但只有在 HBM4、SerDes、封装良率与 Broadcom IP 移植同时成立时才可进入产品。Verdict: do not chase Gen1. Begin follow-on PDK/IP/package co-design in 2H26. 14A plus 18A‑PT/HBI fits technically, but only if HBM4, SerDes, package yield and Broadcom IP porting all close.

决策Decision

为什么做、为什么不做Why engage, why not

做：未来代Engage: future gen

需求规模足以摊薄高 NREDemand can amortize high NRE

10GW、多代、内部可预测推理负载，具备模型—芯片—网络—机架联合优化价值。TSMC 先进封装持续紧张也形成供应分散动因。A 10GW multi-generation program and predictable internal inference load support model-to-rack co-optimization. Tight advanced-packaging supply also creates a diversification trigger.

不做：第一代主 dieNo-go: Gen1 main die

样片已出，迁移会重置时间表Samples exist; migration resets schedule

切换流程意味着重做物理实现、SRAM、HBM/SerDes PHY、封装、软件和系统验证，无法保持 2026 年底部署。A process switch means redoing physical implementation, SRAM, HBM/SerDes PHYs, package, software and system qualification—breaking the end-2026 target.

优先次序Priority sequence

① 18A‑PT/Foveros/HBM 技术验证 → ② I/O/network/base die → ③ 18A‑P 后续 derivative → ④ 14A 计算 tile。不要从第一代大型 compute die 的双流程制造开始。1) 18A‑PT/Foveros/HBM proof, 2) I/O/network/base die, 3) 18A‑P follow-on derivative, 4) 14A compute tile. Do not start with dual-process manufacturing of the Gen1 large compute die.

为什么做Why use it

去除焊球，缩小 pitch，提高单位面积 I/O；降低 RC、延迟与 pJ/bit；适合 logic-on-logic、stacked cache、base-die/compute 和突破光罩限制。Eliminates solder bumps, tightens pitch and raises I/O density; lowers RC, latency and pJ/bit; enables logic-on-logic, stacked cache, base-die/compute and reticle-scale escape.

为什么不轻易做Why not by default

颗粒、平整度、对准、KGD、复合良率、翘曲、测试和不可返修风险上升；3D 堆叠还会叠加热点和热路径。Particles, planarity, alignment, KGD, compound yield, warpage, test and non-repairability risk all rise; 3D stacking also compounds hotspots and thermal paths.

BSPDN 热结论：BSPDN thermal conclusion: “背面供电一定更热”不成立。PowerVia 降低 IR drop 和正面拥塞；风险来自薄硅、背面金属、3D 堆叠和热点的系统组合。Intel 在 2026 VLSI 公告中称 18A‑P 的热阻改善 20–40%；这是 vendor-stated 的工艺结果，不等于大 AI die + HBM 系统实测。最终必须用目标 die、封装和真实推理负载做热—电—机械联合验证。“Backside power always runs hotter” is incorrect. Use critical-IP availability roughly 18 months before architecture freeze as a proposed internal planning line; otherwise limit work to package or I/O proofs. Confirm this timeline against the actual design window.
