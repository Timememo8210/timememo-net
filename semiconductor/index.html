本周五条结论Five conclusions this week

  1. Phison 当前高端与主流 Gen5 控制器 E28/E37T 均为台积电 N6；PCIe 6.0 X3 的媒体时间表为 2026 年底样片、2027 年中量产，节点未公开。Phison’s current high-end and mainstream Gen5 controllers, E28/E37T, both use TSMC N6. Media reports place PCIe 6.0 X3 samples at end-2026 and volume in mid-2027; its node is undisclosed.
  2. Phison 的近期主芯片窗口偏弱：N6 成本/生态成熟，X3 平台基本锁定；18A 的技术价值更可能出现在 X3 后继的企业控制器，而不是低价客户端产品。Phison’s near-term main-die window is weak: N6 economics/ecosystem are mature and X3 is substantially locked. 18A is more relevant to an enterprise successor after X3 than low-cost client parts.
  3. OpenAI Jalapeño 已有工程样片，首代制造平台已经关闭；真正的评估窗口是 10GW 多代计划中的第二、三代，决定因素是 HBM、封装、SerDes、良率与系统软件，而不只是逻辑节点。OpenAI Jalapeño is in engineering samples, so Gen1’s platform is closed. The real evaluation window is Gen2/3 of the 10GW program, where HBM, packaging, SerDes, yield and software matter as much as the logic node.
  4. Hybrid bonding 不是所有 HBM 加速器的必选项；它在 3D 逻辑堆叠、base-die/compute 直连和极高 I/O 密度时才成为关键。Intel 18A-PT 已公开 <5µm HBI，TSMC SoIC 以 sub-10µm 起步并已在 2025 年量产 3nm 堆叠。Hybrid bonding is not mandatory for every HBM accelerator. It becomes critical for 3D logic stacking, base-die/compute integration and extreme I/O density. Intel 18A-PT discloses <5µm HBI; TSMC SoIC starts below 10µm and put 3nm stacking into volume production in 2025.
  5. Anthropic 仍没有已公布的自研芯片、团队或节点；近期价值在架构共研，真正的晶圆需求只有在“是否自研”立项后才成立，时间更可能落在 2029 前后。Anthropic still has no announced custom chip, team or node. Near-term value lies in architecture co-development; a wafer opportunity exists only after a build decision, with timing more plausibly around 2029.

#2 · ANTHROPIC · 25%

战略中高；项目确定性最低Medium-high priority; lowest certainty

现在只做架构与 TCO 塑形。专门硅负责人、ASIC/EDA 合作、HBM/SerDes 招聘或 PDK 信号出现后，立即升级为 Pursue now。Limit current work to architecture and TCO shaping. Upgrade to Pursue now when a silicon lead, ASIC/EDA partner, HBM/SerDes hiring or PDK signal appears.

#3 · PHISON · 15%

近期主 die 价值低；未来条件式Low near-term main-die value; future conditional
