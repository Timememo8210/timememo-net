REPORT 03 · AI COMPUTE STRATEGY

Anthropic 多芯片计算战略与自研芯片的真实进度Multi-silicon compute strategy and the real status of custom chips

截至 2026 年 7 月，Anthropic 没有已公布、已流片或已选定节点的自研芯片。Reuters 的最新信息是“正在评估”：没有专门芯片团队、没有具体设计，也可能继续采购而不自研。与此同时，公司已通过 AWS、Google、NVIDIA 与 AMD 建立跨平台的数 GW 算力组合。As of July 2026, Anthropic has no announced, taped-out or node-selected custom chip. Reuters’ latest information is an “evaluation”: no dedicated chip team, no concrete design, and the company may continue buying rather than building. In parallel, Anthropic has assembled a multi-gigawatt, multi-platform compute portfolio across AWS, Google, NVIDIA and AMD.

核心结论Bottom line

Anthropic 目前最重要的“芯片战略”是跨平台共同优化，不是已经存在的自研芯片。Anthropic’s most important “chip strategy” today is cross-platform co-optimization—not a custom chip that already exists.

公司一边向 AWS Trainium、Google TPU、NVIDIA GPU 和 AMD Instinct 分散供给，一边把 Claude 的编译器、内核和模型适配深入到供应商平台。若未来自研，最合理的第一步是针对大规模推理的专用加速器；但这只是基于工作负载经济性的研究判断，不是公司公布的路线。The company diversifies supply across AWS Trainium, Google TPU, NVIDIA GPU and AMD Instinct while pushing Claude compiler, kernel and model optimization into each platform. If it eventually builds silicon, the most rational first step is a large-scale inference accelerator—but that is a workload-economics inference, not a disclosed roadmap.

An advanced AI chip typically takes years and roughly $500 million in design expense.

以下全部是研究判断，不是 Anthropic 路线图：Everything below is research analysis, not an Anthropic roadmap: 推理流量更稳定、规模更可预测，能围绕 Claude 的权重格式、KV cache、低延迟和批处理优化；训练则需要更强的通用性、集群容错和快速跟随新模型架构。Inference traffic is steadier and more predictable, enabling optimization around Claude weight formats, KV cache, low latency and batching. Training demands greater generality, cluster resilience and rapid adaptation to new model architectures.

HBM 与封装and packaging

推理模型参数和 KV cache 使内存容量、带宽、封装面积与良品组合成为一等公民；逻辑晶圆够用不代表系统能出货。Model weights and KV cache make memory capacity, bandwidth, package area and known-good-die assembly first-class; logic-wafer supply alone does not ship systems.

互连Interconnect

机内 scale-up 与机间 scale-out 需要高速 SerDes、交换、光学和集体通信软件；低利用率会吞噬节点节省的功耗。Intra-rack scale-up and inter-rack scale-out require fast SerDes, switching, optics and collective software; poor utilization can erase node-level power gains.

芯片到机架验证Chip-to-rack validation

需要从晶圆测试、封装测试、板卡、电源和液冷到模型精度回归的完整质量闭环。A complete quality loop must span wafer test, package test, boards, power, liquid cooling and model-accuracy regression.
