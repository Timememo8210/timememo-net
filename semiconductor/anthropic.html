Anthropic 多供应商算力、潜在自研芯片与进入窗口Multi-vendor compute, potential custom silicon and the entry window

更新至Updated 2026-07-25仅处于评估报道Evaluation reported only自研芯片Custom chip · 未立项 / 未公布not announced

老板先看：五条结论Executive brief: five conclusions

  1. 截至 2026-07-25，Anthropic 没有公布自研芯片、硅团队、架构、ASIC 合作方、节点或流片；Reuters 仅报道其在评估可能性。现在不能把它当作确定晶圆客户。As of 2026-07-25, Anthropic has disclosed no custom chip, silicon team, architecture, ASIC partner, node or tapeout. Reuters only reports an evaluation. It is not yet a committed wafer customer.
  2. 当前算力已经被长期伙伴覆盖：AWS 最多 5GW 且使用超过 100 万颗 Trainium2；Google/Broadcom 为 multiple GW；另有 NVIDIA/Azure 最多 1GW、AMD 最多 2GW。各协议时间、选项和用途不同，不能直接相加。Long-term partners already cover current compute: up to 5GW with AWS and over one million Trainium2 chips, multiple GW with Google/Broadcom, up to 1GW with NVIDIA/Azure and up to 2GW with AMD. Different terms, options and uses mean these cannot be directly added.
  3. 直接价值不是立刻争取主计算 die，而是 2026–27 年参与架构、推理 TCO、内存/互连与 chiplet 分工；若这时才立项，首片产品更可能在 2028–29 年。The immediate value is not a main compute die, but 2026–27 architecture, inference-TCO, memory/interconnect and chiplet partitioning work. A program launched then would more plausibly reach first silicon in 2028–29.
  4. 最可信的第一颗芯片是推理、KV-cache/数据搬移或 I/O 卸载，而不是训练旗舰：范围较小、软件切换风险更低，也更容易与 AWS/Google/GPU 共存。A first chip is more plausibly inference, KV-cache/data movement or I/O offload than a flagship trainer: smaller scope, lower software-switching risk and easier coexistence with AWS, Google and GPUs.
  5. 结论：列为“能力塑形”，不是“立即产品”。只有出现专门硅团队、ASIC/EDA 合作、HBM/SerDes 招聘和架构冻结，评分才从 63 上调为 Pursue now。Verdict: capability shaping, not an immediate product. Upgrade the 63 score to Pursue now only after a dedicated silicon team, ASIC/EDA engagement, HBM/SerDes hiring and architecture freeze appear.

决策Decision

为什么做、为什么暂不做产品Why engage, why not yet pursue a product

做：架构塑形Engage: architecture shaping

规模与供应分散动机真实存在Scale and diversification motives are real

多云、多芯片的采购组合证明 Anthropic 愿意为成本、容量和议价权分散供应。模型负载可预测，也具备把 KV-cache、互连和推理算子固化的潜在收益。Its multi-cloud, multi-chip portfolio shows a willingness to diversify for cost, capacity and bargaining power. Predictable model workloads create potential gains from specializing KV cache, interconnect and inference operators.

暂不做：主计算 dieNo-go now: main compute die

没有立项，且现有替代方案很强No program, with strong substitutes

Trainium、TPU、NVIDIA 和 AMD 已覆盖训练与推理。没有团队、软件栈和架构冻结之前，节点讨论不会转化为产品收入。Trainium, TPU, NVIDIA and AMD already cover training and inference. Without a team, software stack and architecture freeze, node discussion will not convert into product revenue.

第一步First move

用 8–12 周完成一份公开负载假设下的推理 TCO / 内存带宽 / 互连瓶颈研究；目标是得到一个可验证的 chiplet 或推理 ASIC 规格，而不是先推节点。Run an 8–12 week inference-TCO, memory-bandwidth and interconnect bottleneck study under public workload assumptions. The output should be a testable chiplet or inference-ASIC specification—not a node pitch.

背供电与散热Backside power and thermals

PowerVia 本身不是“不适合 AI”的结论。风险来自 3D 堆叠后的纵向热路径、base die 功耗密度和封装冷却；18A-P 已公开热阻改善，但仍须用目标 die size、功率图和堆叠结构做 chip-package-system 热联合仿真。PowerVia does not make AI inherently unsuitable. Risk comes from vertical thermal paths after 3D stacking, base-die power density and package cooling. 18A-P reports better thermal resistance, but target die size, power maps and stack geometry still require chip-package-system co-simulation.

战略模型Strategy models

SWOT + Porter’s Five Forces

S · STRENGTH

模型—负载可联合优化Model–workload co-optimization

Claude 负载、长上下文和产品流量可形成稳定规格；多云经验降低单一软件栈依赖。Claude workloads, long context and product traffic can define stable requirements; multi-cloud experience reduces one-stack dependence.

W · WEAKNESS

没有公开硅组织No disclosed silicon organization

芯片、封装、板卡、编译器、集群与供应链都要从零建立或依赖伙伴。Silicon, package, boards, compiler, clusters and supply chain must be built or partner-supplied.

O · OPPORTUNITY

推理 TCO 与供应分散Inference TCO and diversification

高速增长和多 GW 需求足以摊薄 ASIC NRE；第一颗可从窄域 accelerator/chiplet 切入。Fast growth and multi-GW demand can amortize ASIC NRE; a narrow accelerator/chiplet offers a lower-risk entry.

T · THREAT

云厂 ASIC 迭代更快Cloud ASICs iterate faster

Trainium、TPU、NVIDIA、AMD 已形成产品、软件和容量闭环；自研可能在量产前落后一代。Trainium, TPU, NVIDIA and AMD already close the product-software-capacity loop; an internal chip can lag a generation before launch.

什么出现才升级优先级What must appear before upgrading priority

G1 · 商业立项 · 证据型Program · evidence-based必须先出现专门硅负责人、团队、预算与首代 workload KPI；30–50 人只作为建议的早期核心团队量级，非公开客户要求。A dedicated silicon lead, team, budget and first-generation workload KPI must appear first. A 30–50 person core team is only a proposed early scale, not a public customer requirement.
G2 · 架构与时间 · 分析Architecture & timing · analysis若目标是 2029 产品，建议 2027 H1 前完成 build/buy、2027 H2 前冻结主架构；该时间为反推，不是 Anthropic 公布计划。For a 2029 target, proposed timing is build/buy by 1H27 and architecture freeze by 2H27. This is backward planning, not an Anthropic-announced plan.

G4 · 封装与热Package & thermal目标 HBM 数量、<5µm HBI（若使用）、KGD、复合良率与冷却方案通过联合验证。Target HBM count, <5µm HBI if used, KGD, compound yield and cooling validated together.
G5 · TCO · 建议内部标准proposed internal建议在 Claude 实际模型上达到 ≥25% tokens/$ 或 tokens/J 优势；该数值需与真实 workload 共同确认，否则继续使用多供应商现货平台。Proposed exit criterion: ≥25% tokens/$ or tokens/J gain on real Claude models, to be confirmed against the actual workload; otherwise retain merchant/cloud platforms.
90 天内应学什么What to learn in 90 days

① 自研是正式项目还是供应商议价工具；② 训练还是推理；③ 首代容量和部署地点；④ Broadcom/Marvell/云厂是否参与；⑤ Claude 的带宽、互连、缓存和软件瓶颈。任意前三项不清楚，不进入产品承诺。1) Real program or negotiating lever, 2) training or inference, 3) first volume and deployment, 4) ASIC/cloud partners, 5) Claude’s bandwidth, fabric, cache and software bottlenecks. Do not make a product commitment if any of the first three remain unclear.
