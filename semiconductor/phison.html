REPORT 01 · SSD CONTROLLER

Phison 群联电子：从 PCIe 3.0 到 PCIe 6.0 的控制器、节点与竞争地图Controllers, nodes and competition from PCIe 3.0 to PCIe 6.0

群联的优势并非只是一颗芯片，而是控制器、固件、NAND 验证、模组与客户交付的整套能力。当前旗舰 E28 与主流 E37T 已进入台积电 N6；下一代 X3 已公开性能与时间表，但制程节点尚未披露。Phison’s advantage is not a single chip but an integrated stack of controllers, firmware, NAND qualification, modules and customer delivery. Current E28 and E37T have moved to TSMC N6; next-generation X3 has disclosed performance and timing, but not its process node.

一句话结论Bottom line

N6 已成为群联 PCIe 5.0 高端与主流产品的共同平台；PCIe 6.0 X3 的制造节点仍是公开资料中的空白。N6 is now the common platform for Phison’s high-end and mainstream PCIe 5.0 products; the manufacturing node for PCIe 6.0 X3 remains undisclosed.

E28/E37T 对 E26/E31T 的核心跃迁，是从 12/7nm 收敛到 6nm，并通过更快 NAND PHY 与更高并行度逼近 PCIe 5.0 上限。下一代 X3 的竞争不只看 28GB/s，还看 64GT/s PHY、16 通道、功耗、企业固件、OCP 兼容与量产时间。The E28/E37T step from E26/E31T is a convergence from 12/7nm to 6nm, paired with faster NAND PHY and greater parallelism to approach PCIe 5.0 limits. X3 competition is not just 28GB/s: 64GT/s PHY, 16 channels, power, enterprise firmware, OCP compliance and volume timing all matter.

Yet its 2025 annual report also says clear market statistics are unavailable and provides no auditable segment share.

制造与制程Manufacturing & process

先进 SSD 控制器以台积电为主，但群联供应链并非“全部台积电”Advanced SSD controllers lean on TSMC, but Phison’s supply chain is not “all TSMC”

群联 2025 年报将 UMC 集团与台积电列为主要控制器供应商，并把 UMC 与 GlobalFoundries 列入 PMIC/高速 IC 供应链。公开产品资料确认 E12/E16 为 28nm、E18/E26/E27T/U21 为 12nm、E31T 为 7nm、E28/E37T 为 N6。年报同时提出新增供应商以降低晶圆制造集中风险。Phison’s 2025 annual report lists the UMC group and TSMC as primary controller suppliers, and UMC plus GlobalFoundries for PMIC/high-speed ICs. Public product materials confirm E12/E16 at 28nm, E18/E26/E27T/U21 at 12nm, E31T at 7nm, and E28/E37T at N6. The annual report also calls for new suppliers to reduce wafer-manufacturing concentration risk.

2018–19

28nm · E12 / E16

成熟节点配合早期 PCIe 3/4；数字逻辑、SRAM、PCIe 与 NAND PHY 在成本和风险间平衡。A mature-node balance of digital logic, SRAM, PCIe and NAND PHY for early PCIe 3/4.

2020–23

12nm · E18 / E26 / E27T

频率与功耗改善，但 E26 的 PCIe 5.0 首发仍需要较强散热。Frequency and power improved, though first-wave E26 PCIe 5.0 drives still demanded substantial cooling.

2024–25

7nm → N6 · E31T / E28

E31T 先在 7nm 建立低功耗 Gen5 DRAMless；E28 以 N6 冲击 Gen5 上限。E31T first established low-power Gen5 DRAMless on 7nm; E28 then used N6 to approach the Gen5 ceiling.

2026

N6 · E37T

旗舰与主流平台统一到 N6；竞争对手 SM2508/SM2524XT 同样选择 N6。Flagship and mainstream converge on N6, also selected by peer SM2508/SM2524XT.

2027+

X3 · 节点未披露X3 · node undisclosed

从 2026 年 12 月样片和 2027 年中量产目标判断，主芯片设计与制造平台在 2026 年中应已基本确定；公开资料不足以判断是 N6、N5/N4 或其他节点。Given December 2026 samples and a mid-2027 volume target, the main-die design and manufacturing platform should already be substantially fixed by mid-2026. 为什么 SSD 控制器不必盲目追逐 3nm / 2nmWhy SSD controllers need not chase 3nm / 2nm blindly

可缩放部分What scales

CPU 核、纠错编码、FTL 加速器和部分 SRAM 控制逻辑会从更高密度与更低电压获益。CPU cores, ECC, FTL accelerators and some SRAM-control logic benefit from density and lower voltage.

缩放受限部分What scales poorly

PCIe/NAND PHY、模拟电路、ESD、I/O 和大 SRAM 阵列不会按节点名称同比缩小；先进节点的掩模、IP 与验证成本也更高。PCIe/NAND PHYs, analog, ESD, I/O and large SRAM arrays do not shrink in lockstep with node labels; masks, IP and validation also cost more.

供给与现金Supply and cash

NAND/DRAM 涨价和预付款要求增加营运资金压力；库存不足又限制出货。供应紧张既提高名义收入，也提高执行风险。Rising NAND/DRAM prices and prepayments increase working-capital needs while insufficient inventory caps shipments. Tight supply lifts nominal revenue and execution risk simultaneously.

从零售转向 AI/CSPRetail to AI/CSP

2026 年 6 月官方称零售占比已降至 5% 以下，部分 AI/CSP 项目能见度到 2027 上半年。产品组合价值上升，但验证和服务复杂度也上升。In June 2026 Phison said retail had fallen below 5% while some AI/CSP visibility extended into 1H 2027. Mix value rises, as do qualification and service complexity.

竞品两面夹击Two-sided competition

Silicon Motion 在相近节点和规格正面对位；Maxio/InnoGrit 压低客户端价格；NAND 原厂则用垂直整合把控制器利润留在内部。Silicon Motion matches nodes and specs directly; Maxio/InnoGrit pressure client pricing; NAND vendors retain controller economics through vertical integration.

PCIe 6.0 系统难度PCIe 6.0 system difficulty

PAM4 信号、16 通道 NAND、散热、OCP 固件与超大容量同时提高验证难度。首发性能不等于稳定量产。PAM4 signaling, 16 NAND channels, thermals, OCP firmware and extreme capacities all raise qualification complexity. A launch demo is not the same as stable volume.
