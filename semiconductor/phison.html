REPORT 01 · SSD CONTROLLER

Phison / 群联 PCIe 5/6 控制器、竞争节点与 post-X3 窗口PCIe 5/6 controllers, competitor nodes and the post-X3 window

更新至Updated 2026-07-25E28/E37T · TSMC N6X3 · 节点未披露node undisclosed

老板先看：五条结论Executive brief: five conclusions

  1. 当前主力高端 E28 与主流无 DRAM E37T 均为台积电 N6。E28 于 2025 年 5 月进入量产；E37T 已发布并展示，但截至本报告日没有找到官方量产公告。Current high-end E28 and mainstream DRAMless E37T both use TSMC N6. E28 entered volume production in May 2025; E37T has launched and been demonstrated, but no official volume-production announcement was found by this date.
  2. E37T 的核心规格是 14.9/14.5GB/s、3M/3M IOPS、4 通道、4.8GT/s NAND；Phison 标示负载功耗 <2.1W，但公开资料未定义完整测量边界。它说明客户端产品已由“最先进节点”转向成熟 N6 的成本/功耗优化。E37T’s key specifications are 14.9/14.5GB/s, 3M/3M IOPS, four channels and 4.8GT/s NAND. Phison states <2.1W under load, but public material does not define the full measurement boundary. It shows client products optimizing cost/power on mature N6 rather than chasing the newest node.
  3. 企业 PCIe 6.0 已出现时间差：Samsung PM1763 于 2026-07-08 宣布量产，使用 4nm 控制器，28.4/21.9GB/s；Phison X3 仅为预览，媒体报道 2026 年底样片、2027 年中量产。Enterprise PCIe 6.0 now has a timing gap: Samsung announced PM1763 volume production on 2026-07-08 with a 4nm controller and 28.4/21.9GB/s, while Phison X3 is a preview, reportedly sampling end-2026 and ramping mid-2027.
  4. 当前 E28/E37T 和大概率已锁定的 X3 不适合先进节点迁移；最合理窗口是 post-X3/PCIe 7 企业产品（约 2028+）或成熟节点第二来源。首要门槛是 PCIe/NAND PHY、低 Vmin SRAM、固件验证和 TCO，不是散热。Current E28/E37T and the likely locked X3 are poor advanced-node migration targets. The credible window is a post-X3/PCIe 7 enterprise product around 2028+, or a mature-node second source. PCIe/NAND PHY, low-Vmin SRAM, firmware validation and TCO—not thermals—are the primary gates.
  5. 结论：当前代主 die 为 No-Go；用 6–8 周先做 N6/N4 对 18A-P 的 die+mask+IP+封装总成本筛选。建议内部退出标准是性能/W 提升 ≥20% 且控制器总成本成立；该阈值不是 Phison 公开要求，需后续确认。Verdict: current-generation main die is No-Go. First run a 6–8 week N6/N4-versus-18A-P total-cost screen covering die, masks, IP and package. A proposed internal exit criterion is ≥20% performance/W gain with viable controller cost; this is not a public Phison requirement and must be confirmed.

做：post-X3 / 第二来源Engage: post-X3 / second source

多代工经验与企业 SSD 上行Multi-foundry experience and enterprise upside

年报确认控制器使用 UMC 与 TSMC，并持续寻找新供应商。AI/CSP 存储增长、PCIe 7 和计算存储可提高节点价值，使企业产品比低价客户端更可能承担先进节点 NRE。Its annual report confirms UMC and TSMC controller sourcing and continued supplier expansion. AI/CSP storage growth, PCIe 7 and computational storage raise node value, making enterprise products more able to absorb advanced-node NRE than low-cost client parts.

不做：E28 / E37T / X3No-go: E28 / E37T / X3

时间和经济性都不支持迁移Timing and economics reject migration

E28 已量产，E37T 已发布，X3 已预览。迁移会重做高速 PHY、SRAM、模拟、固件与 NAND 兼容矩阵；先进晶圆、mask 与 IP 成本难由客户端 ASP 回收。E28 is in volume, E37T launched and X3 previewed. Migration would redo high-speed PHYs, SRAM, analog, firmware and NAND qualification, while advanced-wafer, mask and IP cost is hard to recover in client ASP.

一句话汇报One-line executive call

不要把 18A 当作 N6 的直接替代；先证明 post-X3 企业控制器能用更高性能/W、美国供应或第二来源价值覆盖高速 IP 和 NRE。Do not treat 18A as a direct N6 replacement. First prove that a post-X3 enterprise controller can cover high-speed IP and NRE through better performance/W, U.S. supply or second-source value.

竞争结论Competitive takeaway

客户端竞争的轴是 NAND 兼容、固件、idle/active 功耗、reference drive 成本和量产节奏。N6 已能打满 PCIe 5；换到 18A/14A 不会自动产生客户可支付的价值。Client competition turns on NAND compatibility, firmware, idle/active power, reference-drive cost and ramp timing. N6 already saturates PCIe 5; moving to 18A/14A does not automatically create payable customer value.

散热判断Thermal judgment

Phison 称 E37T under load <2.1W（测量边界未公开），X3 约 7W 是媒体按 28GB/s÷4GB/s/W 推算；因此背供电热问题不是首要 no-go。Intel 称 18A-P 热阻改善 20–40%，但这是 vendor-stated 工艺数据，最终仍需在 SSD 封装、板级风道和 NAND 温度约束下验证。Phison states E37T is <2.1W under load without a disclosed boundary; X3’s ~7W is media-derived from 28GB/s÷4GB/s/W. Backside-power thermal risk is therefore not the first no-go. Intel states 20–40% better thermal resistance for 18A-P, but that vendor-stated process data still requires SSD-package, board-airflow and NAND-temperature validation.
