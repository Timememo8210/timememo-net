01

这是什么比赛？Understand the competition

CyberPatriot 是美国 Air & Space Forces Association 主办的全国青少年网络防御竞赛。队员在模拟环境中修复系统安全问题，并完成 Cisco 网络知识测验与实操。CyberPatriot is the National Youth Cyber Defense Competition run by the Air & Space Forces Association. Teams secure simulated computer systems and complete Cisco networking quizzes and practical exercises.

项目Item说明What it means
团队Team每队2–6人，同时最多5人参赛，每轮可替补一次。2–6 registered students; up to five compete at once, with one substitution per round.
组别Divisions初中组 Middle School；普通高中 Open；JROTC等组织高中组 All Service。Middle School; Open for high-school teams; All Service for eligible youth service organizations.
比赛形式Format全国决赛前均为线上比赛，可以在学校、俱乐部或其他合适地点集中参赛。Online before the National Finals; teams can meet at a school, club or other suitable venue.
一队 ≠ 高中组First team ≠ high-school division“一队”是内部编组信息，不能据此推断登记组别。本页按初中组规划，须与教练确认。An internal first-team selection does not establish the registered division. This guide assumes Middle School; confirm with the coach.

初中组不分白金、黄金、白银档；入门轮与州赛累计成绩前60%晋级半决赛。Middle School teams have no Platinum, Gold or Silver tiers. The top 60% by combined Introductory and State Round scores advance to the Semifinals.

从现在到1月的训练路线A training route through January

以下是家庭训练建议，不是官方作业量。先把俱乐部课程与作业计入总量，试行每周4–5小时Cisco专项学习，两周后根据负担与进步调整。This is a suggested family routine, not an official workload. Count club lessons and homework toward an initial total of 4–5 hours of Cisco study per week, then adjust after two weeks.

AI与比赛资料：使用边界AI and competition materials

依据CP19规则书第3010节（印刷页32–33）。赛外学习与正式比赛的边界要分清；本页是家长资源导航，不是比赛中使用的AI工具。Based on CP19 §3010 (printed pages 32–33). Distinguish preparation from scored competition. This is a parent resource guide, not an in-round AI tool.

情境Situation规则摘要Rule summary
赛外免费AIUnpaid AI outside rounds可用于学习与非比赛材料练习。Allowed for learning with non-competition materials.
付费AIPaid AI不得用于与比赛相关的活动；条文不限于赛中。Prohibited in connection with the competition, not just during rounds.
正式比赛材料Competition material任何时候均不得提交给AI。Never submit it to AI.
赛中AI协助In-round AI help不得生成命令、答案或脚本，也不得连接比赛系统。No generated commands, answers, scripts or AI connections to competition systems.
AI生成程序AI-created programs即使提前制作，全部或部分由AI生成的程序也禁止赛中使用。Programs created wholly or partly by AI are barred in rounds, even if prepared earlier.
分享与学校规定Sharing and local rules比赛相关AI材料不得向队外分享；学校更严格规定优先。Do not share competition-related AI materials outside the team. Stricter school rules apply.

偶然显示的搜索AI摘要不因此违规。正式比赛时，家长与教练不能提供解题帮助。边界问题请由教练向主办方确认。Incidental search summaries are not themselves a violation. Parents and coaches cannot provide solving help during rounds. Ask the coach to clarify edge cases with the organizer.

开赛前，准备到位Before the round

  • 确认Middle School登记，而非仅凭“一队”称呼推断。Confirm the registered Middle School division; do not infer it from “first team”.
  • 拿到本届Cisco Content Course；分别核对学习与比赛登录方式。Access this season’s Content Course and verify separate training/competition credentials.
  • 使用本届要求的Packet Tracer 9.0.0，先完成官方练习轮兼容性测试。Use the specified Packet Tracer 9.0.0 and test compatibility during practice.
  • 与教练确认本地比赛4小时时段，以及10月21日队员名单截止事项。Confirm the local four-hour session and the October 21 roster deadline with the coach.
  • 不提前打开正式比赛镜像；打开首个镜像可能启动计时。Do not open a scored competition image early; opening the first image can start the clock.
  • 准备稳定网络、电源，以及清晰的团队分工。Prepare reliable internet, power and a clear division of team responsibilities.
