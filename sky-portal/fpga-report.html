不管是 CPU、GPU 还是 FPGA，所有数字芯片的最底层都是同一样东西：晶体管组成的逻辑门。 Whether CPU, GPU, or FPGA, every digital chip is built from the same foundation: logic gates made of transistors.

A modern chip has billions to hundreds of billions of gates. The difference lies in: how these gates are wired together.

最关键的区别：CPU/GPU 是"软件定义功能"，FPGA/ASIC 是"硬件即功能"。

CPU 运行程序时，是一个固定的电路在一步步读取并执行你的指令。无论你写什么程序，底层的电路都是一样的——区别在于指令不同。

FPGA 没有"指令"的概念。你做的不是写程序，而是设计一个电路。配置完成后，FPGA 内部的电路就变成了你设计的那个样子——数据直接流过这个电路，中间没有"取指→解码→执行"的过程。这就是为什么 FPGA 能接近 ASIC 的速度：它本质上就是一个定制电路，只不过这个电路可以擦掉重画。 The key distinction: CPU/GPU are "software-defined," while FPGA/ASIC are "hardware IS the function."

When a CPU runs a program, a fixed circuit reads and executes your instructions step by step. No matter what program you write, the underlying circuit is the same — only the instructions differ.

FPGA has no concept of "instructions." You don't write a program — you design a circuit. Once configured, the FPGA's internal wiring becomes the circuit you designed — data flows directly through it with no fetch-decode-execute overhead. That's why FPGAs approach ASIC speed: it IS a custom circuit, except this circuit can be erased and redrawn.

一句话总结：FPGA 是一块可以擦掉重画的定制电路板。它比 CPU 快（因为没有取指解码的开销），比 ASIC 灵活（因为可以重新配置），比 GPU 更精确（因为没有调度抖动）。代价是：面积比等效 ASIC 大 10-40 倍，主频低 3-10 倍，单价贵 100 倍。但对于量小、要求灵活、需要确定性延迟的场景——尤其是军事和航天——这些代价完全值得。 One-line summary: FPGA is a custom circuit board that can be erased and redrawn. Faster than CPU (no fetch-decode overhead), more flexible than ASIC (reconfigurable), more precise than GPU (no scheduling jitter). The price: 10-40x more area, 3-10x lower clock speed, 100x higher unit cost than equivalent ASIC. But for low-volume, flexible, deterministic-latency scenarios — especially military and aerospace — these trade-offs are absolutely worth it.

💡 什么是 FPGA — 全面技术概览What is FPGA — Comprehensive Technology Overview

FPGA（Field-Programmable Gate Array，现场可编程门阵列）是一种半导体芯片，出厂时内部逻辑功能是"空白"的，由用户在"现场"（field）通过加载配置文件来定义芯片要执行的逻辑。这与传统ASIC（出厂即固化功能，不可更改）形成鲜明对比。 FPGA (Field-Programmable Gate Array) is a semiconductor chip that ships with "blank" logic — the user defines the chip's function "in the field" by loading a configuration bitstream. This contrasts with ASICs, where functionality is permanently fixed at fabrication.

SRAM编程的详细过程（主流技术）SRAM Programming in Detail (Mainstream)

以AMD/Xilinx为例，SRAM-based FPGA的编程/配置过程如下： Using AMD/Xilinx as an example, SRAM-based FPGA configuration works as follows:

  1. 设计阶段：工程师用HDL（Verilog/VHDL）写逻辑 → 综合工具将其转化为LUT映射和布线方案 → 生成比特流文件（bitstream），通常几MB到几十MB Design phase: Engineer writes logic in HDL (Verilog/VHDL) → synthesis tool maps it to LUTs and routing → generates a bitstream file, typically several MB to tens of MB
  2. 存储：bitstream存放在板上的外部SPI Flash中 Storage: Bitstream is stored in an external SPI flash chip on the board
  3. 上电加载：FPGA上电后，自动从Flash读取bitstream，将配置数据写入片上数百万个SRAM单元（几十毫秒到几百毫秒） Power-up load: On power-up, FPGA automatically reads bitstream from flash and writes configuration data into millions of on-chip SRAM cells (tens to hundreds of ms)
  4. 运行：每个SRAM单元的0/1值决定了：① 每个LUT存储的真值表内容 ② 互连矩阵中每个开关的通/断状态 ③ I/O引脚的电压标准和方向 Operation: Each SRAM cell's 0/1 value determines: ① Truth table content of each LUT ② On/off state of each routing switch ③ I/O pin voltage standard and direction
  5. 重编程：要改功能？更新Flash中的bitstream文件，重新上电或触发重配置即可。甚至支持部分重配置（Partial Reconfiguration）——运行中只改变芯片的一部分逻辑 Reprogram: Need a different function? Update the bitstream in flash, power-cycle or trigger reconfiguration. Even supports Partial Reconfiguration — changing only part of the chip while the rest keeps running
回答核心问题：不是"烧"的，不是一次性的。 主流FPGA（AMD/Xilinx、Intel/Altera）使用SRAM技术，可以无限次重新编程。每次掉电后需要重新加载配置，但这也意味着你可以随时更改芯片功能。只有老式的反熔丝FPGA（Actel）才是一次性的——高压击穿金属间绝缘层形成永久连接，不可逆。Flash-based FPGA（Microchip PolarFire）则介于两者之间：可重编程约1000次，且掉电不丢失。 The key answer: It's NOT "burned," and it's NOT one-time. Mainstream FPGAs (AMD/Xilinx, Intel/Altera) use SRAM technology — infinitely reprogrammable. Configuration is lost at power-off but can be changed anytime. Only legacy antifuse FPGAs (Actel) are one-time — high voltage permanently breaks through insulation between metal layers, irreversible. Flash-based FPGAs (Microchip PolarFire) sit in between: reprogrammable ~1000 times and non-volatile.

抗辐射深度解析 ①：总剂量 TID — 慢性"中毒"Radiation Deep Dive ①: Total Ionizing Dose (TID) — Chronic "Poisoning"

TID 是什么？想象你在太阳底下暴晒——不是被一颗子弹打中，而是日积月累的紫外线损伤。TID 就是芯片在辐射环境中累积吸收的总电离辐射剂量，单位是 rad(Si) 或 krad(Si)。 What is TID? Think of sun exposure — not a single bullet hit, but cumulative UV damage over years. TID is the total ionizing radiation dose accumulated by a chip in a radiation environment, measured in rad(Si) or krad(Si).

TID 对芯片做了什么？电离辐射在晶体管的栅极氧化层中积累正电荷陷阱。随着剂量增加： What does TID do to chips? Ionizing radiation accumulates positive charge traps in transistor gate oxide. As dose increases:

  • 晶体管的阈值电压漂移 → 逻辑翻转变慢甚至失效Transistor threshold voltage shifts → logic switching slows or fails
  • 漏电流增大 → 功耗飙升、信号干扰Leakage current increases → power surges, signal interference
  • 最终导致芯片参数超标直至完全失效Eventually chip parameters exceed limits until total failure

制程瓶颈 #1：为什么 TID 和制程节点密切相关？

更先进的制程（7nm, 5nm）栅极氧化层更薄，电荷陷阱密度反而降低了——这一点有利于 TID 耐受。但同时，更小的晶体管工作电压更低（0.7V vs 老工艺的 1.2V），热载流子效应和界面态更复杂，需要专门优化。

目前航天级 FPGA 的制程瓶颈在 20nm（AMD XQRKU060 是 20nm，已是最先进的航天 FPGA）。商用 FPGA 已到 7nm/6nm，但航天认证落后 2-3 代，因为每个制程节点需要 3-5 年的辐射测试和认证周期。 Process Bottleneck #1: Why TID is closely tied to process node

Advanced nodes (7nm, 5nm) have thinner gate oxides with lower charge trap density — this actually helps TID tolerance. But smaller transistors run at lower voltages (0.7V vs 1.2V in older nodes), with more complex hot carrier and interface state effects requiring specialized optimization.

Current space-grade FPGA process bottleneck is 20nm (AMD XQRKU060 at 20nm is the most advanced space FPGA). Commercial FPGAs are at 7nm/6nm, but space qualification lags by 2-3 generations because each node needs 3-5 years of radiation testing and qualification.

抗辐射深度解析 ②：单粒子效应 SEE — 瞬间"狙击"Radiation Deep Dive ②: Single Event Effects (SEE) — Instant "Sniper Hit"

如果 TID 是慢性中毒，SEE 就是被狙击手打了一枪——一颗高能粒子（宇宙射线中的重离子、质子、中子）穿过芯片，沿途产生大量电荷，瞬间改变电路状态。 If TID is chronic poisoning, SEE is a sniper shot — a single high-energy particle (heavy ion, proton, or neutron from cosmic rays) penetrates the chip, generating a trail of charge that instantly alters circuit state.

制程瓶颈 #2：为什么先进制程让 SEU 更难对付？

制程越先进，晶体管越小，每个存储节点上的电荷量（临界电荷 Qcrit）越少。一颗宇宙射线粒子沉积的电荷更容易超过 Qcrit，翻转 bit 的概率更高。

具体数据：28nm FDS-OI 工艺（Lattice Nexus）的 FIT 率约 1（每十亿小时 1 次故障）。普通 28nm SRAM FPGA 的 FIT 率约 100。先进到 7nm/5nm 后，如果不做特殊设计，SEU 率会更高。

这就是为什么航天 FPGA 制程落后商用 2-3 代。不是做不出来，而是要确保每个新制程在辐射环境下的 SEU 率可控，需要大量测试（用粒子加速器模拟太空辐射）和专门的抗辐射电路设计。 Process Bottleneck #2: Why advanced nodes make SEU harder to handle

Smaller transistors mean less charge per storage node (critical charge Qcrit). A single cosmic ray particle deposits enough charge to exceed Qcrit more easily, increasing bit-flip probability.

Data point: 28nm FDS-OI (Lattice Nexus) achieves FIT rate of ~1 (1 failure per billion hours). Standard 28nm SRAM FPGA has FIT rate ~100. At 7nm/5nm without special design, SEU rates would be even higher.

This is why space FPGAs lag commercial by 2-3 generations. Not because they can't be built, but because every new node's SEU rate must be proven controllable through extensive testing (particle accelerators simulating space radiation) and specialized rad-hard circuit design.

总结：FPGA 抗辐射的核心矛盾

FPGA 的灵魂是"可编程"——但可编程的核心机制（SRAM 配置存储）恰恰是辐射最容易攻击的目标。这就像造了一扇万能钥匙能开的门，然后发现宇宙射线就是一把万能钥匙。

解决方案只有三条路：
1. 冗余弥补（TMR + EDAC + Scrubbing）— 接受 SRAM 会被翻转，但靠冗余纠错。代价：面积 ×3，功耗 ×3
2. 换存储介质（Flash / 反熔丝）— 从根源消除问题。代价：不可或有限重编程
3. 两者结合 — 现代航天 FPGA 的主流做法 Summary: The core paradox of FPGA radiation hardening

FPGA's soul is "programmability" — but the core mechanism (SRAM config storage) is exactly what radiation attacks most easily. It's like building a door that any master key can open, then discovering cosmic rays ARE master keys.

Only three paths:
1. Redundancy compensation (TMR + EDAC + Scrubbing) — accept SRAM will flip, correct with redundancy. Cost: 3x area, 3x power
2. Change storage medium (Flash / Antifuse) — eliminate the problem at source. Cost: non/limited reprogrammability
3. Combine both — the mainstream approach for modern space FPGAs
