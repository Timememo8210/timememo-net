2. 中国央行开展5000亿元MLF操作，保持流动性充裕2. PBOC Conducts 500B MLF Operation to Maintain Liquidity

中国人民银行公告，为保持银行体系流动性充裕，于6月25日开展了5000亿元中期借贷便利（MLF）操作，期限为1年期。此次操作采用固定数量、利率招标、多重价位中标方式。市场分析认为，央行此举旨在对冲季末因素影响，稳定市场预期，支持实体经济融资需求。The People's Bank of China announced a 500 billion yuan MLF operation on June 25 to maintain banking system liquidity, with a 1-year term. The operation used fixed quantity, interest rate bidding, and multiple price determination. Market analysts believe this move aims to offset quarter-end effects and support real economy financing.
