🏛️ 新时代开启 — Warsh在白宫宣誓成为第17任美联储主席🏛️ A New Era — Warsh Sworn in as 17th Fed Chair at White House

5月22日，Kevin Warsh在白宫由总统Trump主持宣誓仪式，正式成为美联储第17任主席，接替 Jerome Powell。这是历史上首次在白宫举行的美联储主席宣誓仪式。Warsh此前以54-45票的党派线投票获得参议院确认。他在宣誓后发表简短声明，强调美联储的独立性，并表示"不会被政治压力左右"。On May 22, Kevin Warsh was sworn in as the 17th Chair of the Federal Reserve in a White House ceremony hosted by President Trump, succeeding Jerome Powell. This is the first time a Fed chair has been sworn in at the White House. Warsh was confirmed 54-45 along party lines. In his statement, he emphasized Fed independence and said he "won't be swayed by political pressure."
