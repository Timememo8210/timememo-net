Paper #1 ⚛️ 量子计算

硅基移动自旋量子比特的双量子比特逻辑与量子隐形传态Two-qubit logic and teleportation with mobile spin qubits in silicon

Nature · 2026
Y. Matsumoto, M. De Smet, L. Tryputen, S. L. de Snoo, S. V. Amitonov, A. Sammak, M. Rimbach-Russ, G. Scappucci & L. M. K. Vandersypen
量子计算架构的可扩展性和计算能力在很大程度上取决于高保真度的量子操作以及量子比特之间灵活的连接方式。本研究在硅基半导体器件中实现了一种"移动量子比特"架构——通过电势阱将电子自旋量子比特在器件上传输，并让两个移动中的量子比特相互靠近以执行量子门操作。研究团队发现，两个量子比特之间的相互作用强度可以通过改变它们的空间间距精确调控。当两个自旋各向中心移动120纳米（总位移240纳米）时，研究者在硅基平台上实现了约99%的双量子比特门保真度。更令人振奋的是，团队还成功演示了两个相距320纳米的量子比特之间的条件后选量子态隐形传态，平均门保真度达到87%。这证明了移动自旋量子比特在非局域量子信息处理方面的巨大潜力，预示着移动量子比特操作将成为未来大规模半导体量子处理器的基本特性。 The scalability and power of quantum computing architectures depend critically on high-fidelity operations and flexible qubit connectivity. This study demonstrates a "mobile qubit" architecture in silicon semiconductor devices, where electron spin qubits are shuttled through the device via potential wells and brought together to perform quantum gate operations. The team found that the interaction strength between two qubits is highly tunable via their spatial separation. When shuttling two spins towards the center by 120 nm each (total displacement 240 nm), they achieved an average two-qubit gate fidelity of about 99%. Furthermore, they implemented conditional post-selected quantum state teleportation between qubits separated by 320 nm with an average gate fidelity of 87%, showcasing the potential of mobile spin qubits for non-local quantum information processing. Mobile qubit operations are expected to become a universal feature of future large-scale semiconductor quantum processors.

🔬 核心发现Key Findings

  • 首次在硅基移动量子比特上实现约99%保真度的双量子比特逻辑门操作First demonstration of ~99% fidelity two-qubit logic gate operations on mobile silicon spin qubits
  • 成功演示了320纳米距离上的量子态隐形传态，保真度达87%Successful quantum state teleportation over 320 nm separation with 87% fidelity
  • 量子比特间相互作用强度可通过空间间距精确调控，提供了灵活的连接方案Qubit interaction strength is precisely tunable via spatial separation, providing flexible connectivity
💡 为什么重要：💡 Why it matters: 这项突破为构建大规模可扩展的半导体量子处理器开辟了新路径。移动量子比特架构允许动态重构量子比特阵列、在同一硬件上实现不同的量子纠错码，并通过专用功能区域优化资源利用，是通向实用量子计算的关键一步。 This breakthrough opens a new path toward building large-scale scalable semiconductor quantum processors. The mobile qubit architecture enables dynamic reconfiguration of qubit arrays, implementation of different quantum error correction codes on the same hardware, and resource optimization through dedicated functional zones — a crucial step toward practical quantum computing.

Paper #2 🦠 合成生物学

基于CRISPR–Cas12a2的RNA触发细胞选择性杀伤系统RNA-triggered cell killing with CRISPR–Cas12a2

Nature · 2026
Paul Scholz, Jared Thompson, Kadin T. Crosby, Torsten Fauth, Nathan M. Krah, Grant Schlauderaff, Robin Back, Zachary A. Berkheimer, Alivia Jolley, Dirk Sombroek, Rebekka Medert, Christian Zurek, Oleg Dmytrenko, Emily Wilson, Friso T. Schut, Jared Rutter, Xiaoyang Zhang, Michael Krohn, Ryan N. Jackson, Chase L. Beisel & Yang Liu
基于基因或转录特征选择性消灭目标细胞是基础研究、医学、生物技术和农业领域的重要需求。虽然CRISPR核酸酶已被用于细菌中的RNA引导反向选择，但其在真核细胞中的应用一直受到很大限制。本研究发现，新发现的V型CRISPR核酸酶Cas12a2具有RNA触发的DNA shredding（DNA广泛切割）能力，能够实现对酵母和人类细胞的可编程、序列特异性消灭。当Cas12a2被目标转录本激活后，会在细胞内引发大规模的双链DNA断裂，导致细胞死亡。该系统可被广泛的靶标转录本激活，且未观察到脱靶激活。利用这一方法，研究团队成功选择性地消灭了携带人乳头瘤病毒（HPV）的细胞、基因编辑失败的细胞，以及携带常见KRAS致癌点突变的细胞。这些发现极大拓展了CRISPR工具箱，使基于转录谱的真核细胞选择性消除成为可能。 Selectively eradicating target cells based on their genetic or transcriptional identity is crucial for basic research, medicine, biotechnology, and agriculture. While CRISPR nucleases have been used for RNA-guided counterselection in bacteria, their application in eukaryotes has been much more restrictive. This study shows that Cas12a2, a recently discovered type V CRISPR nuclease, exhibits RNA-triggered DNA shredding capability, enabling programmable, sequence-specific elimination of yeast and human cells. When activated by a target transcript, Cas12a2 elicits rampant double-stranded DNA breaks, leading to cell death. The system can be activated by a wide range of target transcripts with no observed off-target activation. The team selectively eliminated cells harboring human papillomavirus (HPV), cells that failed gene editing, and cells encoding a prevalent oncogenic KRAS point mutation. These findings greatly expand the CRISPR toolbox for selective eukaryotic cell elimination based on transcriptional profiles.

🔬 核心发现Key Findings

  • Cas12a2可被目标RNA激活，引发广泛的DNA切割（DNA shredding），导致真核细胞死亡Cas12a2 can be activated by target RNA to trigger widespread DNA shredding, causing eukaryotic cell death
  • 成功选择性消灭HPV感染细胞、基因编辑失败细胞和KRAS突变癌细胞，无脱靶效应Successfully eliminated HPV-infected cells, failed gene-edited cells, and KRAS-mutant cancer cells with no off-target effects
  • 系统可被广泛类型的靶标转录本激活，具有高度的灵活性和可编程性The system can be activated by a wide range of target transcripts, offering high flexibility and programmability
💡 为什么重要：💡 Why it matters: 这项研究将CRISPR的应用从基因编辑扩展到"基于转录特征的细胞选择性消灭"，为癌症治疗（精准清除突变细胞）、抗病毒治疗和基因治疗质量控制提供了全新工具。Cas12a2系统可以被看作一个"RNA感知的细胞自杀开关"，具有革命性的生物医学应用前景。 This research extends CRISPR applications from gene editing to "transcription-profile-based selective cell elimination," providing a powerful new tool for cancer therapy, antiviral treatment, and gene therapy quality control. The Cas12a2 system can be viewed as an "RNA-sensing cell suicide switch" with revolutionary biomedical potential.

Paper #3 🔬 材料科学

多层电容器在室温及更低温度下实现高效电卡制冷效应Electrocaloric effects across room temperature in multilayer capacitors

Nature · 2026
M. Guo, V. Farenkov, X. Chen, A. Mohanathan, A. Z. K. Goh, Y. Tang, J. Zhang, M. Vickers, S. M. Fairclough, C. Ducati, X. Moya, S. Hirose & N. D. Mathur
全球制冷需求持续增长，传统蒸气压缩制冷技术效率有限且使用有害制冷剂。电卡效应（Electrocaloric Effect）利用电场驱动铁电材料相变产生温度变化，是一种极具前景的固态制冷方案。然而此前的PbSc₀.₅Ta₀.₅O₃（PST）多层电容器仅在高于室温的居里温度以上工作，且需要耗能巨大的42天退火处理来获得高B位有序度和最大潜热。本研究通过将PST与PbMg₀.₅W₀.₅O₃（PMW）形成固溶体，巧妙地扩大了价态失配，在无需退火的情况下就保持了高B位有序度和潜热，同时通过破坏偶极有序将居里温度降低至230K。所制备的PST-PMW多层电容器在17.1 V μm⁻¹的电场下，于室温及更低温度范围展现出约3K的超临界电卡效应，且可承受超过一千万次循环而不发生击穿。在理想流体再生器中使用这些电容器并假设功回收，循环效率可达70-90%。这一成果意味着PST-PMW多层电容器已准备好取代现有PST电容器，推动电卡制冷技术走向实用化。 Global cooling demand continues to grow, while traditional vapor-compression refrigeration is limited in efficiency and uses harmful refrigerants. The electrocaloric effect — using electric fields to drive ferroelectric phase transitions for temperature changes — is a promising solid-state cooling approach. However, previous PbSc₀.₅Ta₀.₅O₃ (PST) multilayer capacitors only operated above the room-temperature Curie point and required an energetically expensive 42-day anneal for high B-site order and maximum latent heat. This study forms a solid solution of PST with PbMg₀.₅W₀.₅O₃ (PMW), exaggerating valence mismatch to maintain high B-site order and latent heat with no anneal, while disrupting dipolar order to reduce the Curie temperature to 230 K. The resulting PST-PMW multilayer capacitors show supercritical electrocaloric effects of ~3 K across and below room temperature under 17.1 V μm⁻¹ fields, withstanding over 10 million cycles without breakdown. Using these capacitors in an ideal fluid regenerator with work recovery yields cycle efficiencies of 70-90%. These findings establish PST-PMW multilayer capacitors as ready to replace existing PST devices for practical electrocaloric refrigeration.

🔬 核心发现Key Findings

  • 通过PST与PMW形成固溶体，在无需退火条件下保持高B位有序度，居里温度可降至230KPST-PMW solid solution maintains high B-site order without annealing, reducing Curie temperature to 230 K
  • 在室温及更低温度范围实现约3K的电卡效应，可承受超过一千万次循环Achieves ~3 K electrocaloric effects across and below room temperature, withstanding over 10 million cycles
  • 理想条件下循环效率可达70-90%，远超传统制冷技术Ideal cycle efficiencies of 70-90%, far exceeding conventional refrigeration technology
💡 为什么重要：💡 Why it matters: 这项研究解决了电卡制冷技术长期面临的核心难题——工作温度范围和制备成本。PST-PMW材料无需耗能退火、工作温度覆盖室温区间、循环稳定性极高，为下一代绿色、高效固态制冷设备的商业化铺平了道路，有望显著降低全球制冷能耗。 This study solves core challenges in electrocaloric cooling — operating temperature range and fabrication cost. PST-PMW materials require no energy-intensive annealing, operate across room temperature, and show extreme cycling stability, paving the way for commercialization of next-generation green, efficient solid-state cooling devices that could significantly reduce global cooling energy consumption.
