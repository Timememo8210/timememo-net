Paper #1 🚀 天体物理与宇宙学

在十亿秒差距尺度上探测到各向异性宇宙结构Detection of anisotropic cosmic structures on a gigaparsec scale

Nature · 2026
Francesco Sylos Labini & Marco Galoppo
宇宙学原理是标准宇宙学模型的基石，它假设宇宙在足够大的尺度上具有统计上的均匀性和各向同性。星系红移巡天绘制出宇宙网状结构，为检验这一假设提供了关键的观测手段。在该框架下，超出结构形成的非线性范围后，不均匀和各向异性特征应当迅速衰减。然而，星系分布中复杂的大尺度结构和空洞网络，以及独立探针报告的潜在大尺度偏离，正在对这一观点提出越来越多的挑战。本研究开发了一种名为"成对距离角分布"（ADPD）的无参数统计方法，用于测量方向相关性。通过对星系分布数据的分析，研究者探测到超过3σ显著性的各向异性信号，这一信号超过了各向同性对照组和几何匹配的ΛCDM模拟星表中的预期值。这些结果提供了直接证据，表明方向相干性持续延伸到比标准模型预测更大的尺度，挑战了宇宙在大尺度上各向同性的假设，并呼吁基于方向统计重新评估宇宙模型。 The cosmological principle—the assumption that the Universe becomes statistically homogeneous and isotropic on sufficiently large scales—underpins the standard cosmological model. Galaxy redshift surveys map the cosmic web and provide a key observational test of this assumption. Here, the authors develop the Angular Distribution of Pairwise Distances (ADPD), a parameter-free statistic measuring directional correlations, and detect anisotropy signals exceeding isotropic controls and geometry-matched ΛCDM mock catalogues with significance greater than 3σ. These results provide direct evidence that directional coherence persists to gigaparsec scales—larger than predicted in the standard framework—challenging the assumption of large-scale isotropy and motivating new tests of cosmological models based on directional statistics.

🔬 核心发现Key Findings

  • 利用全新的"成对距离角分布"（ADPD）无参数统计方法，发现星系分布在约10亿秒差距（gigaparsec）尺度上仍保持方向相干性Using the novel ADPD parameter-free statistic, persistent directional coherence is found at gigaparsec scales in galaxy distributions
  • 各向异性信号超过3σ显著性，远超各向同性对照和ΛCDM标准宇宙学模型的模拟预期Anisotropy signals exceed 3σ significance, far beyond isotropic controls and ΛCDM mock catalogue expectations
  • 结果基于DESI星系巡天公开数据，直接挑战了宇宙在大尺度上各向同性的核心假设Results based on public DESI survey data directly challenge the core assumption of large-scale isotropy
💡 为什么重要：💡 Why it matters: 宇宙学原理是现代宇宙学的一切基础——从暗能量测量到宇宙年龄估算都依赖这一假设。如果宇宙在大尺度上确实不是各向同性的，标准宇宙学模型可能需要根本性修正。 The cosmological principle is foundational to all of modern cosmology—from dark energy measurements to age estimates. If the Universe is not isotropic on large scales, the standard cosmological model may need fundamental revision.

Paper #2 🦠 免疫学与分子生物学

STING诱导免疫的突变景观图谱The mutational landscape of STING-induced immunity

Nature · 2026 (Open Access)
Bing Zhang, Pengbiao Xu, Yu Meng, Laure Gallay, François Lestelle, Hélène Morel, Marie-Louise Frémond, Bruno E. Correia & Andrea Ablasser
STING（干扰素基因刺激蛋白）是一种进化上保守的免疫信号蛋白，在宿主防御、癌症、衰老和炎症中扮演关键角色。在STING下游，I型干扰素、炎症细胞因子信号和非经典自噬受到一种多层机制的调控，该机制整合了配体诱导的结构转变、蛋白质-蛋白质相互作用以及协调的胞内运输。尽管STING在免疫中居于中心地位且是重要的治疗靶点，但调控其在细胞中激活或失活的序列元件仍然知之甚少。本研究开发了一种大规模平行测定技术，系统地绘制了STING的序列-功能图谱。通过分析数千个单氨基酸变异体，研究者鉴定了决定STING免疫刺激能力及其将配体识别转化为不同信号输出的结构和功能决定因素。冷冻电镜结构分析揭示了调控STING从非活性状态向信号传导状态构象转变的新规律。突变效应广泛存在于整个功能图谱上，可使STING对天然配体2'3'-cGAMP更加敏感，或将干扰素诱导与非经典自噬解耦。这些发现还展示了自然发生的STING蛋白变异体的临床和进化相关性。 STING is an evolutionarily conserved immune signalling protein with key roles in host defence, cancer, senescence, and inflammation. Here, the authors developed a massively parallel assay to systematically chart the sequence-function landscape of STING. Profiling thousands of single amino-acid variants, they identified structural and functional determinants shaping STING's immunostimulatory capacity. Cryo-EM structures of hyperactive variants revealed new regulatory principles for conformational transitions from inactive to signalling-competent states. Mutational effects can sensitize STING towards 2'3'-cGAMP or decouple interferon induction from non-canonical autophagy, demonstrating a diversity of responses accessible through single point substitutions. The data also showed clinical and evolutionary relevance of naturally occurring STING variants.

🔬 核心发现Key Findings

  • 开发了大规模平行测定技术，系统性绘制了STING蛋白数千个单氨基酸变异体的功能图谱Developed a massively parallel assay profiling thousands of single amino-acid STING variants
  • 冷冻电镜揭示了超活跃STING变异体从非活性到信号传导状态构象转变的新调控原则Cryo-EM structures of hyperactive variants revealed new regulatory principles for STING conformational transitions
  • 发现单个点突变即可将干扰素诱导与非经典自噬解耦，展示免疫响应的多样可调性Single point mutations can decouple interferon induction from non-canonical autophagy, revealing tunable immune response diversity
💡 为什么重要：💡 Why it matters: STING是当前最热门的免疫治疗靶点之一，涉及抗肿瘤免疫和自身免疫疾病。这项研究为精准调控STING活性提供了"密码本"，对开发针对癌症和炎症性疾病的下一代STING调节药物具有直接指导意义。 STING is one of the hottest immunotherapy targets, implicated in anti-tumor immunity and autoimmune disease. This study provides a "codebook" for precisely tuning STING activity, directly informing next-generation STING-modulating therapeutics for cancer and inflammatory diseases.

Paper #3 ⚛️ 物理与材料科学

二维异质结构中通过界面电荷转移实现光学制冷Optical cooling by interfacial charge transfer in 2D heterostructures

Nature · 2026
Jiamin Lin, Baixu Xiang, Renguang Liu, Jinyang Ling, Gang Wang, ... Weigao Xu
光学制冷（即固体激光制冷）为量子和电子系统提供了一种无需液氮等低温冷却剂即可实现温度控制的新途径。现有方法依赖声子辅助的上转换光致发光途径，但受到严格的材料纯度和激发条件限制。本研究展示了一种全新的光学制冷路线——在二维半导体异质结构中通过界面电荷转移驱动制冷。光激发的载流子从WSe₂层跨越II型结进入MoSe₂或WS₂层，通过声子辅助的界面电荷转移过程以非辐射方式提取晶格能量。拉曼和光致发光测量显示WSe₂层出现显著的低温特征，瞬态吸收光谱学确认了一种声子辅助、势垒激活的层间电荷转移过程。分子动力学模拟揭示了显著的界面热阻，能够维持温度梯度。这种势垒介导的声子提取机制绕过了对接近100%量子效率或共振激发的要求，为量子、光电子和纳米尺度系统提供了极具前景的无低温冷却剂制冷和热管理策略。 Optical refrigeration offers a cryogen-free route to temperature control for quantum and electronic systems. Existing approaches rely on phonon-assisted up-conversion photoluminescence, constrained by stringent material and excitation requirements. Here, the authors demonstrate a distinct route—interfacial-charge-transfer-driven optical cooling—in 2D semiconductor heterostructures. Photo-excited carriers in WSe₂ cross a type-II junction into MoSe₂ or WS₂, extracting lattice energy nonradiatively through phonon-assisted interfacial charge transfer. Raman and photoluminescence measurements show prominent low-temperature signatures in the WSe₂ layer, with transient absorption spectroscopy identifying a phonon-assisted, barrier-activated interlayer charge transfer. Molecular dynamics simulations reveal prominent interfacial thermal resistance sustaining the temperature gradient. This barrier-mediated phonon extraction bypasses the need for near-unity quantum efficiency or resonant excitation.

🔬 核心发现Key Findings

  • 提出了一种全新的光学制冷机制：通过二维半导体异质结（WSe₂/MoSe₂、WSe₂/WS₂）的界面电荷转移来提取晶格热量A novel optical cooling mechanism: interfacial charge transfer in 2D semiconductor heterostructures (WSe₂/MoSe₂, WSe₂/WS₂) extracts lattice heat
  • 该声子辅助的界面电荷转移过程绕过了传统光学制冷对接近100%量子效率和共振激发的严格要求The phonon-assisted interfacial charge transfer bypasses the stringent near-unity quantum efficiency and resonant excitation requirements of traditional optical cooling
  • 拉曼、光致发光、瞬态吸收光谱和分子动力学模拟多维度验证了制冷效应和界面热阻机制Raman, photoluminescence, transient absorption spectroscopy, and molecular dynamics simulations provide multi-dimensional verification of the cooling effect and interfacial thermal resistance
💡 为什么重要：💡 Why it matters: 量子计算机和先进光电子器件对极低温环境的需求日益迫切，而传统液氦制冷既昂贵又笨重。这项工作开辟了一条全新的无冷却剂制冷路线，有望彻底改变量子器件、红外探测器和纳米电子系统的热管理方式。 Quantum computers and advanced optoelectronic devices increasingly demand cryogenic environments, while traditional liquid helium cooling is expensive and bulky. This work opens a fundamentally new cryogen-free cooling route that could transform thermal management for quantum devices, infrared detectors, and nanoscale electronic systems.
