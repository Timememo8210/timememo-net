Paper #1 ⚛️ 物理学与量子科学

亮压缩真空态驱动的非线性原子隧穿增强效应Nonlinear Atomic Tunnelling Boosted by Bright Squeezed Vacuum

Nature · 20 May 2026
Zhejun Jiang, Shengzhe Pan, Jianqi Chen, Mingyu Zhu, Chenhao Zhao, Yiwen Wang, Ru Zhang, Jianshi Lu, Lulu Han, Suwen Xiong, Dian Wu, Wenxue Li, Shicheng Jiang, Hongcheng Ni & Jian Wu
非线性光学过程由多光子相互作用驱动，而非单光子响应，在激光设备和应用中被广泛利用。通常通过提高光场强度来增强非线性效应，但这受到辐射损伤风险的限制。本研究提出了一种基于量子光的全新策略——利用「亮压缩真空」（Bright Squeezed Vacuum, BSV）来大幅增强非线性隧穿电离过程。研究团队在孤立原子体系中实现了BSV驱动的非线性隧穿电离实验，这是一种驱动高次谐波产生的关键过程，也是阿秒科学的基石。实验表明，平均脉冲能量仅为300纳焦的BSV光，可以实现等效于7.1微焦相干光的有效光强，展现出超过20倍的非线性效应量子增强。这一增强效应通过角条纹技术测量BSV和相干光产生的光电子动量谱的峰值匹配得以验证。此外，研究人员还展示了通过在固定平均脉冲能量下调节关联函数来控制BSV的有效强度，建立了一种利用量子统计而非经典强度标度来调控非线性过程的稳健方法。这一发现为利用定制量子光源实现量子控制强场动力学开辟了新的道路，有望推动阿秒物理、高次谐波产生以及量子光学等领域的重大进展。 Nonlinear optical processes, mediated by multiphoton interactions, are routinely exploited for light-based functionalities. Enhancement traditionally relies on higher-intensity fields, limited by radiation damage. Here the researchers demonstrate an alternative strategy using quantum light—bright squeezed vacuum (BSV)—to boost nonlinear tunnelling ionization of isolated atoms, the pivotal process driving high-harmonic generation and attosecond science. A BSV pulse with average energy of just 300 nJ achieves an effective intensity equivalent to coherent light of 7.1 μJ, demonstrating a more than 20-fold quantum boost. This enhancement is verified through angular streaking measurements matching photoelectron momentum spectra. The team further demonstrates control of the effective BSV intensity by tuning correlation functions at fixed pulse energy, establishing a method to tailor nonlinear processes through quantum statistics rather than classical intensity scaling. These findings could revolutionize quantum-controlled strong-field dynamics using tailored quantum light sources.

🔬 核心发现Key Findings

  • 亮压缩真空（BSV）光以300纳焦的平均脉冲能量实现了超过20倍的非线性隧穿效应增强BSV light with 300 nJ average pulse energy achieves over 20-fold enhancement in nonlinear tunnelling
  • BSV光的有效光强等效于7.1微焦的相干光，展现了量子光在强场物理中的巨大潜力The effective intensity of BSV equals that of 7.1 μJ coherent light, revealing quantum light's enormous potential
  • 通过调节量子统计关联函数（而非经典光强）即可精确控制非线性过程的强度Nonlinear process intensity can be precisely controlled by tuning quantum correlation functions rather than classical intensity
💡 为什么重要：💡 Why it matters: 这项研究首次在原子层面展示了量子光对非线性隧穿过程的巨大增强效应，为量子控制强场物理和阿秒科学提供了全新范式，有望推动更安全、更高效的超快激光技术发展。 This study provides the first demonstration at the atomic level that quantum light can dramatically enhance nonlinear tunnelling, opening a new paradigm for quantum-controlled strong-field physics and attosecond science.

Paper #2 🧠 神经科学与认知

多巴胺驱动母体大脑的持续性重塑Dopamine Drives Persistent Remodelling of the Maternal Brain

Nature · 20 May 2026
Jennifer C. O'Chan, Giuseppina Di Salvo, Ashley M. Cunningham, Sohini Dutta, Elizabeth Brindley, Benjamin H. Weekley, Winnie Chen, Rasika R. Iyer, Ethan Wan, Cindy Zhang, Naguib Mechawar, Gustavo Turecki & Ian Maze
怀孕和产后经历是改变母体生理状态的关键过程，对母体身体和大脑产生持久的需求，导致终生的神经适应。然而，驱动这些持续性改变的精确分子机制尚不清楚。本研究利用全脑转录组分析，定义了由生殖经历诱导的神经可塑性分子图谱，识别出背侧海马结构（dHF）是转录重塑的关键区域。结合单细胞RNA测序和母鼠-幼崽分离实验范式，研究人员进一步发现慢性产后压力通过改变多巴胺动力学显著破坏dHF的适应性，导致多巴胺依赖性组蛋白翻译后修饰——H3多巴胺化的变化，从而因果性地调控下游基因表达和行为的改变。在人类背侧下托（dHF内的一个脑结构）中，研究团队揭示了与产次相关的H3多巴胺化和转录改变的保守模式。此外，通过化学遗传学抑制释放到dHF的多巴胺，研究人员证明了多巴胺调控在调节这些适应中的充分性——该操作在处女雌性小鼠中再现了生殖经历的关键表观基因组和行为特征。总而言之，本研究确立了多巴胺作为人和小鼠中产次诱导神经适应的核心调控因子，揭示了一种将雌性生殖经历转化为大脑持久行为适应的基础性转录机制。 Pregnancy and postpartum experiences impose lasting demands on the maternal brain, resulting in lifelong neural adaptations, though the precise molecular mechanisms remain poorly understood. This study used brain-wide transcriptomic profiling to define the molecular landscape of reproductive experience-induced neuroplasticity, identifying the dorsal hippocampal formation (dHF) as a key site of transcriptional remodelling. Combining single-cell RNA sequencing with a maternal-pup separation paradigm, the researchers found that chronic postpartum stress disrupts dHF adaptations by altering dopamine dynamics, leading to changes in dopamine-dependent histone post-translational modification (H3 dopaminylation) that causally mediate downstream gene expression and behavioural changes. In human dorsal subiculum, conserved patterns of parity-dependent alterations in H3 dopaminylation and transcription were uncovered. Chemogenetic suppression of dopamine release into the dHF recapitulated key epigenomic and behavioural features of reproductive experience in virgin female mice, establishing dopamine's sufficiency. These findings establish dopamine as a central regulator of parity-induced neuroadaptations in both humans and mice.

🔬 核心发现Key Findings

  • 首次绘制了生殖经历诱导的母体大脑全脑转录组变化图谱，锁定背侧海马结构为核心重塑区域First brain-wide transcriptomic map of reproductive experience-induced changes, identifying dorsal hippocampal formation as the core remodelling site
  • 发现多巴胺通过调控组蛋白H3多巴胺化修饰（一种新型表观遗传机制）驱动母体大脑的长期适应Dopamine drives long-term maternal brain adaptations via H3 dopaminylation, a novel epigenetic mechanism
  • 在人类大脑中验证了与产次相关的H3多巴胺化保守模式，建立了从动物到人类的跨物种机制联系Conserved parity-dependent H3 dopaminylation patterns verified in human brain, establishing cross-species mechanistic links
💡 为什么重要：💡 Why it matters: 这项研究揭示了多巴胺作为母性大脑持久重塑的核心驱动力，阐明了「为人母」如何从分子层面永久改变女性大脑。这一发现对理解产后抑郁、母性认知变化及相关精神健康问题具有深远意义。 This study reveals dopamine as the central driver of persistent maternal brain remodelling, showing how motherhood permanently changes the female brain at the molecular level, with profound implications for understanding postpartum depression and related conditions.

Paper #3 🧬 生命科学与基因组学

61万余人大规模循环代谢性状的遗传分析Genetic Analysis of Circulating Metabolic Traits in 619,372 Individuals

Nature · 20 May 2026
Ralf Tambets, Mihkel Jesse, Jaanika Kronberg, Adriaan van der Graaf, Erik Abner, Urmo Võsa, Ida Rahu, Nele Taba, Anastassia Kolde, Dzvenymyra Yarish, Sariyya Abdullayeva, Anastasiia Alekseienko, Andres Veidenberg, Estonian Biobank Research Team, Krista Fischer, Zoltán Kutalik, Tõnu Esko, Kaur Alasoo & Priit Palta
理解遗传变异与复杂性状之间的关联，需要更深入地认识这些变异的分子后果。虽然复杂疾病的全基因组关联分析（GWAS）通常纳入超过百万个体，但分子性状的研究远远落后。本研究在爱沙尼亚生物库和英国生物库中对249种循环代谢性状进行了GWAS荟萃分析，涵盖多达619,372名个体。研究鉴定出来自8,398个基因座的88,127个常见和低频基因座-性状关联，这些关联汇聚于共享的基因和通路。利用统计精细定位、系统性全表型组共定位分析和顺式孟德尔随机化，研究团队探索了代谢性状与疾病结局之间的假定因果关系。一个重要发现是：尽管观察性研究将血浆支链氨基酸（BCAAs）与2型糖尿病相关联，但通过靶向BCAA分解代谢通路来降低BCAA水平不太可能降低2型糖尿病风险。借助大样本量和高质量基因型填补，研究发现19.4%的精细定位变异的次等位基因频率在0.1%至1%之间，且这些变异中预测的错义和剪接改变变异富集了两倍。这些结果突显了将低频变异纳入遗传关联研究的价值，为精准医学和代谢疾病的靶向治疗提供了重要依据。 Interpreting genetic variant associations with complex traits requires understanding their molecular consequences. While disease GWAS routinely profile over one million individuals, molecular trait studies lag behind. This study performed a GWAS meta-analysis of 249 circulating metabolic traits in the Estonian and UK Biobanks covering up to 619,372 individuals. It identified 88,127 common and low-frequency locus-trait associations from 8,398 loci converging on shared genes and pathways. Using statistical fine mapping, phenome-wide colocalization, and cis-Mendelian randomization, the team explored putative causal links between metabolic traits and disease outcomes. A key finding: although observational studies link plasma BCAAs to type 2 diabetes, lowering BCAA levels via the catabolism pathway is unlikely to reduce diabetes risk. Among confidently fine-mapped variants, 19.4% had minor allele frequencies of 0.1–1%, with a twofold enrichment for predicted missense and splice-altering variants. The results highlight the value of integrating low-frequency variants into genetic association studies.

🔬 核心发现Key Findings

  • 在619,372名个体中鉴定出88,127个代谢性状遗传关联，来自8,398个基因座——迄今最大规模的代谢组GWASIdentified 88,127 metabolic trait genetic associations from 8,398 loci in 619,372 individuals — the largest metabolomics GWAS to date
  • 关键发现：降低支链氨基酸水平可能无法降低2型糖尿病风险，挑战了此前的观察性研究结论Key finding: lowering BCAA levels may not reduce type 2 diabetes risk, challenging previous observational conclusions
  • 19.4%的精细定位变异为低频变异（频率0.1-1%），且富集了两倍的错义和剪接改变变异19.4% of fine-mapped variants are low-frequency (0.1–1% MAF) with twofold enrichment in missense and splice-altering variants
💡 为什么重要：💡 Why it matters: 这是迄今最大规模的代谢组遗传学研究，挑战了「降低支链氨基酸可预防糖尿病」的传统认知，为代谢疾病的精准靶向治疗提供了关键遗传学证据。 The largest metabolomics genetics study to date challenges the conventional belief that lowering BCAAs can prevent diabetes, providing critical genetic evidence for precision targeting of metabolic diseases.
