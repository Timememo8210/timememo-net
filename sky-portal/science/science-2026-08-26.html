Paper #1 ⚛️ 物理学与量子科学

用聚焦电子探针实现原子尺度双缝干涉Atomic-scale double-slit interferometry with a focused electron probe

Nature · 2026
Koudai Tabata, Takehito Seki, Toma Susi, Ryo Ishikawa, Naoya Shibata
双缝干涉是量子力学中最经典的实验之一，直观展示了物质的波动性，一直是电子、中子、原子和分子干涉仪的基础。然而，将双缝干涉真正推进到原子尺度此前一直未能实现。在这项研究中，研究人员利用球差校正扫描透射电子显微镜，将聚焦电子探针精确对准硅晶体中相邻的两个原子，让这两个原子充当"双缝"。借助分段与像素化探测器，团队在每个探针位置记录了会聚束电子衍射（CBED）图案，从而观察到来自单个原子对的干涉条纹。由于在有限温度下两个原子"狭缝"会剧烈振动，它们的运动被直接印刻到干涉条纹上，使条纹携带了原子尺度的定量信息。作者建立了基于完备声子谱的关联模型，成功提取了相邻原子间的振动关联系数，并在不同温度范围内验证了该模型的准确性。他们还从"探测路径"视角解释了声子诱导的电子退相干，揭示了导致条纹可见度下降的声子模式选择性。这项工作首次将双缝干涉实验推进到原子长度尺度，为在原位、局部直接探测材料的微观结构和原子振动动力学提供了全新手段。 The double-slit interferometer is one of the most iconic demonstrations of quantum wave-particle duality and underpins modern electron, neutron, atom and molecule interferometers. Extending it to atomic length scales, however, had remained unexplored. In this work, researchers used an aberration-corrected scanning transmission electron microscope to aim a focused electron probe at pairs of adjacent atoms in a silicon crystal, using the two atoms as atomic "slits". Using segmented and pixelated detectors, they recorded convergent-beam electron diffraction (CBED) patterns at each probe position and observed interference fringes emanating from individual atomic pairs. At finite temperature the two atomic slits vibrate strongly, imprinting their motion on the fringes and thereby carrying quantitative atomic-scale information. By developing a full phonon-based correlation model, the authors extracted the vibrational correlation coefficients of neighbouring atoms and validated the model across a range of temperatures. They further interpreted the phonon-induced loss of fringe visibility as a 'which-path' reading of electron decoherence. This study extends double-slit interferometry to the atomic scale for the first time, offering direct, localized access to microscopic structure and vibrational dynamics.

🔬 核心发现Key Findings

  • 首次实现原子尺度双缝干涉——让晶格中相邻两个原子充当"双缝"，聚焦电子探针充当波源First atomic-scale double-slit interference, using two adjacent atoms as the "slits" and a focused electron probe as the wave source
  • 结合球差校正电子探针与分段/像素化探测器，记录会聚束衍射图案并捕获每个原子对的干涉条纹Aberration-corrected electron probe plus segmented/pixelated detectors recorded CBED patterns revealing interference fringes per atomic pair
  • 提取相邻原子间振动关联，并阐明导致条纹可见度下降的声子模式及其退相干机制Extracted vibrational correlations between adjacent atoms and explained the phonon modes and decoherence behind fringe visibility loss
💡 为什么重要：💡 Why it matters: 把双缝干涉推进到原子尺度，能在纳米/原子层面直接、局部地读取材料的微观结构与原子振动动力学，为材料科学和量子探测开启新工具。 Extending double-slit interferometry to the atomic scale offers direct, localized reads of microscopic structure and vibrational dynamics, opening new tools for materials science and quantum probing.

Paper #2 🧬 生命科学与基因组学

冰岛泛基因组参考An Icelandic pangenome reference

Nature · 2026
Guillaume Holley, Hannes P. Eggertsson, et al.
目前绝大多数基因组研究都依赖将短读长序列比对到单一线性参考基因组（如GRCh38），但这种方式存在明显的"参考偏差"——参考基因组无法充分代表人类基因多样性，导致大量变异被漏检或偏向参考等位基因。泛基因组通过同时代表多个单倍型来缓解这一问题，但其结构复杂、计算开销大，严重阻碍其大规模应用。在这项来自冰岛deCODE遗传学研究所的研究中，作者开发了两种新方法：Emblask——一种结合长读长和短读长、面向亲子三人家系的单倍型"双组装"流程，用于构建泛基因组；以及Weaver——一种可大规模对泛基因组进行比对的高效映射工具。利用Emblask，团队组装了698条冰岛单倍型，并将其加入人类泛基因组参考联盟（HPRC）的泛基因组，构建了包含788条单倍型、携带5.141亿个小变异的冰岛泛基因组参考（HPRC-ICE）。将57,630名冰岛人的短读长数据比对到HPRC-ICE后，共确切出9,896万（9896万个）变异，较比对到线性参考增加了6.17%。研究还在低可比性区域发现了新变异来源，包括与早发性帕金森病相关的GBA1致病性SNP，与同型半胱氨酸血症相关的CBS错义SNP，并通过英国生物样本库（429,193名参与者的靶向重映射）验证了GBA1关联。 Most genomic studies map short reads to a single linear reference genome (such as GRCh38), which suffers from "reference bias" — the reference cannot fully represent human diversity, causing many variants to be missed or biased toward the reference allele. Pangenomes mitigate this by representing multiple haplotypes, but their structural complexity and high computational cost hinder adoption. In this study from deCODE genetics/Amgen, the authors introduce two methods: Emblask, a hybrid long- and short-read haplotype-resolved dual-assembly pipeline for pangenome construction from parent–offspring trios, and Weaver, a fast mapping tool for large-scale alignment to pangenomes. Using Emblask they assembled 698 Icelandic haplotypes, added them to the Human Pangenome Reference Consortium (HPRC) pangenome, and built the HPRC-ICE Icelandic pangenome reference (788 haplotypes, 51.41 million small variants). Mapping short reads of 57,630 Icelanders to HPRC-ICE, they called 98.96 million variants — a 6.17% increase over linear-reference mapping. New variants were uncovered in low-mappability regions, including a pathogenic SNP in GBA1 associated with early-onset Parkinson's disease and a missense SNP in CBS pathogenic for homocystinuria, with the GBA1 association replicated by targeted remapping in the UK Biobank (429,193 participants).

🔬 核心发现Key Findings

  • 开发了Emblask（单倍型双组装）与Weaver（大规模泛基因组比对）两个新工具Developed Emblask (haplotype dual-assembly) and Weaver (large-scale pangenome mapping) tools
  • 组装698条冰岛单倍型、构建788条单倍型的HPRC-ICE泛基因组，检出9896万个变异，较线性参考增加6.17%Assembled 698 haplotypes into the 788-haplotype HPRC-ICE pangenome; 98.96 million variants called, up 6.17% vs the linear reference
  • 在低可比性区域发现与早发帕金森病（GBA1）和同型半胱氨酸血症（CBS）相关的致病性变异，并在UK Biobank验证Found pathogenic variants for early-onset Parkinson's (GBA1) and homocysturia (CBS) in low-mappability regions, replicated in the UK Biobank
💡 为什么重要：💡 Why it matters: 泛基因组通过覆盖人体更多单倍型显著提高变异检出率，甚至能发现线性参考"隐藏"的致病突变，正成为精准医学基因组分析的新标准基础设施。 Pangenomes raise variant-calling sensitivity over linear references and can uncover previously "hidden" disease variants, making them an emerging standard infrastructure for precision-medicine genomics.

Paper #3 🌍 地球与环境科学

热带冰芯揭示的全球大气甲烷记录A global atmospheric methane record from a tropical ice core

Nature · 2026
Kara Lamantia, et al.
大气甲烷（CH₄）是重要的温室气体，热带湿地被普遍认为是最主要的自然排放源，但由于对湿地面积和甲烷生成量的不确定，模型对CH₄排放趋势的估算差异很大。以往的历史重建主要依赖极地冰芯记录，但极地记录难以完整反映热带地区在CH₄收支中的贡献。该研究对秘鲁安第斯山瓦斯卡兰雪峰（Nevado Huascarán，南纬9.122°、6,768米海拔）的冰芯（Summit Core A）进行了分析，重建了长达2,000年的热带大气甲烷浓度记录。结果显示，这一热带记录的总体趋势与幅度与极地记录（如GISP2、WAIS）大体一致，确认了它首次作为低纬度冰芯准确捕捉到一致的全球CH₄记录。同时，约1530至1999年的δ¹³C-CH₄同位素测量结果与热带源占主导的特征相吻合。将这些数据整合进大气四箱模型后，模型显示过去两个千年赤道甲烷源强度持续占据主导地位，且热带排放量高于此前仅基于极地冰芯的估算，有力支持了"低纬度排放主导工业化前期CH₄变化"的长期假说。研究凸显了热带冰芯在重建全球甲烷演变和纬度分布方面的关键价值。 Atmospheric methane (CH₄) is the second most important greenhouse gas, and tropical wetlands are widely regarded as its largest natural source — yet uncertainties about wetland extent and CH₄ production cause large discrepancies among modelled emission trends. Most historical reconstructions rely on polar ice cores, which resolve the tropical contribution poorly. This study analyses ice drilled from the summit (Summit Core A) of Nevado Huascarán in the Peruvian Andes (9.122° S, 6,768 m), producing a 2,000-year record of tropical atmospheric CH₄. Its trends and magnitudes are broadly consistent with polar records, establishing it as the first low-latitude core to reliably capture a global CH₄ record. δ¹³C-CH₄ isotope measurements (c. 1530–1999 CE) align with a dominant tropical source signature. Integrated into a four-box atmospheric model, the record indicates sustained equatorial dominance of CH₄ source strength over the past two millennia, with equatorial emissions higher than polar-only estimates — supporting the long-standing hypothesis that low-latitude emissions dominated pre-industrial CH₄ variability. The study highlights the importance of tropical ice cores for reconstructing CH₄ variability and its latitudinal distribution.

🔬 核心发现Key Findings

  • 首次从低纬度热带冰芯获得一致的全球甲烷记录，覆盖2000年历史First low-latitude ice core to capture a consistent global CH₄ record spanning 2,000 years
  • 检测记录与极地记录一致，δ¹³C-CH₄同位素支持以热带源为主导Trajectory matches polar records, with δ¹³C-CH₄ isotopes pointing to a dominant tropical source
  • 四箱模型揭示赤道甲烷源强度在过去两千年持续占主导，且排放量高于此前仅基于极地的估计Four-box modelling shows sustained equatorial dominance of CH₄ source strength, with emissions higher than polar-only estimates
💡 为什么重要：💡 Why it matters: 填补了热带大气甲烷历史数据的关键空白，有助于修正全球甲烷收支与气候模型，对理解并应对温室气体减排具有直接意义。 Fills a key gap in tropical methane history, refining global methane budgets and informing greenhouse-gas mitigation efforts.
