Paper #1 ⚛️ 物理学与量子科学

通过极化与量子纠缠探索重子半轻子衰变Exploring baryon semileptonic decays through polarization and entanglement

Nature 657, 92–97 · 2026
BESIII Collaboration et al.
标准模型的核心支柱之一是"卡比博–小林–益川"（CKM）矩阵的幺正性，对这一性质进行精确检验需要独立测定其各矩阵元，其中 |Vus| 控制着奇异夸克到上夸克的跃迁。超子（含奇异夸克的重子）半轻子衰变本可提供另一种独立的探测途径，但由于以往实验缺乏足够的运动学信息，对相关形状因子不敏感，这些过程长期未获开发。在本研究中，作者利用正负电子对撞机在 J/ψ 共振上产生的 Λ–Λ̄ 纠缠对的内禀极化与量子纠缠特性，首次测量了 Λ→p e⁻ ν̄ₑ 衰变中的轴矢量耦合与弱磁耦合强度，并首次测定其绝对分支比和弱电耦合。研究通过联合角分布分析从单个事件中提取更多物理信息，显著提升了单事件灵敏度。实验测得轴矢量耦合比 g_av = 0.742，并与第一性原理格点量子色动力学（QCD）计算相结合，提取出 |Vus| 的值，为标准模型提供了独立而严格的检验。 The unitarity of the Cabibbo–Kobayashi–Maskawa (CKM) matrix is a cornerstone of the standard model (SM). Precise tests of this unitarity require independent determinations of its elements, such as |Vus|, which governs the transition between strange and up quarks. Hyperon semileptonic decays offer alternative probes, but they have remained largely untapped because previous experiments lacked sufficient kinematic information, rendering measurements insensitive to the relevant form factors. Here the authors report measurements of the axial-vector and weak-magnetism couplings, as well as the first determinations of the absolute branching fraction and weak-electricity coupling in Λ→pe⁻ν̄ₑ, achieved by exploiting the polarization and quantum entanglement of ΛΛ̄ pairs produced at the J/ψ resonance. By pioneering this approach, the method enhances single-event sensitivity and is broadly applicable to other baryon semileptonic decays, establishing the foundation for a systematic research programme with precision comparable to that of kaon decays, providing a stringent independent test of the SM.

🔬 核心发现Key Findings

  • 首次测定 Λ→p e⁻ ν̄ₑ 衰变的绝对分支比与弱电耦合强度First determinations of the absolute branching fraction and weak-electricity coupling in Λ→pe⁻ν̄ₑ
  • 利用 J/ψ 上产生的 Λ–Λ̄ 纠缠对的极化和量子纠缠提升单事件灵敏度Exploiting polarization and quantum entanglement of ΛΛ̄ pairs produced at the J/ψ resonance to boost sensitivity
  • 结合格点 QCD 提取 CKM 矩阵元 |Vus|，为标准模型提供独立检验Combined with lattice QCD to extract |Vus|, offering an independent test of the standard model
💡 为什么重要：💡 Why it matters: 为标准模型幺正性检验开辟了基于重子半轻子衰变的全新通道，若发现与预期偏离，可能是超越标准模型新物理的首个信号。 Opens a new channel for testing CKM unitarity via baryon semileptonic decays; any deviation could signal physics beyond the standard model.

Paper #2 🧬 医学与衰老研究

晚年用司美格鲁肽治疗可减缓雌鼠衰老并延长寿命Late-life semaglutide treatment slows ageing and extends lifespan in female mice

Nature · 2026
Danica Chen et al.
司美格鲁肽（semaglutide）是GLP-1受体激动剂，广泛用于糖尿病和减重治疗，但能否真正"延缓衰老本身"仍未明确。在本研究中，加州大学伯克利分校的团队以20月龄（相当于人类60多岁）的雌性小鼠为对象，连续注射司美格鲁肽3个月。结果显示，治疗显著改善小鼠的生理功能，削弱了多项自然衰老的标志（hallmarks），并调节了营养感知通路和保守的衰老调控基因表达。在生命周期实验里，持续接受治疗的小鼠中位寿命比对照组延长约12%（从约742天增至834天）。有趣的是，与仅限制饮食（24%热量限制，与治疗组进食量匹配）的组相比，司美格鲁肽在探索行为、空间记忆和血糖维持等指标上甚至超越了热量限制的获益，而其代谢率几乎不变。基因表达分析显示，司美格鲁肽服用的肝脏基因特征与热量限制高度重叠（抑制炎症与脂肪加工、上调血糖处理和蛋白质维护相关基因），提示两者共享核心机制，但药物在认知等方面具有额外优势。 Semaglutide, a GLP-1 receptor agonist widely used in diabetes and weight loss, has not yet been shown to slow physiological ageing itself. Here the UC Berkeley team treated 20-month-old female mice (roughly equivalent to a 60-year-old woman) with semaglutide for three months. Treatment improved physiological function, attenuated multiple hallmarks of ageing and modulated nutrient sensors and conserved genetic regulators of ageing. In a lifespan cohort, treated mice had a median lifespan roughly 12% longer than controls (from about 742 to 834 days). Notably, compared with a calorie-restricted group (24% diet restricted to match intake), semaglutide surpassed the dietary benefits on exploratory behaviour, spatial memory and blood-sugar maintenance, while metabolic rate remained largely unchanged. Gene-expression analysis revealed that the liver transcriptional signature of semaglutide overlaps heavily with calorie restriction (dampening inflammation and fat processing, upregulating glucose-handling and protein-maintenance genes), implying shared core mechanisms but with additional cognitive benefits for the drug.

🔬 核心发现Key Findings

  • 20月龄雌鼠接受3个月司美格鲁肽治疗，中位寿命延长约12%（742→834天）3-month late-life treatment extended median lifespan in female mice by ~12% (742 to 834 days)
  • 改善肌肉、认知与干细胞再生能力，抑制炎症等多项衰老标志Improved muscle, cognition and stem-cell regeneration while dampening ageing hallmarks including inflammation
  • 在探索、空间记忆和血糖维持上优于匹配的热量限制组，提示不仅是"少吃"的替代Outperformed matched calorie restriction on exploration, spatial memory and glucose control, beyond a mere 'eat less' proxy
💡 为什么重要：💡 Why it matters: 为 GLP-1 类药物作为真正"长寿药"提供了首个有力动物证据，未来有望将抗衰老获益与减重、降糖疗效统一起来。 Offers the strongest animal evidence yet that GLP-1 drugs could act as true longevity agents, potentially unifying anti-ageing benefits with weight-loss and glucose control.

Paper #3 🔬 分子与合成生物学

改造核糖体使其翻译自身 RNA 编码的蛋白质Rewiring the ribosome to translate proteins encoded in its own RNA

Nature · 2026
Kasra Alizadeh, Dorota Klepacki, Nora Vázquez-Laslop, Alexander S. Mankin
核糖体是细胞内合成蛋白质的分子机器，其自身由核糖体RNA（rRNA）与多种蛋白质构成。几十年来，一个根本问题悬而未决：核糖体能否翻译编码在自身rRNA内的蛋白质？在本研究中，芝加哥伊利诺伊大学（UIC）的团队给出了肯定的回答。研究人员在细菌核糖体中发现并鉴定出rRNA内部存在能够被核糖体本身识别、起始翻译的信号序列——即"顺式翻译"（cis-translation）位点。当这些序列位于rRNA的特定结构域时，正在合成的核糖体可以通过内部的 mRNA 通道重新定向，把 rRNA 自身当作模板进行翻译，产生由rRNA编码的多肽。这一过程揭示了核糖体既是"机器"又是"原料"的自我编码能力，打破了此前认为核糖体只能翻译外源信使RNA的传统认知，为理解核糖体生物合成、细胞应激调控以及早期生命起源中RNA世界的演化提供了全新视角。 Whether a ribosome can translate proteins encoded within its own ribosomal RNA (rRNA) has remained unclear. Here researchers at the University of Illinois Chicago show that bacterial ribosomes can synthesize proteins encoded within their own RNA. They identify signal sequences inside rRNA that can be recognized by the ribosome itself to initiate translation, a phenomenon of cis-translation whereby the elongating ribosome redirects its mRNA channel to use its own rRNA as a template. This reveals the ribosome's self-encoding capacity — at once machine and raw material — overturning the long-held view that ribosomes translate only exogenous messenger RNA, and offering new perspectives on ribosome biogenesis, cellular stress regulation and the origin of life in the RNA world.

🔬 核心发现Key Findings

  • 证实细菌核糖体能翻译编码在自身 rRNA 内的蛋白质Demonstrated that bacterial ribosomes can synthesize proteins encoded within their own rRNA
  • 鉴定出 rRNA 内部可被核糖体自身识别和起始翻译的信号位点（顺式翻译）Identified self-recognized cis-translation initiation signals within rRNA
  • 揭示核糖体"向内翻译"的自我编码能力，突破传统 mRNA 模板范式Revealed the ribosome's inward self-coding, challenging the exogenous-mRNA paradigm
💡 为什么重要：💡 Why it matters: 刷新了对核糖体这一生命核心机器功能的理解，并可能为 RNA 世界假说与新型基因表达调控手段提供关键线索。 Rewrites our understanding of the ribosome's role in life and may inform the RNA-world hypothesis and new gene-expression engineering tools.
