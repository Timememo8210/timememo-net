Paper #1 🔬 材料科学与凝聚态物理

科学家打破常压超导30年纪录：压力淬火实现151K零电阻Scientists Break 30-Year Superconductivity Record at Normal Pressure: Pressure Quenching Achieves 151K Zero Resistance

Proceedings of the National Academy of Sciences (PNAS) · 2026
Ching-Wu Chu, Liangzi Deng et al. (Texas Center for Superconductivity, University of Houston)
休斯顿大学德克萨斯超导中心（TcSUH）的研究人员实现了凝聚态物理学领域的一项重大突破：在常压条件下将超导转变温度提升至151K（约-122°C），打破了由汞基铜氧化物Hg1223保持了超过30年的133K常压纪录。该研究由超导领域先驱朱经武教授和邓良子教授领衔完成，成果发表于《美国国家科学院院刊》（PNAS）。 研究团队采用了一种名为"压力淬火"（pressure quenching）的创新技术路线。具体而言，研究人员首先对材料施加极高压力以增强其超导特性并提升转变温度，在保持压力的状态下将材料冷却至特定温度后，突然释放压力。这一快速释压过程有效"锁定"了增强后的超导特性，使得材料在恢复常压条件后仍能保持优异的超导性能。 此前，虽然科学家已在高压条件下实现了室温超导，但维持高压的设备成本极高，严重制约了实际应用。这项研究的核心意义在于证明了一条可行路径：先在高压下获得高温超导态，再通过淬火手段将其"冻结"到常压环境中。尽管目前151K距离室温（约300K）仍有约140度的差距，但朱经武教授表示，随着更多研究力量的投入，这一目标有望最终实现。该成果对高效电力传输、磁共振成像、量子计算和聚变能源等领域具有深远的潜在影响。 Researchers at the Texas Center for Superconductivity (TcSUH) at the University of Houston have achieved a major breakthrough in condensed matter physics: raising the superconducting transition temperature to 151K (approximately -122°C) under ambient pressure conditions, breaking the 133K ambient-pressure record held by the mercury-based copper oxide Hg1223 for more than 30 years. The team employed an innovative technique called "pressure quenching." Researchers first subjected the material to extremely high pressure to enhance its superconducting behavior and increase its transition temperature. While still under pressure, the material was cooled to a carefully chosen temperature before the pressure was suddenly removed. This rapid release effectively preserved the enhanced superconducting properties, allowing the material to remain superconducting even after returning to normal pressure conditions. While room-temperature superconductivity at ambient pressure remains elusive (room temperature is roughly 300K), this achievement represents a crucial step forward. The research, led by superconductivity pioneer Ching-Wu Chu and Liangzi Deng, demonstrates a viable pathway toward practical superconducting technologies that could transform power grids, medical imaging, quantum computing, and fusion energy.

🔬 核心发现Key Findings

  • 在常压条件下实现151K超导转变温度，打破保持了30年的133K纪录，提升了18度Achieved 151K superconducting transition temperature at ambient pressure, breaking the 30-year 133K record by 18 degrees
  • 创新性地使用"压力淬火"技术：先高压增强超导性，再快速释压"冻结"超导态Pioneered "pressure quenching" technique: enhance superconductivity under high pressure, then rapidly release pressure to "freeze" the superconducting state
  • 证明高压诱导的超导态可以在常压下保持稳定，为实用化开辟新路径Demonstrated that pressure-induced superconducting states can remain stable at ambient pressure, opening new pathways toward practical applications
💡 为什么重要：💡 Why it matters: 电力传输中约8%的电能因电阻而损耗，常压高温超导的实现有望节省数十亿美元并大幅减少环境影响。这是通往"室温常压超导"圣杯的关键一步。 About 8% of electricity is lost to resistance in power grids. Practical ambient-pressure high-temperature superconductors could save billions of dollars and dramatically reduce environmental impact — a crucial step toward the holy grail of room-temperature superconductivity.

Paper #2 ⚛️ 粒子物理与量子科学

反氢基态超精细分裂测量精度提升百倍至百万分之四Four ppm Measurement of the Antihydrogen Ground-State Hyperfine Splitting

Nature · Volume 653, Pages 1022–1026 · 2026
R. Akbari, L. O. de Araujo Azevedo, C. J. Baker, W. Bertsche, N. M. Bhatt ... M. Charlton, J. S. Hangst & The ALPHA Collaboration (CERN)
CERN的ALPHA合作组在反物质精密测量领域取得了里程碑式进展。研究团队将反氢原子（氢原子的反物质对应体）基态超精细分裂能量（hyperfine splitting）的测量精度从之前的百万分之400（400 ppm）大幅提升至百万分之4（4 ppm），精度提高了整整100倍。该成果发表于《Nature》杂志。 氢原子作为量子理论的基石，其光谱线已被测量到万亿分之一的精度。反氢原子则是验证物质-反物质对称性的关键探针——通过比较氢与反氢的光谱特性，科学家可以严格检验CPT对称性（电荷共轭、宇称反演和时间反演联合对称性），这是粒子物理标准模型的基石之一。 此次测量的4 ppm精度使得实验结果首次对反质子（antiproton）的内部结构变得敏感。此前1S-2S光谱测量虽然达到了万亿分之一的精度，但超精细分裂测量对反质子的磁矩和内部结构提供了互补性的信息。这一精度提升意味着如果反氢与氢之间存在差异，其效应可能在0.0004%的水平上被探测到，为寻找超越标准模型的新物理提供了更严格的约束条件。 The ALPHA Collaboration at CERN has achieved a landmark advance in antimatter precision measurement. The team improved the measurement precision of the antihydrogen ground-state hyperfine splitting energy from the previous 400 parts per million (ppm) to just 4 ppm — a hundredfold improvement in accuracy. The results were published in Nature. Hydrogen serves as a touchstone for quantum theory, with its spectral lines measured to parts-per-trillion precision. Antihydrogen, the antimatter counterpart, is a crucial probe for testing matter-antimatter symmetry. By comparing the spectral properties of hydrogen and antihydrogen, scientists rigorously test CPT symmetry — charge conjugation, parity inversion, and time reversal — a cornerstone of the Standard Model of particle physics. The 4 ppm precision makes the measurement sensitive to the internal structure of the antiproton for the first time. While previous 1S-2S spectroscopic measurements achieved parts-per-trillion precision, hyperfine splitting provides complementary information about the antiproton's magnetic moment and internal structure. This precision means any matter-antimatter asymmetry at the 0.0004% level could be detected, providing tighter constraints on physics beyond the Standard Model.

🔬 核心发现Key Findings

  • 将反氢基态超精细分裂测量精度从400 ppm提升至4 ppm，精度提高100倍Improved antihydrogen hyperfine splitting measurement from 400 ppm to 4 ppm — a 100x precision increase
  • 测量精度首次达到对反质子内部结构敏感的量级，为CPT对称性检验提供新维度Precision now reaches the level where results are sensitive to the internal structure of the antiproton, providing a new dimension for CPT symmetry tests
  • 与1S-2S光谱测量形成互补，共同构成目前最严格的物质-反物质对称性检验体系Complements 1S-2S spectroscopic measurements, together forming the most stringent test system for matter-antimatter symmetry
💡 为什么重要：💡 Why it matters: 宇宙中物质-反物质的不对称性是物理学最大的未解之谜之一。这项测量为寻找超越标准模型的新物理提供了前所未有的精确约束，任何微小的偏差都可能揭示宇宙为何由物质而非反物质构成。 The matter-antimatter asymmetry of the universe is one of physics' greatest unsolved mysteries. This measurement provides unprecedentedly precise constraints for physics beyond the Standard Model — any tiny deviation could reveal why the universe is made of matter, not antimatter.

Paper #3 🧠 神经科学与认知

海马体CA3到CA1区的稀疏到密集编码转换机制Sparse-to-Dense Coding Transformation Between Hippocampal Areas CA3 and CA1

Nature · 2026
Shir R. Maimon, Tamir Eliav, Johnatan Aljadeff, Aviya Shalev, Yishai Gronich, Nikita M. Finger, Keegan E. Eveland, Cynthia F. Moss, Liora Las & Nachum Ulanovsky (Weizmann Institute of Science; University of Chicago; Johns Hopkins University)
魏茨曼科学研究所的Nachum Ulanovsky团队在神经科学领域取得了重要发现，揭示了海马体两个核心子区域——CA3和CA1——之间根本性的编码方式差异。该研究利用蝙蝠在长达200米的飞行隧道中自由飞行的独特实验范式，颠覆了此前认为CA3和CA1具有相似空间编码模式的传统认知。 此前研究主要使用小尺寸实验环境（如1-2米的小围栏），发现CA1和CA3的位置细胞（place cells）表现出高度相似的空间编码特性，这引发了"为什么两个连续处理阶段使用相同编码"的疑问。本研究假设这种相似性是实验范式的局限所致——实验空间太小，无法充分展现两个区域的编码差异。 研究团队在蝙蝠身上同时记录了CA1和CA3的神经元活动，在6米到200米不等的5种环境尺寸中进行实验。结果显示出高度显著的编码差异：CA1神经元展现"密集编码"（dense coding），单个细胞具有多个位置野；而CA3神经元展现"超稀疏编码"（ultrasparse coding），绝大多数细胞仅有一个位置野。尽管编码密度截然不同，两个区域的位置野大小在不同环境尺寸下却高度相似。 通过神经网络模型，研究者证明这种稀疏到密集的转换可以促进新空间地图的快速学习。此外，在大型多隔室环境中，位置细胞还表现出强烈的轨迹历史调制效应——一种持续超过100米的回顾性编码（retrospective coding），揭示了海马体空间编码的上下文依赖性。 A team led by Nachum Ulanovsky at the Weizmann Institute of Science has made an important discovery in neuroscience, revealing fundamentally different coding schemes between two core hippocampal subregions — CA3 and CA1. Using a unique experimental paradigm of bats flying freely in tunnels up to 200 meters long, the study overturns the conventional view that CA3 and CA1 share similar spatial coding patterns. Previous studies, primarily using small experimental environments (1-2 meter enclosures), found highly similar spatial coding between CA1 and CA3 place cells, raising the question of why two consecutive processing stages would use identical coding. The researchers hypothesized that this similarity was an artifact of small experimental arenas. By simultaneously recording from CA1 and CA3 neurons in bats across 5 environment sizes ranging from 6m to 200m, the team discovered highly distinct neural coding: CA1 neurons exhibited dense spatial coding with multiple place fields per cell, while CA3 neurons showed ultrasparse coding, with most cells having only a single place field. Despite this marked difference in coding density, place field sizes were remarkably similar between the two subregions. Using neural network modeling, the researchers showed that this sparse-to-dense transformation can facilitate fast learning of new spatial maps. They also found that in large multicompartment environments, place cells were strongly modulated by trajectory history — a retrospective coding effect lasting over 100 meters — revealing the context-dependent nature of hippocampal spatial coding.

🔬 核心发现Key Findings

  • CA3和CA1具有根本不同的空间编码策略：CA3为超稀疏编码（单位置野），CA1为密集编码（多位置野）CA3 and CA1 use fundamentally different spatial coding strategies: CA3 employs ultrasparse coding (single place fields), while CA1 uses dense coding (multiple place fields)
  • 此前实验未发现差异是因为实验空间太小——在200米隧道中差异显著呈现Previous experiments missed the difference because arenas were too small — the distinction became dramatic in 200m tunnels
  • 稀疏到密集的转换能促进新空间地图的快速学习，位置细胞还表现出持续超过100米的轨迹历史效应The sparse-to-dense transformation facilitates fast learning of new spatial maps; place cells showed trajectory-history effects lasting over 100 meters
💡 为什么重要：💡 Why it matters: 这是首次在自然尺度环境中揭示海马体内部的编码转换机制，颠覆了40年来关于CA3和CA1功能相似性的传统认知，对理解大脑如何高效编码空间信息和学习新环境具有深远意义。 This is the first study to reveal hippocampal coding transformations at naturalistic scales, overturning 40 years of conventional wisdom about CA3-CA1 functional similarity — with profound implications for understanding how the brain efficiently encodes spatial information and learns new environments.
