Paper #1 ⚛️ 物理学与量子科学Physics & Quantum Science

受激霍金辐射在光学类比中的反作用Backreaction of Stimulated Hawking Radiation in an Optical Analogue

Nature · July 2026
Lorenzo M. Procopio, Raul Aguero-Santacruz, David Bermudez & Ulf Leonhardt
霍金辐射是黑洞事件视界处量子粒子的发射现象，它将引力、量子力学和热力学三大领域联系在一起。然而，霍金辐射从未在天文观测中被探测到，仅在实验室类比系统中被观察到，且在太空中直接观测到的可能性微乎其微。霍金辐射的能量必然来源于黑洞周围的引力场，但场量子究竟如何产生霍金量子这一核心机制此前一直未知。本研究利用光纤光学系统构建了事件视界的类比模型，从理论和实验两个层面揭示了霍金辐射的产生过程。研究团队在光纤光学类比中发现，此前学界普遍认为霍金辐射来源于一个复杂的级联过程，但他们识别出了一个简洁、直接的物理过程，并通过实验观察到了该过程如何反过来作用于光场本身。这一反作用效应（backreaction）的实验验证，为理解黑洞辐射机制提供了关键证据。研究结果表明，类似的直接过程可能同样存在于其他实验室类比系统乃至真实的引力场中，为揭示黑洞如何辐射提供了新的理论框架和实验线索。 Hawking radiation — the emission of quantum particles at the event horizon of a black hole — connects gravity with quantum mechanics and thermodynamics. However, Hawking radiation has never been observed in astronomy, only in laboratory analogues, and the chances of ever observing it in space are astronomically small. The energy of Hawking radiation must come from the gravitational field around the black hole, but how field quanta generate Hawking quanta has been unknown. This study uses a fibre-optical analogue of the event horizon and provides both theoretical and experimental evidence for the process that generates Hawking radiation. The team identified a simple, direct process — contrary to the previously assumed complicated cascaded mechanism — and experimentally observed how this process reacts back onto the optical field. These findings suggest an equally direct process may exist in gravitational fields, shedding light on how black holes might radiate.

🔬 核心发现Key Findings

  • 首次在光纤光学类比系统中实验观测到霍金辐射的反作用效应（backreaction），即辐射过程反过来影响产生它的光场First experimental observation of Hawking radiation backreaction in a fibre-optical analogue system
  • 推翻了霍金辐射源于复杂级联过程的传统认知，识别出一个简洁直接的量子产生机制Identified a simple, direct quantum generation process, overturning the assumption of a complicated cascaded mechanism
  • 理论预测和实验结果高度一致，为其他实验室类比系统乃至真实引力场中的类似过程提供了新框架Theory and experiment agree closely, providing a new framework for analogous processes in other systems and possibly in gravitational fields
💡 为什么重要：💡 Why it matters: 霍金辐射是连接广义相对论与量子力学的关键现象，本研究首次实验验证了辐射的反作用机制，为最终理解黑洞如何辐射能量迈出了重要一步。 Hawking radiation bridges general relativity and quantum mechanics. This study provides the first experimental evidence of the backreaction mechanism, a crucial step toward understanding how black holes radiate energy.

Paper #2 🔬 材料科学与化学Materials Science & Chemistry

同分异构体多氢键网络实现蓝色钙钛矿LEDIsomeric Multi-Hydrogen-Bonding Enables Blue Perovskite LEDs

Nature · July 2026
Yuanzhi Wang, Chengxi Zhang, Yingguo Yang, Lingmei Kong, ... Ning Wang & Xuyong Yang
钙钛矿发光二极管（PeLEDs）近年来取得了巨大进展，但蓝色PeLEDs的电致发光性能远远落后于红绿两色，严重制约了PeLED技术在全彩显示中的广泛应用。蓝色发光材料的宽带隙要求器件在更高工作电压下运行，这加剧了钙钛矿材料的八面体结构不稳定性——这是阻碍蓝色PeLEDs效率提升的核心瓶颈。本研究提出了一种基于同分异构体分子的创新策略，通过在钙钛矿薄膜内部及界面处构建多氢键网络来同时提升效率和稳定性。研究团队在空穴传输层与发光层之间引入O-苄基羟胺盐酸盐（OBCl），其作为氢键供体与钙钛矿无机骨架结合，增强了结构稳定性并利用其大偶极矩降低了空穴能量势垒。同时，将同分异构体N-苄基羟胺盐酸盐（NBCl）添加到钙钛矿中，为与OB+和钙钛骨架形成氢键提供了受体和供体位点。这种同分异构分子氢键网络不仅增强了OB+界面分子诱导的钙钛矿薄膜择优取向，还提升了载流子迁移率并进一步强化了材料稳定性。最终实现的蓝色PeLEDs在463纳米波长下外量子效率达16.8%，在468纳米下达22.0%——这是目前纯蓝光和深蓝光PeLEDs的最优性能水平。 Despite huge progress in perovskite light-emitting diodes (PeLEDs), blue PeLEDs lag far behind in performance, constraining full-colour display applications. The wider bandgaps of blue emitters require higher voltages, intensifying the octahedral instability of ionic perovskites. This study constructs hydrogen-bonding networks using isomeric molecules — OBCl at the interface and NBCl within the perovskite — to enhance both efficiency and stability. The result: blue PeLEDs with external quantum efficiencies of 16.8% at 463 nm and 22.0% at 468 nm, representing state-of-the-art performance for pure- and deep-blue PeLEDs.

🔬 核心发现Key Findings

  • 利用同分异构体分子OBCl和NBCl在钙钛矿内部及界面构建多氢键网络，首次实现高效稳定的纯蓝光钙钛矿LEDFirst efficient and stable pure-blue PeLEDs using isomeric molecules OBCl and NBCl to build multi-hydrogen-bonding networks
  • 外量子效率在463nm处达16.8%、468nm处达22.0%，创下纯蓝光和深蓝光PeLEDs的世界纪录Record external quantum efficiencies: 16.8% at 463 nm and 22.0% at 468 nm, world-leading for pure- and deep-blue PeLEDs
  • 氢键网络策略同时解决了效率低和稳定性差两大瓶颈，为全彩钙钛矿显示技术铺平道路The hydrogen-bonding strategy simultaneously solves both efficiency and stability bottlenecks, paving the way for full-colour perovskite displays
💡 为什么重要：💡 Why it matters: 蓝色钙钛矿LED是全彩显示技术的最后一块拼图，22%的外量子效率标志着钙钛矿显示从实验室走向商业化应用的关键突破。 Blue perovskite LEDs are the missing piece for full-colour displays. A 22% external quantum efficiency marks a critical breakthrough toward commercial perovskite display technology.

Paper #3 🧠 神经科学与认知Neuroscience & Cognition

恢复皮层去抑制改善亨廷顿舞蹈症表型Restoring Cortical Disinhibition Improves Huntington's Disease Phenotypes

Nature · July 2026 · Open Access
Sonja Blumenstock, David Arakelyan, Nicholas del Grosso, ... Irina Dudanova & Takaki Komiyama
亨廷顿舞蹈症（HD）是一种毁灭性的遗传性运动障碍疾病，目前尚无治愈方法。虽然HD的单基因致病基础（HTT基因外显子1的CAG重复扩展）已十分明确，但导致行为症状的复杂下游效应仍知之甚少。其中皮层功能障碍是重要表现之一，但特定皮层神经元亚型在HD症状中的具体作用几乎未被探索。本研究利用纵向活体双光子钙成像技术，在R6/2转基因HD小鼠模型的运动皮层中，追踪了疾病进展过程中三类皮层抑制性神经元（IN）亚型和兴奋性皮层纹状体（CStr）投射神经元的活动。研究发现，R6/2小鼠的运动缺陷伴随着神经元亚型特异性的运动相关活动异常，其中血管活性肠肽（VIP）阳性抑制性神经元和CStr神经元表现出显著的低活动状态——这一现象在敲入型zQ175DN HD小鼠模型中同样被观察到。最关键的发现是：通过光遗传学手段激活R6/2小鼠的VIP抑制性神经元，能够恢复VIP神经元及其下游CStr神经元的健康活动水平，并显著改善运动缺陷，且行为改善在刺激结束后可持续数天。这一发现揭示了皮层抑制性神经元作为亨廷顿舞蹈症潜在治疗靶点的重要价值。 Huntington's disease (HD) is a devastating movement disorder without a cure. Although the monogenic basis is well defined, the downstream effects underlying behavioural symptoms remain poorly understood. This study used longitudinal in vivo two-photon calcium imaging in R6/2 HD mice to track three cortical inhibitory neuron (IN) subtypes and corticostriatal (CStr) projection neurons. Motor deficits were accompanied by subtype-specific activity abnormalities, including marked hypoactivity of VIP-INs and CStr neurons. Optogenetic activation of VIP-INs restored healthy activity levels and ameliorated motor deficits, with improvements persisting for days. These findings highlight cortical inhibitory neurons as a potential therapeutic target for HD.

🔬 核心发现Key Findings

  • 通过活体双光子钙成像发现，HD小鼠中VIP阳性抑制性神经元和皮层纹状体投射神经元出现显著低活动状态In vivo two-photon imaging revealed marked hypoactivity of VIP-positive inhibitory neurons and corticostriatal neurons in HD mice
  • 光遗传学激活VIP抑制性神经元可恢复下游神经元的正常活动模式，显著改善运动缺陷Optogenetic activation of VIP-INs restored normal activity in downstream neurons and significantly improved motor deficits
  • 行为改善在光刺激结束后持续数天，提示存在持久的神经环路可塑性机制Behavioural improvements persisted for days after stimulation, suggesting durable neural circuit plasticity
💡 为什么重要：💡 Why it matters: 首次将亨廷顿舞蹈症的治疗靶点从纹状体扩展到皮层抑制性神经元，为数百万HD患者提供了全新的治疗思路，光遗传调控的持久效果尤为令人振奋。 For the first time, HD therapeutic targets are extended from the striatum to cortical inhibitory neurons, offering a novel treatment approach. The lasting effects of optogenetic modulation are particularly exciting.
