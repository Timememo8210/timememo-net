Paper #1 ⚛️ 量子计算

98量子比特离子阱量子计算机实现全连通架构A 98-qubit trapped-ion quantum computer with all-to-all connectivity

Nature · 2026-06-17
Anthony Ransford, M. S. Allman, ... Justin G. Bohnet et al. (Quantinuum)
量子计算机要超越经典计算能力，既需要高保真度操作，也需要大规模量子比特。离子阱平台在所有量子计算方案中展示了最高的门保真度，但如何在扩大比特规模的同时保持性能一直是核心挑战。本研究报告了Quantinuum公司开发的Helios量子处理器——一台基于量子电荷耦合器件（QCCD）架构的98量子比特离子阱量子计算机。Helios采用¹³⁷Ba⁺超精细量子比特，通过一个可旋转的离子存储环连接两个量子操作区域，实现了真正的全连通性——任意两个量子比特之间都可以直接执行双比特门操作。该系统还引入了并行化操作提速和全新的实时编译软件栈，支持动态量子程序的执行。在所有操作区域上，Helios实现了单比特门平均错误率2.5×10⁻⁵、双比特门7.9×10⁻⁴、态制备与测量3.3×10⁻⁴，这些指标均未达到物理极限，仍有提升空间。在随机Clifford电路和随机线路采样（RCS）测试中，Helios展示了远超经典模拟能力的性能水平，为量子计算确立了新的保真度和复杂性前沿。 Quantum computers require both high-fidelity operations and large qubit numbers to surpass classical capabilities. Trapped-ion platforms have demonstrated the highest gate fidelities of any modality, but scaling to larger qubit numbers while preserving performance has remained a central challenge. This study reports on Quantinuum Helios, a 98-qubit trapped-ion quantum processor based on the quantum charge-coupled device (QCCD) architecture. Helios features ¹³⁷Ba⁺ hyperfine qubits, all-to-all connectivity enabled by a rotatable ion storage ring connecting two quantum operation regions by a junction, speed improvements from parallelized operations, and a new software stack with real-time compilation of dynamic programs. Average infidelities achieved: 2.5×10⁻⁵ for single-qubit gates, 7.9×10⁻⁴ for two-qubit gates, 3.3×10⁻⁴ for state preparation and measurement (SPAM). Random circuit sampling demonstrates operation well beyond the reach of classical simulation, establishing a new frontier of fidelity and complexity for quantum computers.

🔬 核心发现Key Findings

  • Helios拥有98个量子比特，采用可旋转离子存储环实现全连通架构——任意两个比特之间可直接通信Helios features 98 qubits with all-to-all connectivity via a rotatable ion storage ring connecting two quantum operation regions
  • 单比特门错误率低至2.5×10⁻⁵，双比特门7.9×10⁻⁴，均为离子阱平台最高水平之一Single-qubit gate infidelity of 2.5×10⁻⁵, two-qubit gate 7.9×10⁻⁴ — among the best for trapped-ion platforms
  • 随机线路采样（RCS）测试证明Helios的运算已远超经典计算机的模拟能力，确立了量子计算新的复杂性前沿Random circuit sampling demonstrates operation well beyond classical simulation capability, establishing a new complexity frontier
💡 为什么重要：💡 Why it matters: 这是迄今为止单一离子阱量子处理器达到的最大规模（98比特），且在全连通架构下保持了极高的门保真度。Helios的成功运行证明了QCCD架构的可扩展性，为未来数千比特量级量子计算机的研制铺平了道路。 This is the largest single trapped-ion quantum processor to date (98 qubits) maintaining extremely high gate fidelities under all-to-all connectivity. Helios demonstrates the scalability of the QCCD architecture, paving the way for future thousand-qubit quantum computers.

Paper #2 🧬 分子生物学

冷冻电镜揭示DNA复制起始复合物结构，破解CMGE解旋酶组装之谜Structure of the pre-initiation complex explains CMGE biogenesis

Nature · 2026-06-17 (Open Access)
Thomas Pühringer, Berta Canal, ... John F. X. Diffley & Alessandro Costa
DNA复制必须每个细胞周期精确发生一次，以维持基因组稳定性。当细胞进入S期时，双向DNA复制通过激酶调控的三种激活因子（Cdc45、GINS和Pol ε）招募到已装载双链DNA上的MCM双六聚体来启动。这些蛋白共同组成两个CMGE解旋酶，建立分叉的复制叉。为理解CMGE的生成过程，研究人员使用纯化酵母蛋白重构了复制前起始复合物。冷冻电镜结构揭示了多种启动因子"正在组装"两个对称CMGE复合物的瞬间状态。研究展示了逐步的复合物形成如何重塑MCM以准备DNA打开，以及ATP如何促进启动因子弹射和CMGE成熟。特别值得注意的是，研究团队发现Sld2不仅如预期那样协助GINS招募到MCM上，还参与促进CMGE二聚体的有效分离，且对滞后链从MCM中弹出至关重要。这些发现对理解哺乳动物中Sld2的同源蛋白RECQL4具有直接启示意义，指向了一种在真核生物中保守的复制叉建立机制。 When cells enter S phase, bidirectional DNA replication is initiated through kinase-regulated recruitment of three activators (Cdc45, GINS, Pol ε) to a duplex-DNA-loaded double hexamer of MCM ATPases, forming two CMGE helicases. To understand CMGE biogenesis, researchers reconstituted the pre-initiation complex with purified yeast proteins. The cryo-EM structure reveals firing factors caught in the act of assembling two symmetrical CMGEs. The study shows how stepwise complex formation reshapes MCM for DNA opening, and how ATP promotes firing-factor ejection and CMGE maturation. Sld2 not only facilitates GINS recruitment to MCM but also aids efficient CMGE dimer separation and is essential for lagging strand ejection. These findings have implications for the metazoan Sld2 orthologue RECQL4, pointing to a conserved replication-fork establishment mechanism across eukaryotes.

🔬 核心发现Key Findings

  • 通过冷冻电镜首次捕获了CMGE解旋酶组装的中间态结构，揭示了复制起始的分子机制First cryo-EM capture of CMGE helicase assembly intermediate state, revealing molecular mechanisms of replication initiation
  • 发现Sld2蛋白具有此前未知的双重功能：既协助GINS招募，又促进CMGE二聚体分离和滞后链排出Sld2 discovered to have dual functions: facilitating GINS recruitment while also promoting CMGE dimer separation and lagging strand ejection
  • 阐明了ATP驱动的启动因子弹射和CMGE成熟过程，为理解跨物种保守的复制叉建立机制提供框架Elucidated ATP-driven firing-factor ejection and CMGE maturation, providing a framework for conserved replication-fork establishment
💡 为什么重要：💡 Why it matters: DNA复制是生命最基本的过程之一，其起始步骤出错与癌症等疾病密切相关。该研究填补了复制起始机制的关键空白，Sld2/RECQL4的新功能发现对理解人类复制相关疾病（如Rothmund-Thomson综合征）具有重要临床意义。 DNA replication is one of life's most fundamental processes, and errors in its initiation are linked to cancer and other diseases. This study fills a critical gap in understanding replication initiation, and the newly discovered Sld2/RECQL4 functions have clinical implications for human replication-related diseases like Rothmund-Thomson syndrome.

Paper #3 🔋 能源材料

颠覆传统认知：快速化成工艺让锂离子电池容量提升20%、寿命延长36%Fast formation to reinforce lithium-rich cathodes

Nature · 2026-06-17
Mengjian Fan, Jiantao Li, ... Dong-Liang Peng & Qingshui Xie
锂离子电池制造中的化成（formation）工序通常采用低倍率充放电循环来建立稳定的电极-电解质界面，这一过程耗时且成本高昂。本研究以富锂层状氧化物正极材料为对象，发现传统慢速化成不仅不必要，甚至可能缩短电池寿命。相反，快速化成可以降低生产成本，同时提升电池容量和稳定性。利用多尺度同步辐射技术，研究人员揭示了首次充电后残留的锂离子对后续结构演变和循环性能的关键作用。深度锂脱嵌会导致固有脆弱的缺锂基体发生严重结构退化和容量损失。而快速化成产生的残留锂离子通过"自钉扎效应"增强可逆性，阻止有害的晶格变形，加固离子存储框架。研究证明，将首次充电电流密度从0.2C提高到2C，可将可逆容量提高20%，循环寿命延长36%以上。该方法还可扩展到其他电极体系，为更高效的电池生产提供了全新思路。 Formation in lithium-ion battery manufacturing typically involves low-rate charge-discharge cycles to establish stable electrode-electrolyte interfaces — a time-consuming and costly process. This study on lithium-rich layered oxide cathodes finds that conventional slow formation is not only unnecessary but can shorten battery lifespan. Fast formation, by contrast, reduces production cost while enhancing capacity and stability. Multiscale synchrotron-based techniques reveal that residual lithium ions after the initial charge are critical for structural evolution and cycling performance. Deep lithium de-intercalation causes severe structural degradation due to the inherently fragile lithium-deficient matrix. Residual lithium from fast formation enhances reversibility through a "self-pinning effect," preventing pernicious lattice deformation and reinforcing the ion-storage framework. Increasing initial charge current density from 0.2C to 2C improves reversible capacity by 20% and extends cycle life by more than 36%. This approach extends to other electrode systems, providing insights for more efficient battery production.

🔬 核心发现Key Findings

  • 颠覆电池行业百年惯例：快速化成（2C）比传统慢速化成（0.2C）效果更好——容量提升20%，寿命延长36%Overturns industry convention: fast formation (2C) outperforms slow formation (0.2C) — 20% capacity increase, 36% longer cycle life
  • 同步辐射技术揭示了快速化成产生残留锂离子的"自钉扎效应"，阻止晶格变形、加固结构框架Synchrotron techniques reveal "self-pinning effect" of residual lithium from fast formation, preventing lattice deformation and reinforcing structure
  • 该方法具有普适性，可推广到其他正极/电极体系，有望大幅降低电池制造成本The approach is broadly applicable to other electrode systems, potentially significantly reducing battery manufacturing costs
💡 为什么重要：💡 Why it matters: 电池化成是制造中最耗时的工序之一，占生产成本的相当比例。该发现不仅颠覆了"慢即是好"的传统认知，还提供了同时提升性能、降低成本和缩短生产时间的方案，对电动汽车和储能产业的降本增效具有直接工业价值。 Battery formation is one of the most time-consuming manufacturing steps, representing a significant production cost. This finding overturns the "slow is better" convention, offering a solution that simultaneously improves performance, reduces cost, and shortens production time — with direct industrial value for EVs and energy storage.
