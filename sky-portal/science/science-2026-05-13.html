Paper #1 🚀 天体物理与空间科学

再电离时代中发现一个超暗弱、化学原始的正在形成的星系An Ultra-faint, Chemically Primitive Galaxy Forming in the Reionization Era

Nature · 13 May 2026
Kimihiko Nakajima, Masami Ouchi, Yuichi Harikane, Eros Vanzella, Yoshiaki Ono, Yuki Isobe, Moka Nishigaki, Takuji Tsujimoto, Fumitaka Nakamura, Yi Xu, Hiroya Umeda & Yechi Zhang
宇宙中第一批恒星和星系的形成标志着化学增丰的开端，但对这类原始系统的直接观测一直难以获得。本研究利用詹姆斯·韦伯太空望远镜（JWST）对LAP1-B进行了光谱观测——这是一个位于红移 z = 6.625 的超暗弱星系，对应宇宙大爆炸后仅8亿年的时期。该星系受到强烈的引力透镜放大效应。LAP1-B的气相氧丰度仅为太阳值的（4.2 ± 1.8）× 10⁻³倍，是迄今为止发现的最具化学原始特征的恒星形成星系。该星系表现出异常硬的电离辐射场，与化学增丰的恒星种群或吸积黑洞不符，但与极端金属缺乏恒星的理论预测高度吻合。其星际介质中碳氧丰度比相对于其金属丰度偏高，与在无初始金属条件下形成的恒星群体的核合成产物一致。未探测到的恒星连续谱将其恒星质量限制在3300个太阳质量以下，而从发射线运动学推导的动力学质量远超恒星与气体的总质量，表明暗物质晕占据主导地位。这些发现确立了LAP1-B作为"正在形成的化石"的地位——它是本地宇宙中古老超暗弱矮星系的高红移前身，为我们提供了窥探星系形成最早阶段的珍贵窗口。 The formation of the first stars and galaxies marked the onset of chemical enrichment, yet direct observations of such primordial systems remain elusive. This study presents JWST spectroscopic observations of LAP1-B, an ultra-faint galaxy at redshift z = 6.625, corresponding to a cosmic age of just 800 million years after the Big Bang. Strongly magnified by gravitational lensing, LAP1-B exhibits a gas-phase oxygen abundance of only (4.2 ± 1.8) × 10⁻³ times the solar value, making it the most chemically primitive star-forming galaxy discovered to date. The galaxy displays an exceptionally hard ionizing radiation field inconsistent with chemically enriched stellar populations or accreting black holes, but matching predictions for extremely metal-deficient stars. An elevated carbon-to-oxygen ratio in its interstellar medium is consistent with nucleosynthetic yields from a population formed without initial metals. The stellar mass is constrained to below 3,300 solar masses, while the dynamical mass derived from emission-line kinematics exceeds the combined stellar and gas mass, indicating a dominant dark matter halo. These findings establish LAP1-B as a "fossil in the making" — a direct high-redshift progenitor of ancient ultra-faint dwarf galaxies observed locally, offering a rare window into the earliest stages of galaxy formation.

🔬 核心发现Key Findings

  • LAP1-B是目前已知最具化学原始特征的恒星形成星系，氧丰度仅为太阳值的千分之四，几乎可以视为宇宙第一代恒星的"活化石"LAP1-B is the most chemically primitive star-forming galaxy known, with oxygen abundance just 0.4% of solar — a living fossil of the first stellar generation
  • 星系恒星质量不足3300个太阳质量，但动力学质量远超可见物质，证明暗物质晕主导了该系统的引力结构Stellar mass below 3,300 solar masses yet dynamical mass far exceeds visible matter, proving a dominant dark matter halo
  • 异常偏高的碳氧比与无初始金属条件下恒星核合成理论预测完全吻合，提供了第一代恒星化学印记的直接观测证据Elevated carbon-to-oxygen ratio matches theoretical predictions for nucleosynthesis without initial metals, providing direct observational evidence of first-generation stellar chemistry
💡 为什么重要：💡 Why it matters: 这项发现连接了高红移早期宇宙与本地近邻宇宙中的超暗弱矮星系，首次以直接观测证实了暗物质主导的原始星系形成理论，为理解宇宙从黑暗时代走向再电离的关键转折提供了"正在形成的化石"级别的证据。 This discovery bridges high-redshift early Universe and local ultra-faint dwarf galaxies, providing direct observational confirmation of dark matter-dominated primordial galaxy formation and "fossil-in-the-making" evidence for understanding the transition from cosmic dark ages to reionization.

Paper #2 🧬 生命科学与再生医学

修复溶酶体功能使衰老血液干细胞重返年轻状态Restoring Lysosomal Function Rejuvenates Aging Blood Stem Cells

Cell Stem Cell · May 2026
Saghi Ghaffari et al. (Icahn School of Medicine at Mount Sinai)
西奈山伊坎医学院的研究团队成功在小鼠中逆转了造血干细胞（HSCs）的衰老过程，关键在于修复细胞内被称为"溶酶体"的微小回收中心的缺陷。研究发现，随着机体衰老，HSCs中的溶酶体会变得过度活跃、过度酸化并出现损伤，破坏了干细胞的代谢平衡和表观遗传稳定性，最终导致炎症水平升高和再生能力下降。研究团队利用单细胞转录组学和功能测试，发现通过空泡ATP酶抑制剂阻断溶酶体的过度活动，可以恢复溶酶体的健康状态并改善衰老血液干细胞的功能。处理后，衰老干细胞重新表现出年轻健康细胞的特征——恢复了有效再生、平衡产生血液和免疫细胞以及生成新干细胞的能力。在体外处理实验中，用溶酶体抑制剂处理的衰老干细胞在活体动物中的造血能力提高了八倍以上。更健康溶酶体改善了线粒体DNA的处理，降低了cGAS-STING免疫信号通路的激活水平，从根源上减轻了干细胞炎症和衰老相关的分子信号。这一发现表明血液干细胞的衰老并非不可逆转的命运。 Researchers at the Icahn School of Medicine at Mount Sinai have successfully reversed aging in hematopoietic stem cells (HSCs) in mice by repairing defects in lysosomes — the cell's internal recycling centers. The study reveals that lysosomes in aged HSCs become excessively acidic, damaged, depleted, and abnormally active, disrupting metabolic balance and epigenetic stability while triggering inflammation. Using single-cell transcriptomics and functional testing, the team found that blocking excessive lysosomal activity with a vacuolar ATPase inhibitor restored lysosomal health and dramatically improved aged stem cell function. After treatment, old stem cells regained the ability to regenerate effectively, produce balanced blood and immune cells, and generate new stem cells. In ex vivo experiments, treating old stem cells with the lysosomal inhibitor increased their blood-forming capacity in living animals by more than eightfold. Healthier lysosomes improved mitochondrial DNA processing and reduced activation of the cGAS-STING immune signaling pathway. The findings demonstrate that aging in blood stem cells is not an irreversible fate, opening new therapeutic avenues for age-related blood disorders.

🔬 核心发现Key Findings

  • 衰老HSCs中的溶酶体会变得过度活跃和过度酸化，这是干细胞衰老的核心驱动因素，而非伴随现象Lysosomes in aged HSCs become hyperactive and excessively acidic, identified as a central driver — not just a byproduct — of stem cell aging
  • 通过空泡ATP酶抑制剂"降温"溶酶体活性后，衰老干细胞的造血能力提升了超过8倍，证明衰老干细胞可以被逆转回年轻状态Calming lysosomal activity with a V-ATPase inhibitor increased blood-forming capacity by over 8-fold, proving aged stem cells can be rejuvenated
  • 溶酶体修复通过改善线粒体DNA处理和降低cGAS-STING通路激活，从根源上减轻了干细胞炎症和衰老信号Lysosomal repair addresses root causes by improving mitochondrial DNA processing and reducing cGAS-STING pathway activation
💡 为什么重要：💡 Why it matters: 这项研究首次将溶酶体功能障碍定位为干细胞衰老的核心驱动因子，而非仅仅揭示了一条新的抗衰通路——它证明了衰老的血液干细胞具有重返年轻状态的内在能力，为开发针对衰老相关血液疾病、改善老年患者干细胞移植效果以及整体抗衰老策略开辟了全新方向。 This study positions lysosomal dysfunction as the central driver of stem cell aging and proves that aged blood stem cells retain the intrinsic capacity to revert to a youthful state — opening entirely new therapeutic directions for age-related blood disorders, stem cell transplantation, and systemic anti-aging strategies.

Paper #3 🤖 AI与计算社会科学

国家媒体管控如何影响大语言模型的输出State Media Control Influences Large Language Models

Nature · 13 May 2026
Hannah Waight, Eddie Yang, Yin Yuan, Solomon Messing, Margaret E. Roberts, Brandon M. Stewart & Joshua A. Tucker
全球数百万人通过大语言模型（LLMs）获取信息。虽然多项研究已记录了这些模型的强大说服力，但对于"谁在影响模型本身"这一问题，证据仍然有限。本研究通过六项系统性实验证明，世界各国政府对媒体的控制已经通过训练数据影响了LLMs的输出。研究团队进行了跨国审计，发现LLMs在媒体自由度较低国家的语言中表现出更强的亲政府倾向，而在媒体自由度较高国家的语言中则没有这种倾向。为验证具体的因果机制，研究团队以中国媒体体系为案例进行多层面分析，证明由国家策划和管控的媒体内容确实出现在LLM的训练数据集中。通过在开源权重模型上额外预训练中国国家协调的媒体内容，模型对中国政治制度和领导人产生了更积极的回答。两项商业模型审计研究进一步证实：用中文提问时，模型对中国制度和领导人的回答比用英文提问同样问题更加积极。国家媒体管控对LLM的影响及其跨语言的潜在说服力，意味着各国和强势机构将面临更大的战略动机，试图通过控制媒体来塑造AI模型的输出。 Millions of people worldwide query LLMs for information. While studies have documented the persuasive potential of these models, evidence about who influences the models themselves remains limited. Through six systematic studies, this research demonstrates that government control of media across the world already influences LLM output via training data. A cross-national audit reveals that LLMs exhibit stronger pro-government valence in the languages of countries with lower media freedom than in those with higher media freedom. Using China's media system as a multi-part case study, the team shows that state-scripted and curated media content appears in LLM training datasets. Additional pretraining on Chinese state-coordinated media caused an open-weight model to generate more positive responses about Chinese political institutions and leaders. Two commercial model audit studies confirmed that prompting models in Chinese generates more positive responses about China's institutions and leaders than identical queries in English. The combination of influence and persuasive potential across languages suggests states and powerful institutions face increased strategic incentives to leverage media control to shape LLM output.

🔬 核心发现Key Findings

  • 跨国审计表明：LLMs在媒体自由度较低国家的语言中表现出更强的亲政府倾向，揭示了训练数据中的系统性政治偏见Cross-national audit shows LLMs exhibit stronger pro-government valence in languages of countries with lower media freedom, revealing systematic political bias in training data
  • 以中国为案例的实验证实：国家管控媒体内容确实存在于LLM训练数据中，额外预训练此类内容会使模型产生更多亲政府回答China case study confirms state-controlled media content exists in LLM training data, and additional pretraining generates more pro-regime responses
  • 语言审计揭示"语言切换效应"：同一商业模型，用中文提问比用英文提问产生更积极的中国政治评价，跨语言影响力构成隐蔽的说服渠道Language audit reveals a "language-switching effect": the same commercial model produces more positive evaluations of Chinese politics when queried in Chinese versus English, creating a covert persuasion channel across languages
💡 为什么重要：💡 Why it matters: 这项研究首次以严格实证证据揭示了国家媒体管控如何通过训练数据渗透到AI系统核心输出中，并指出这不仅是一个技术偏见问题——随着LLMs说服力不断增强，各国政府将面临更大的动机去操控媒体以影响全球AI模型的输出，这对AI安全治理和信息生态具有深远的政策启示。 This study provides the first rigorous empirical evidence that state media control permeates AI system outputs through training data, warning that as LLMs grow more persuasive, governments face increasing incentives to manipulate media to influence global AI output — with profound implications for AI safety governance and the global information ecosystem.
