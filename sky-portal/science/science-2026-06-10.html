Paper #1 ⚛️ 物理学与量子科学Physics & Quantum Science

电子-空穴双层结构中的双组分激子凝聚体Two-component exciton condensates in an electron–hole bilayer

Nature · 10 June 2026
Ruishi Qi, Qize Li, Jiahui Nie, Ruichen Xia, Haleem Kim, Hyungbin Lim, Jingxu Xie, Takashi Taniguchi, Kenji Watanabe, Michael F. Crommie, Allan H. MacDonald & Feng Wang
玻色-爱因斯坦凝聚（BEC）是宏观量子相干性的经典体现，而激子因其强相互作用、电学可调性和潜在的多分量自旋序特性，被认为是实现高温BEC的理想固态途径。然而，平衡态激子凝聚的确凿实验证据一直难以获得。本研究在MoSe₂/hBN/WSe₂电子-空穴双层范德华异质结构中，通过探测组成电子和空穴的自旋-谷磁化率，报告了双组分激子玻色-爱因斯坦凝聚体的实验证据。该异质结构具有四种自旋-谷自由度的平衡态激子流体。在稀释制冷机中进行的磁光光谱实验揭示了三个具有不同自旋-谷极化的激子凝聚相：零磁场下，多体基态是两种谷内激子的相干叠加；施加磁场后，谷内凝聚体先在弱临界场处经历一级量子相变转变为双组分谷间凝聚体，随后在高场下变为完全极化的单分量凝聚体。凝聚体特征在密度-温度空间中呈穹顶状分布，最高可持续至约1.8 K。这一成果确立了范德华电子-空穴双层结构作为研究强相互作用多分量激子BEC的通用平台。 Macroscopic quantum coherence emerges when bosons condense into a Bose–Einstein condensate (BEC). Excitons are a long-sought solid-state route to high-temperature BECs with strong interactions, electrical tunability and potentially multicomponent spinor order, but conclusive evidence for equilibrium condensation has remained elusive. Here we report evidence for two-component exciton BECs in MoSe₂/hBN/WSe₂ electron–hole bilayers by probing the spin–valley susceptibility of constituent electrons and holes. This heterostructure hosts equilibrium exciton fluids with four spin–valley flavours. Magneto-optical spectroscopy in a dilution refrigerator reveals three exciton condensate phases with distinct flavour polarizations. At zero magnetic field, the many-body ground state is a coherent superposition of two condensed intravalley exciton flavours. Under a magnetic field, the intravalley exciton condensate first switches to a two-component intervalley condensate through a first-order quantum phase transition at a weak critical field and then turns into a fully polarized single-component condensate at high fields. The condensate signatures form a dome in density–temperature space, persisting up to approximately 1.8 K. Our results establish van der Waals electron–hole bilayers as a versatile platform for strongly interacting, multicomponent exciton BECs.

🔬 核心发现Key Findings

  • 首次在MoSe₂/hBN/WSe₂范德华异质结构中观测到双组分激子玻色-爱因斯坦凝聚体的确凿证据First conclusive evidence for two-component exciton BECs in MoSe₂/hBN/WSe₂ van der Waals heterostructures
  • 揭示了三种具有不同自旋-谷极化的激子凝聚相，包括零场下的谷内相干叠加态和磁场诱导的谷间凝聚态Discovered three exciton condensate phases with distinct spin-valley polarizations, including zero-field intravalley coherent superposition and field-induced intervalley condensates
  • 观察到一级量子相变行为——弱磁场即可驱动凝聚体在谷内和谷间状态之间切换Observed first-order quantum phase transitions driven by weak magnetic fields switching between intravalley and intervalley condensate states
💡 为什么重要：💡 Why it matters: 这项研究为探索宏观量子现象开辟了全新的固态平台。范德华异质结构中可电学调控的多分量激子凝聚体，为量子模拟、拓扑物态研究和未来量子器件设计提供了前所未有的实验可能性。 This work opens a new solid-state platform for exploring macroscopic quantum phenomena. The electrically tunable multicomponent exciton condensates in van der Waals heterostructures offer unprecedented possibilities for quantum simulation, topological matter studies, and future quantum device design.

Paper #2 🌍 地球科学与古生物学Earth Science & Paleontology

迪亚曼蒂纳海区530万年前的深海鲸鱼墓地A 5.3-million-year-old deep-sea whale necropolis in the Diamantina Zone

Nature · 10 June 2026 (Open Access)
Xiaotong Peng, Peng Zhou, Xikun Song, Giovanni Bianucci, Mengran Du, Alberto Collareta, Zhaoming Gao, Tongtong Xie, Mingyao Teng, Daniel Leduc, Sadie Mills, Kaiwen Ta, Jiwei Li, Taoshu Wei, Shamik Dasgupta, Hao Liu, Yuan He, Wenjing Xu, Shuangquan Liu & Hanyu Zhang
鲸落是深海海床上的生物多样性绿洲，但迄今为止从海洋中获得的记录一直稀少且零散。本研究报告了在印度洋东南部迪亚曼蒂纳海区（水深4,616至7,001米）发现的一处庞大鲸鱼墓地，沿着海底延伸约1,200公里。该区域拥有深而广泛的堆积层，包含五个现代自然鲸落群落和476个化石鲸类记录。研究表明，这些鲸类遗骸上栖息着以蛇尾海星、食骨蠕虫和化能合成双壳类为主导的特化生物群落。该区域的化石记录涵盖了现存和已灭绝的深潜喙鲸种类。同位素测年结果显示，这一地区的鲸落至少从530万年前就已经开始发生。这些发现从根本上改变了我们对鲸落生态系统极限和生物地理分布的认知，并确立了某些深海海底作为追溯鲸类地质演化历史的化石档案库的地位。 Whale falls are biodiversity oases at seabeds, yet their record from the oceans has remained sparse and fragmentary. Here we report the discovery of a vast whale necropolis in the Diamantina Zone (4,616- to 7,001-m depth), extending about 1,200 km along the sea floor of the southeastern Indian Ocean. This area has a deep and extensive accumulation comprising five modern natural whale-fall communities and 476 fossil cetaceans recorded. We show that carcasses host specialized communities dominated by brittle stars, bone-boring worms and chemosynthesis-based bivalves and that the fossil record in this area comprises both extant and extinct deep-diving beaked whales. Isotopic dating shows that whale falls in this region have occurred since at least 5.3 million years ago. These findings reshape the understanding of the limits and biogeography of whale-fall ecosystems and establish some deep sea floors as a fossil archive for tracing cetacean evolution over geological time.

🔬 核心发现Key Findings

  • 在印度洋东南部迪亚曼蒂纳海区发现了一处延伸约1,200公里的巨型深海鲸鱼墓地，包含476个化石鲸类标本Discovered a giant deep-sea whale necropolis extending ~1,200 km in the Diamantina Zone of the southeastern Indian Ocean, containing 476 fossil cetacean specimens
  • 同位素测年证实鲸落活动至少持续了530万年，从上新世至今从未间断Isotopic dating confirms whale fall activity has persisted for at least 5.3 million years, from the Pliocene to the present
  • 化石记录包含现存和已灭绝的深潜喙鲸种类，揭示了深海鲸类演化的连续谱系The fossil record includes both extant and extinct deep-diving beaked whale species, revealing a continuous lineage of deep-sea whale evolution
💡 为什么重要：💡 Why it matters: 这是迄今发现的规模最大的深海鲸落化石群，彻底颠覆了我们对深海生态系统生物地理学的认知。它证明深海海底可以保存数百万年的生物演化档案，为理解鲸类演化和深海极端环境生态学提供了全新的窗口。 This is the largest deep-sea whale fall fossil deposit ever discovered, fundamentally changing our understanding of deep-sea ecosystem biogeography. It proves that deep-sea floors can preserve millions of years of evolutionary archives, providing a new window into whale evolution and deep-sea extremophile ecology.

Paper #3 🚀 天体物理学Astrophysics

超重型超高能宇宙射线——破解天照粒子之谜Ultraheavy Ultrahigh-Energy Cosmic Rays

Physical Review Letters · 2026 · Vol. 136, No. 18
B. Theodore Zhang, Kohta Murase, Nick Ekanger, Mukul Bhattacharya, Shunsaku Horiuchi
超高能宇宙射线是来自太空、以远超人造粒子加速器能量轰击地球的粒子。其中最引人注目的例子是2021年由犹他州望远镜阵列探测到的天照粒子，其能量约为240艾电子伏特（约244万亿电子伏特），被列为有史以来最强大的宇宙射线事件之一，与1991年的上帝粒子同属一个稀有类别。然而，天照粒子的来源一直是个谜——其估计到达方向指向一片没有任何明确源头的宇宙空洞。宾州州立大学领导的研究团队在Physical Review Letters上发表的这项新研究提出了一种突破性解释：这些极端宇宙射线可能是比铁更重的超重原子核。研究团队通过详细的计算机模拟，模拟了不同大小的粒子穿越星际空间时的能量得失过程，发现超重原子核在穿越星系际空间时比质子或较轻原子核的能量损失更慢，因此能够携带极端能量穿越宇宙距离到达地球。这一发现不仅有助于解释天照粒子的来源之谜，还为科学家识别能够发射此类粒子的宇宙天体类型提供了新线索。 Ultrahigh-energy cosmic rays are particles from space that slam into Earth with energies far beyond anything produced by human-built particle accelerators. Among the most extraordinary examples is the Amaterasu particle, detected by the Telescope Array in Utah in 2021 at about 240 exa-electron volts, ranking it among the most powerful cosmic-ray events ever observed. Yet scientists still do not know where it came from, as its estimated arrival direction traces back to a cosmic void. New research led by Penn State scientists and published in Physical Review Letters suggests that some of the highest-energy cosmic rays could be ultraheavy atomic nuclei heavier than iron. Through detailed computer simulations modeling how particles of different sizes gain or lose energy while traveling through intergalactic space, the team found that ultraheavy nuclei lose energy more slowly than protons or lighter nuclei, enabling them to survive cosmic distances while still carrying extreme amounts of energy. This finding provides new clues about the types of cosmic objects powerful enough to launch such particles and reshapes the search for their origins.

🔬 核心发现Key Findings

  • 提出超高能宇宙射线可能是比铁更重的超重原子核，而非传统认为的质子Proposes that the highest-energy cosmic rays may be ultraheavy atomic nuclei heavier than iron, rather than protons as traditionally assumed
  • 计算机模拟证明超重原子核在穿越星系际空间时能量损失更慢，能够携带极端能量到达地球Computer simulations demonstrate that ultraheavy nuclei lose energy more slowly in intergalactic space, enabling them to reach Earth with extreme energies intact
  • 为天照粒子为何从宇宙空洞方向到达提供了合理解释，重新定义了超高能宇宙射线的来源搜索策略Provides a plausible explanation for why the Amaterasu particle arrives from a cosmic void, redefining search strategies for ultrahigh-energy cosmic ray sources
💡 为什么重要：💡 Why it matters: 超高能宇宙射线是现代天体物理学最大的未解之谜之一。这项研究从根本上改变了我们理解这些极端粒子的方式——如果确认它们是超重原子核，将重新定义寻找宇宙中最剧烈天体事件的方向，并可能揭示中子星碰撞或大质量恒星坍缩等极端天体物理过程的新信息。 Ultrahigh-energy cosmic rays represent one of the biggest unsolved mysteries in modern astrophysics. This research fundamentally changes how we understand these extreme particles — if confirmed as ultraheavy nuclei, it would redefine the search for the most violent cosmic events and potentially reveal new information about extreme astrophysical processes like neutron star collisions or massive star collapses.
