Apple与NVIDIA拟将2028年部分产能转向Intel代工，行业格局加速重塑

Apple and NVIDIA are considering shifting part of their chip production to Intel Foundry by 2028. Intel's new CEO Tan is pivoting focus from 18A to 14A process. NVIDIA previously invested $5B in Intel but reportedly paused 18A testing. If realized, this would be Intel's largest external foundry win and reshape US semiconductor manufacturing.
