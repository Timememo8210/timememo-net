🏭 Apple and NVIDIA Considering Partial Production Shift to Intel by 2028

据多家媒体报道，Apple和NVIDIA正在考虑在2028年前将部分芯片生产从TSMC转移到Intel代工。这一战略转变源于TSMC产能持续紧张以及地缘政治风险。Google和NVIDIA也将Intel视为先进AI芯片的备选制造商，因为TSMC难以满足激增的AI需求。对Intel而言，这是其代工业务（Intel Foundry）的重大机遇——如果交易达成，将验证Intel"成为全球第二大代工"的战略目标。Intel同时也表示NVIDIA进入PC芯片领域的竞争是"好事"，认为AI带来的新商机远超竞争威胁。
Apple and NVIDIA are considering shifting some chip production from TSMC to Intel Foundry by 2028, driven by TSMC capacity constraints and geopolitical risk. Google and NVIDIA also see Intel as a backup for advanced AI chips as TSMC struggles to meet surging demand. For Intel Foundry, this would validate its strategy to become the world's second-largest foundry. Intel also called NVIDIA's entry into PC chips "a good thing," viewing AI opportunities as far outweighing competitive threats.

🔧 TSMC CoPoS Breakthrough: Solving Large AI Chip Packaging Bottleneck

TSMC正在推进CoPoS（Chip-on-Panel-on-Substrate）先进封装技术，旨在解决NVIDIA、AMD等客户日益增长的AI芯片尺寸超过传统晶圆限制的问题。随着AI芯片面积不断增大，传统封装方式已接近物理极限。CoPoS允许在更大面板上进行芯片集成，显著提高良率和产能。TSMC CEO此前警告全球芯片产能可能无法满足AI需求，2026年增长预期超过30%。该技术对维持NVIDIA Blackwell/Rubin系列和AMD MI400系列的产能至关重要。
TSMC is advancing CoPoS (Chip-on-Panel-on-Substrate) packaging technology to solve the challenge of AI chips from NVIDIA and AMD outgrowing traditional wafer limits. As AI chip areas grow, conventional packaging nears physical limits. CoPoS enables chip integration on larger panels, improving yield and capacity. TSMC's CEO warned global chip capacity may not meet AI demand; 2026 growth outlook exceeds 30%. The technology is critical for sustaining NVIDIA Blackwell/Rubin and AMD MI400 series production.
