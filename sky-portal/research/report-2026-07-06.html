🏭 Intel Crescent Island AI GPU：480GB VRAM挑战NVIDIA成本优势

Intel在Computex 2026上公布的Crescent Island AI数据中心GPU细节持续引发关注。该芯片定位推理优化，配备高达480GB LPDDR5X显存，计划2026下半年出货。与NVIDIA H100/B200采用HBM不同，Crescent Island使用LPDDR5X虽然带宽较低，但大幅降低成本——这对于推理负载（不需要顶级带宽但需要大显存）具有吸引力。Google已向Intel Foundry下单300万颗定制TPU（2028年交付），NVIDIA也在评估Intel的18A工艺和EMIB先进封装技术。TSMC CoWoS产能瓶颈（NVIDIA占60%、Broadcom+AMD占26%）正迫使主要芯片设计商寻找替代方案。

Intel's Crescent Island AI data center GPU continues to draw attention after Computex 2026. The chip targets inference optimization with up to 480GB LPDDR5X VRAM, shipping H2 2026. Unlike NVIDIA's HBM approach, LPDDR5X trades bandwidth for cost — attractive for inference workloads needing large memory without top bandwidth. Google ordered 3M custom TPUs from Intel Foundry (2028 delivery), NVIDIA is evaluating Intel's 18A process and EMIB packaging. TSMC's CoWoS bottleneck (NVIDIA 60%, Broadcom+AMD 26%) is forcing diversification.

📊 TSMC产能瓶颈成行业转折点：Intel Foundry迎来历史机遇

TSMC在先进封装（CoWoS）领域的主导地位正面临前所未有的产能压力。2026年AI芯片需求暴涨导致CoWoS供不应求，NVIDIA独占约60% CoWoS产能，Broadcom和AMD合计再占26%，留给其他厂商的空间极为有限。Intel的EMIB封装技术成为现实替代方案，良率据报道已达约90%，获得Google和Meta的采用。Intel股价Q2暴涨216%（新增$4800亿市值），反映市场对其代工业务转型的重新定价。同时，Apple、AMD、NVIDIA、Google、Broadcom都在评估利用Intel Foundry制造芯片的可能性——这可能是半导体行业十年来最大的供应链格局重塑。

TSMC's CoWoS packaging dominance faces unprecedented capacity pressure. AI chip demand surge in 2026 has created supply shortage — NVIDIA alone absorbs ~60% of CoWoS capacity, Broadcom and AMD take another 26%. Intel's EMIB packaging is the real alternative, with ~90% yield, adopted by Google and Meta. Intel stock surged 216% in Q2 (adding $480B market cap), reflecting market revaluation of its foundry transformation. Apple, AMD, NVIDIA, Google, and Broadcom are all evaluating Intel Foundry — potentially the biggest semiconductor supply chain reshuffle in a decade.

🏛️ 白宫AI模型自愿发布标准谈判进入冲刺阶段

Financial Times确认，白宫正与OpenAI、Google、Anthropic进行深入谈判，拟制定前沿AI模型发布的自愿标准框架。核心议题包括：(1) 审查时间线——政府可扣留模型多长时间；(2) 门槛定义——何种能力水平触发审查。这将正式取代当前的事实上"非自愿许可制度"——此前Anthropic的Fable 5被下线20天，OpenAI的GPT-5.6在公开发布前被拦截。网络安全基准分数是关键阈值：GPT-5.6 Sol在Capture the Flag评估中得分96.7%（触发"高风险"分类），而Gemini 3.5 Pro仅70.7%（低于门槛18分以上，未受限制）。对即将到来的OpenAI和Anthropic IPO而言，正式框架将大幅降低监管不确定性风险。

FT confirms the White House is in advanced negotiations with OpenAI, Google, and Anthropic on voluntary standards for frontier AI model releases. Core issues: (1) review timelines — how long government can hold models; (2) threshold definitions — what capability triggers review. This would replace the de facto involuntary licensing regime where Anthropic's Fable 5 was pulled for 20 days and OpenAI's GPT-5.6 was blocked pre-release. Cybersecurity benchmark scores are the key threshold: GPT-5.6 Sol scored 96.7% on Capture the Flag (triggering "high risk"), while Gemini 3.5 Pro scored only 70.7% (below threshold, unrestricted). Formal framework would reduce regulatory uncertainty for upcoming OpenAI/Anthropic IPOs.

来源：Reuters · Yahoo Finance / FT · CNBC
