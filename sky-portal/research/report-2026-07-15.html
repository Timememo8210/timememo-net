OpenAI launched GPT-Live on July 8, a new voice model making AI conversations feel like talking to a real person. Built on a full-duplex architecture, it can listen and speak simultaneously, say "mhmm" or "yeah" to show attention, and handle quick back-and-forth. It delegates to GPT-5.5 for complex tasks while maintaining conversation flow.
