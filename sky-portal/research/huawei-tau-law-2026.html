01

最短答案The Short Answer

把“芯片做小”换成“把信号和数据等待时间做短”。Shift the optimization target from making transistors smaller to making signals and data wait less.

韬（τ）定律是华为在 2026 年 5 月 25 日 ISCAS 2026 上提出的半导体演进原则：用“时间缩微”替代只依赖“几何缩微”。这里的 τ 指跨层特征时间常数，包含晶体管开关、互连 RC、芯片内访存、系统级通信与同步。

更准确地说，它是华为版的后摩尔工程框架：器件层降 RC，电路层用 LogicFolding 缩短关键路径，芯片层做软硬芯协同，系统层用灵衢总线/UnifiedBus 降低超节点通信时延。

需要克制：它目前还不是像摩尔定律那样经过几十年产业验证的经验定律，也不是“华为已经造出 1.4nm 芯片”。华为提出的是 2031 年达到 1.4nm 制程等效晶体管密度的目标。

The Tau (τ) Scaling Law is Huawei's semiconductor evolution principle introduced at ISCAS 2026 on May 25, 2026. It proposes time scaling instead of relying only on geometric scaling. Here, τ means a layered characteristic time constant: transistor switching, interconnect RC delay, chip-level memory access, and system-level communication/synchronization.

More precisely, it is Huawei's post-Moore engineering framework: reduce RC at the device layer, shorten critical paths with LogicFolding at the circuit layer, co-design software/architecture/silicon at the chip layer, and use UnifiedBus at the system layer to cut SuperPoD communication latency.

The cautious reading matters: it is not yet an industry-proven empirical law like Moore's Law, and it does not mean Huawei has already made a 1.4 nm chip. Huawei is targeting transistor-density equivalence to a 1.4 nm-class process by 2031.

我的判断：韬定律最有价值的地方，不是“命名一个新定律”，而是把先进封装、3D 集成、低时延互连和系统软件协同组织成同一条路线。 My read: the value is not the label “law”; it is the organization of advanced packaging, 3D integration, low-latency interconnects, and system software into one roadmap.

02

技术内核：τ 缩的是什么Technology Core: What τ Actually Scales

τ 不是单一元件参数，而是跨层“时间成本”的统一度量。τ is not one component parameter. It is a unified measure of time cost across the computing stack.

从空间到时间From Space to Time

摩尔路径的核心抓手是把晶体管做小。韬定律的说法是：过去缩尺寸的最终收益其实是缩短时间，包括门延迟、互连延迟、访存延迟和系统通信延迟。既然几何缩微的经济性下降，就直接把“时间”设为主目标。

Moore-style progress mainly shrank transistors. Huawei's argument is that the useful outcome of shrinking was time reduction: gate delay, interconnect delay, memory-access delay, and system-communication delay. If geometric scaling is losing economic leverage, optimize time directly.

一个简单公式A Simple Rule

τn+1 = τn / α

论文把 α 设为应用相关，而非固定常数：手机约 1.3x/年，安全关键自动系统约 1.5x/年，AI 工作负载最高可到 10x/年。这个数字需要被看作路线图假设，而不是行业已验证常数。

The paper treats α as workload-specific, not universal: about 1.3x/year for mobile, 1.5x/year for safety-critical autonomous systems, and up to 10x/year for AI workloads. These are roadmap assumptions, not validated industry constants.

Device
器件
优化晶体管与互连的电阻、寄生电容和本征开关延迟。先进器件、局部互连、材料与背面供电都属于这一层的工具箱。Reduce transistor and interconnect resistance, parasitic capacitance, and intrinsic switching delay. Advanced devices, local interconnects, materials, and backside power are part of this toolbox.
Circuit
电路
用 LogicFolding 等方法缩短关键路径线长，降低 RC 传播延迟、时钟偏斜和长线负载。Use methods like LogicFolding to shorten critical-path wiring and reduce RC propagation delay, clock skew, and long-wire loading.
Chip
芯片
围绕真实 workload 细粒度控制指令流、数据流、流水线、缓存和片上互连，提高有效并行度。Control instruction flow, data flow, pipelines, caches, and on-chip fabrics around real workloads to raise effective parallelism.
System
系统
通过灵衢总线/UnifiedBus、统一内存编址、近封装光 I/O 和超节点拓扑降低跨卡、跨机柜通信与同步时间。Use UnifiedBus, unified memory addressing, near-packaged optical I/O, and SuperPoD topology to reduce cross-card and cross-rack communication/synchronization time.
03

LogicFolding：不是普通“堆芯片”LogicFolding: Not Just Stacking Chips

它的关键是把关键路径逻辑按电路时序需求分布到垂直有源层。The key is timing-driven distribution of critical-path logic across vertical active tiers.

What it is

细粒度 3D 逻辑重排Fine-Grained 3D Logic Reordering

传统 3D 堆叠常是 block-level：HBM、cache-on-logic、IO die。LogicFolding 更强调 cell/critical-path 级重排，把跨层边界当作额外设计空间。

Conventional 3D stacking is often block-level: HBM, cache-on-logic, or I/O dies. LogicFolding is more cell/critical-path oriented, treating tier boundaries as part of the design fabric.

Why it helps

线短了，τ 才降Shorter Wires, Lower τ

关键路径 gate 不再被限制在单一平面内，长水平线可以变成短水平线加垂直互连，从而降低寄生 RC、收紧时钟偏斜。

Critical-path gates are no longer confined to one plane. Long horizontal paths can become short horizontal paths plus vertical bonds, reducing parasitic RC and clock skew.

Why it is hard

EDA、热和良率会变成主战场EDA, Heat, and Yield Become Central

跨有源层布局、时序收敛、热堆叠、TSV keep-out zone、键合对准、测试修复和良率叠乘，都可能吞掉部分收益。

Cross-tier placement, timing closure, thermal stacking, TSV keep-out zones, bonding alignment, test/repair, and compounded yield can consume part of the gains.

产品影响：手机、AI 集群、鲲鹏、车与鸿蒙Product Impact: Phones, AI Clusters, Kunpeng, Auto, HarmonyOS

发布会讲的是“定律”，产品上看的是 PPA、token 成本、生态迁移和真实稳定性。The announcement speaks in “law” language; product reality is PPA, token cost, migration friction, and operational stability.

Kirin

旗舰手机 SoCFlagship Smartphone SoC

手机不能靠成百上千芯片并行掩盖单芯片短板，所以 LogicFolding 若能在麒麟 2026 上稳定量产，最直接影响是频率、功耗、缓存、端侧 AI 和散热。

A phone cannot hide weak single-chip performance behind hundreds of accelerators. If LogicFolding ships reliably in Kirin 2026, the direct impact is frequency, power, cache behavior, on-device AI, and thermals.

Ascend

AI 推理集群AI Inference Clusters

对 AI 推理，瓶颈常在数据搬运、KV cache、专家路由和同步。灵衢总线、Hi-ONE 与资源池化的价值，是降低端到端 token 延迟和每 token 成本。

For AI inference, bottlenecks often sit in data movement, KV cache, expert routing, and synchronization. UnifiedBus, Hi-ONE, and resource pooling are valuable if they lower end-to-end token latency and cost per token.

Kunpeng

通用计算超节点General-Compute Supernodes

鲲鹏的重点可能从“国产 ARM CPU”变成“通用计算超节点控制面与内存池核心”，服务数据库、虚拟化、大数据和 AI host 场景。

Kunpeng may shift from being “a domestic Arm CPU” to being a control-plane and memory-pool anchor for general-compute supernodes: databases, virtualization, big data, and AI hosts.

Auto

汽车与工业Auto and Industrial

智能驾驶和工业控制更看重确定性低时延、可靠性和长生命周期。τ 叙事与这类场景天然匹配，但公开资料还不能确认具体车规芯片采用情况。

Autonomous driving and industrial control care about deterministic low latency, reliability, and long lifecycles. The τ narrative fits, but public evidence does not yet confirm specific automotive-chip adoption.

HarmonyOS

软硬芯协同Software-Hardware-Silicon Co-Design

鸿蒙、方舟引擎、编译器、runtime 和端云协同决定底层 τ 收益能否转化为用户感知。硬件缩短时间，软件负责别浪费时间。

HarmonyOS, Ark Engine, compilers, runtimes, and edge-cloud coordination decide whether low-level τ gains become user-perceived gains. Hardware saves time; software must avoid wasting it.

Reality check

最重要的验证点The Critical Test

2026 秋季麒麟上市后的第三方拆解、真实功耗曲线、面积成本、良率迹象、AI 推理延迟和手机温控，会比任何发布会 PPT 更有说服力。

The strongest evidence will be third-party teardown, real power curves, area/cost clues, yield signals, inference latency, and phone thermals after the Fall 2026 Kirin launch.

Core logic: constrained process nodes do not mean performance stops. But the alternative path shifts complexity into packaging, EDA, heat, yield, interconnects, and software ecosystems. The real test is cost, stability, and customer deployment.
