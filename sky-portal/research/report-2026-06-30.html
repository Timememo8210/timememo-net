🏭 NVIDIA 2028年部分芯片生产可能转向Intel代工

🏭 NVIDIA May Shift Some 2028 Chip Production to Intel Foundry

据多家媒体报道，NVIDIA正认真考虑在2028年前将部分芯片生产从TSMC转移至Intel代工。Apple也在做类似评估。这一战略转变源于TSMC先进封装(CoWoS)产能持续紧张——NVIDIA预计将占2026年全球CoWoS需求的60%，加上Broadcom和AMD的26%，TSMC几乎无法满足所有客户需求。Intel Foundry如果获得NVIDIA的订单，将极大验证其"成为全球第二大代工"的战略。与此同时，TSMC正在推进CoPoS先进封装技术以突破制造瓶颈。来源：Bits&Chips

Multiple reports indicate NVIDIA is seriously considering shifting some chip production from TSMC to Intel Foundry by 2028. Apple is also evaluating a similar move. The strategic shift stems from TSMC's strained CoWoS advanced packaging capacity — NVIDIA alone is expected to account for 60% of global CoWoS demand in 2026, with Broadcom and AMD adding another 26%. If Intel secures NVIDIA orders, it would significantly validate its strategy to become the world's second-largest foundry. Meanwhile, TSMC is advancing CoPoS packaging technology to break through manufacturing bottlenecks. Source: Bits&Chips
