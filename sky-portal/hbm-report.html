⚙️ Base Die 制程节点 — 核心技术焦点Base Die Process Nodes — Key Technical Focus

🔴 为什么Base Die如此重要？ Base Die是HBM堆栈最底层的逻辑芯片，负责PHY接口、ECC纠错、电源管理、信号调理等功能。它是DRAM层与外部芯片（GPU/CPU）之间的"大脑"。Base Die的制程越先进，功耗越低、信号完整性越好、能支持的数据速率越高。 🔴 Why is the Base Die so critical? The Base Die is the bottom logic chip in the HBM stack, responsible for PHY interface, ECC, power management, and signal conditioning. It's the "brain" between DRAM layers and the external chip (GPU/CPU). More advanced process = lower power, better signal integrity, and higher data rates.

🔗 Hybrid Bonding 混合键合深度解析Hybrid Bonding Deep Dive

什么是Hybrid Bonding？What is Hybrid Bonding?

Hybrid Bonding（混合键合）是一种铜-铜直接键合技术，不使用传统的焊锡凸点（solder bumps）。芯片之间通过金属（铜）和绝缘体（氧化硅）的同时键合实现连接，因此称为"混合"。 Hybrid Bonding is a copper-to-copper direct bonding technology that eliminates traditional solder bumps. Chips connect through simultaneous bonding of metal (copper) and insulator (silicon oxide) — hence "hybrid."

⚠️ 关键技术挑战Key Technical Challenges

1. 良率 (Yield)Yield

每增加4层DRAM，整个系统的制造难度显著提升：对准精度、焊点间距、Z方向高度控制、翘曲、底部填充（MUF）可靠性。SK Hynix报告良率约80%，Samsung 12层堆叠认证曾遇到困难。16-Hi堆叠的良率预计要到2026年Q3才能稳定。 Every 4-layer increase significantly escalates manufacturing difficulty: placement accuracy, solder joint spacing, Z-height control, warpage, and underfill (MUF) reliability. SK Hynix reports ~80% yield; Samsung struggled with 12-layer qualification. 16-Hi yields expected to stabilize by Q3 2026.

2. 散热 (Thermal)Thermal Management

散热是16-Hi及更高堆叠的首要可靠性挑战。HBM4的16-Hi已需要液冷方案，而HBM5的20-Hi堆叠功耗将飙升至~100W/stack——是HBM3E的4倍。这意味着HBM5时代（2029年）将强制要求浸没式液冷（immersion cooling）。数据中心基础设施需要提前2-3年规划液冷改造。 Thermal management is the primary reliability challenge for 16-Hi+ stacks. HBM4's 16-Hi already requires liquid cooling, and HBM5's 20-Hi stacks will consume ~100W/stack — 4× HBM3E. This means the HBM5 era (2029) will mandate immersion cooling. Data center infrastructure must plan liquid cooling upgrades 2-3 years ahead.

3. 成本 (Cost)Cost Structure

HBM贵在哪？TSV工艺、键合工艺、良率损失、以及测试成本。HBM每GB的晶圆消耗量是DDR5的约3倍。新一代bumpless TSV（wafer-on-wafer bonding）和one-step TSV工艺有望将成本降低50%以上。 What makes HBM expensive? TSV processing, bonding, yield loss, and testing. HBM consumes ~3× the wafer capacity per GB compared to DDR5. New bumpless TSV and one-step TSV approaches promise >50% cost reduction.

4. 供应瓶颈 (Supply Bottleneck)Supply Bottleneck

三个瓶颈叠加：① HBM DRAM产能不足 ② TSMC先进封装（CoWoS）产能不足 ③ 先进节点（3nm/2nm）产能不足。NVIDIA已预订TSMC 2026年60-65%的CoWoS产能。 Three bottlenecks stacking: ① HBM DRAM capacity ② TSMC advanced packaging (CoWoS) capacity ③ Advanced node (3nm/2nm) capacity. NVIDIA has pre-booked 60-65% of TSMC's 2026 CoWoS output.
