01
Executive draft

AI amplifies people. It does not replace them.

Start with people, not tools. AI accelerates the work we already do and unlocks work we never could.

AI moves information. Only people build trust.

Customers trust us not because one run looks clean. They trust us because when the data goes wrong, we own the fix. AI processes information; people deliver judgment, relationships, and accountability.

Accelerate what we already doSearch, summarize, draft reports, prep meetings, and consolidate status.
Enable what one person never couldA single engineer now commands research, coding, editing, and data-analysis capabilities on demand.
Compress the path from question to actionNear term: ask sharper questions, get sourced answers, and act on them in the same workflow.
02
Scenario 1

Meeting scheduling: from manual coordination to one-click confirmation.

A low-risk, high-resonance starting point. Everyone feels this pain.

Now

Manual juggling

Open Outlook, cross-check calendars, hunt for a room, and iterate through options one by one.

Near term

AI proposes, you confirm

State the request once. Copilot surfaces the best time-and-room options; you approve with one click.

Mid / long term

Agents handle end to end

Agents negotiate calendars and book resources autonomously. You simply receive: "Done."

The shift: "I arrange it" → "I approve it" → "I'm notified."

03
Scenario 2

Data questions in meetings: answer now, decide now.

The highest-impact engineering pain point -- and the strongest case for AI adoption.

Now

Defer and follow up

If the answer was pre-prepared, we respond. If not, we take an action item and circle back next meeting.

Near term

Meeting Prep Agent

Before the meeting, AI anticipates the top questions. It drafts answers where data exists and flags the right owner where it does not.

Mid / long term

Live retrieval + action plan

Mid-meeting, AI delivers sourced answers in real time and drafts root-cause hypotheses with experiment options.

The shift: collapse "flag → investigate → reconvene → plan" into "answer → assess → decide" -- all in one sitting.

04
Scenario 3

Weekly updates: stop chasing information -- let it flow to you.

Ground the story in something already underway. This makes the vision concrete, not aspirational.

Now

Manual assembly

Sit through meetings, draft minutes, update spreadsheets, then relay the latest status by hand.

Near term

Copilot-assisted roll-up

Copilot ingests meeting recaps, preserves the master table, appends a weekly-delta column, and highlights what changed.

Mid / long term

Auto-refreshing dashboards

After every meeting, agents update customer, project, and portfolio views. People curate only the views they own.

The shift: people stop chasing information -- information finds the people who need it.

05
Adoption

Start now. Equip the team to go further.

AI creates zero value on the shelf. Adoption requires asking questions, asking faster, asking with evidence -- and giving the team the access and examples to do so.

Three habits to build today

1
Ask first, filter laterEvery unasked question is a missed answer. Lower the bar to ask -- AI makes the cost of inquiry near zero.
2
Demand sources, not guessesRequire citations. Leave gaps where no source exists; surface conflicting evidence for human judgment.
3
Speak, don't typeDon't let typing speed throttle your thinking. AI handles rough input; speed of inquiry matters more than polish.

What we need from leadership

A
M365 Copilot licenses for every engineerBefore building advanced agents, give everyone the basics: ask, search, and summarize.
B
One or two wins shared weeklyBundle real use cases into the weekly update so the team sees tangible productivity gains.
C
Power tools and training for key contributorsCopilot Studio, higher token/model tiers, AI conferences, and hands-on workshops.
D
Cross-functional AI working groupLaunch in automation, QA, and engineering -- domains where alignment already exists.

The narrative arc

Near term: practical, time-saving, evidence-based. Mid term: elevate existing knowledge systems and clarify ownership. Long term: a single source of truth that drives certainty -- not "opening all data to AI."
