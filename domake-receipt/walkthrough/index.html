02 / REVIEW & RECOVERY

The confirmation loop

These are the current workflow rules. The next screenshot round must verify each relevant window for the new files, including failure recovery. A finished OCR job is not a saved record.

  1. 01

    Open and preview

    Select a PDF, JPG, PNG or WebP file. The prototype opens it locally. PDFs use manual entry; English images can use experimental OCR.

  2. 02

    Review what was recovered

    Useful or partial image results open a summary. Compare company, actual worker, work, service date, address and total with the original. Unknown facts stay blank.

  3. 03

    Correct the full form

    Choose Review / edit details. Correct mistakes and assign the property. A property and work summary are required to confirm; optional missing facts may remain unknown.

  4. 04

    Confirm the latest details

    Review the final summary. The save button remains disabled until the confirmation checkbox is ticked. Return to editing if anything is wrong.

  5. 05

    Save and check

    Only a successful local write creates a confirmed record. The history lets you reopen, correct or export JSON. The record is stored in this browser, without cloud or partner synchronisation.

When the input is unsuitable

Unreadable or no useful text

Explain that no usable receipt details were found. Offer another file, retry or manual entry. A no-text result does not prove that the image is blurred.

Readable, unrelated document

A restaurant receipt may be readable but outside home-service scope. Explain the relevance issue; let the uploader choose a relevant file or manually clarify the property connection.

Some information is missing

Keep usable facts and show missing fields. Do not fill a date with today, infer the currency from the address, or substitute a customer for the worker.

The result is uncertain

Keep the original available and request verification. Do not present a confident success or add an empty record automatically.

03 / EVIDENCE

Measured results, with their limits

Three new images were processed through the actual local OCR engine and field parser in Node. This tests recognition and extraction only. It does not test browser upload, dialogs, storage, phone capture or Gemini.

Engine test — not browser screenshots

Clear UK PNG

Recovered Cedar Home Services, James Turner, service date 2026-09-01, issue date 2026-09-02 and GBP 240. Work summary: Replace kitchen mixer tap. Home-system category remains unknown.

Engine test — not browser screenshots

UK simulated phone photo

Only document type and receipt number were retained. Company, worker, dates, address, work and amount remain blank. OCR misread dates and the postcode; the strict parser rejected them. This is not a successful receipt extraction.

Engine test — not browser screenshots

Partly blurred AED photo

The first parser result included OCR symbols in the work summary. A fix now rejects that noisy field entirely, while retaining the raw OCR text for inspection. Company, worker, dates and amount remain blank; a clearer file or manual entry is needed.

Single-run engine times were about 0.48, 0.79 and 0.52 seconds on this Mac, including worker initialisation. They exclude browser loading and user interaction and are not customer latency targets. OCR confidence is not field accuracy.
