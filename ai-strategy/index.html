00执行摘要Executive Summary

先问对问题，让答案可追溯，再让 AI 替你执行。

Ask sharper questions. Trace every answer. Then let AI execute.

转型第一步不是买工具，是改习惯：敢问、会问、答案必须溯源。中期让 agent 处理低风险的数字事务；长期接入关键数据、建立单一可信源（SSOT），进入受控的工程问题解决。窗口已经打开，胜负取决于采用率、数据质量和信任。

Transformation starts as a habit, not a purchase: ask boldly, ask well, and demand sourced answers. Mid-term, let agents handle low-risk digital tasks. Long-term, connect critical data, build a single source of truth, and move into governed engineering problem-solving. The window is open — speed depends on adoption, data quality, and trust.

01为什么做 AIWhy AI

更快，也更强。

Faster — and stronger.

AI 做两件事：把已有工作提速，把原本做不到的事变得可做。结论很简单——AI 不是替代品，是放大器。

AI delivers two gains: it compresses work you already do, and unlocks work that was previously out of reach. The frame is simple — not a replacement, a multiplier.

提速 · Acceleration
Acceleration

已有工作提速

Compress existing work

搜索、总结、写代码、准备报告、整理会议、分析资料——原本几小时甚至几天的数字工作，压缩到分钟。

Search, summarize, draft code, prep reviews, organize meetings, analyze data — hours or days of digital work collapsed into minutes.

扩展 · Expansion
Expansion

打开新的可能

Unlock new reach

一个人同时获得研究、编程、编辑、数据分析、项目管理的部分能力，跨越原有的专业边界。

One person borrows partial capability from a researcher, programmer, editor, analyst, and coordinator at once — crossing old skill boundaries.

互联网时代，工具帮我们找信息；AI 时代，工具帮我们形成答案、采取行动，直到解决问题。

In the internet era, tools helped us find information. In the AI era, tools help us form answers and take action — through to solving the problem.

Information → Answers → Action → Solving.

In the digital world, AI reliably answers questions and is beginning to act across code, documents, email, and data. The boundary is equally clear: end-to-end problem-solving isn't reliable at scale, and the physical world demands validation, audit, and human ownership.

03位置与数据基础Position & Data Foundation

我们还在早期；数据没连起来，是真正的瓶颈。

Still early — and disconnected data is the real bottleneck.

M365 已经提供合规入口：邮件、会议、文档、recap、协同数据，足以启动早期 agent。短板也明确：使用率不深、模型与工具链受限、工厂关键数据尚未安全接入。瓶颈不是模型能力，是数据散落、缺少单一可信源。

M365 already provides a compliant entry point — email, meetings, documents, recaps, collaboration data — enough for early agents. The gaps are equally clear: shallow adoption, limited models and toolchains, and no safe access to factory-level data. The bottleneck isn't model capability; it's fragmented data and the absence of a single source of truth.

数据不是没有，是没连起来

The data exists — it just isn't connected

工程数据、spec、process flow、实验记录、历史问题都在，但散落在不同系统。过去这是数字化问题，现在是 AI 能不能工作的前提。先让关键数据可读、可搜索，再打通关系，最后让 AI 成为统一入口。

Engineering data, specs, process flows, experiment records, and historical issues all exist — scattered across systems. Once a digitization problem, now a prerequisite for useful AI. First make key data readable and searchable, then connect the relationships, then let AI become the unified interface.

关键数字必须唯一

Critical numbers must be singular

AI 可以广读信息，但 process flow、工艺 spec、ET spec 不能模糊——差 0.1 nm 就可能改变判断。同一时刻一个关键 spec 只有一个权威来源，AI 必须引用它，不能在冲突系统间平均或猜测。SSOT 不只是 IT 项目，是 AI 进入严肃工程判断的前提。

AI can read broadly, but process flows and process/ET specs cannot be fuzzy — a 0.1 nm delta can change the call. At any moment a critical spec has exactly one authoritative source; AI must cite it, never average across conflicting systems or guess. SSOT isn't just an IT project — it's the precondition for serious engineering judgment with AI.

可访问 · 可连接
Accessible · Connected

AI 能读到核心数据，而非只有外围文档；数据源之间有关系、上下文和统一 ID。

AI reaches core data, not just peripheral docs; sources share relationships, context, and unified IDs.

唯一正确 · 可追溯
Authoritative · Traceable

关键 spec 和 flow 有唯一权威源；每个答案都能回溯到来源、版本和时间点。

Critical specs and flows resolve to one source; every answer traces back to source, version, and timestamp.

04路线图Roadmap

6 个月打基础，3 年解问题，5 年做研发。

Six months for answers. Three years for problems. Five for R&D.

不从"全自动 AI 工厂"开始。先让团队会问、会用、会验证；再做可审计的数字行动；最后接入关键数据与验证机制，解决受控的工程问题。验收看任务覆盖和答案质量，不看部署了几个工具。

Don't start with a fully autonomous AI factory. First build habits — asking, using, validating. Then auditable digital actions. Then connect deeper data and validation loops. Measure task coverage and answer quality, not how many tools got deployed.

0–6 个月 · 把回答做好mo · Nail the answers
建团队级 prompt 模板、问题库、案例库和 Copilot 使用流程。
Build team prompt templates, a question library, case examples, and Copilot workflows.
验收：周活跃用户、有效问题数、节省时间、答案出处可信度。
Validate on weekly active users, useful questions, time saved, and source credibility.
6–12 个月 · 从答案走向行动mo · Answers to action
AI 整理行动项、生成 follow-up、准备 review 包、更新知识库、跑固定分析。
AI drafts action items and follow-ups, preps review packages, updates knowledge bases, runs fixed analyses.
验收：每个 action 有触发条件、数据来源、人工确认和日志。先做低风险数字任务，不碰物理控制。
Validate on auditable loops: every action has triggers, sources, confirmation, logs. Low-risk digital tasks only.
1–3 年 · 解决重复工程问题yr · Recurring problems
围绕良率异常、defect pattern、process drift、review preparation，把数据、历史案例、推理与验证串起来。
Around yield excursions, defect patterns, process drift, and review prep — link data, history, reasoning, validation.
验收：AI 提候选根因、推荐检查顺序、调用分析工具，靠历史对比或小规模验证降低不确定性。
Validate the shift from suggestion to verification: candidate causes, check order, tool calls, validated against history.
3–5 年 · 参与研发与优化yr · R&D & optimization
受控范围内参与 DOE 设计、工艺窗口分析、参数敏感性、知识沉淀与 R&D-to-HVM 转移。
Within bounds: DOE design, process-window analysis, parameter sensitivity, knowledge capture, R&D-to-HVM transfer.
验收：成功案例系统记录，失败进反例库，人和 AI 都从反馈中改进。
Validate on shared learning: wins recorded, failures into a counter-example library, both human and AI improving.
05数字分身Digital Agents

不是一个万能分身，是一组分工明确的 agent。

Not one universal twin — a portfolio of agents.

"数字分身"是一套分工系统：查知识、做执行、看客户/产品、分析良率/ET。起点要小，边界要硬——守住 M365 已授权数据，不碰生产控制，不替代人的判断。先做能用的，再接数据，最后进入问题解决。每个知识库必须有部门或人对准确性负责。

A "digital double" is a division of labor: knowledge check, execution, product/customer context, yield/ET analysis. Start small, keep boundaries hard — stay within M365-authorized data, don't touch production control, don't replace human judgment. Ship the usable one first, connect data next, tackle problem-solving last. Every knowledge base needs a named owner accountable for accuracy.

Trust — sourced, traceable, free to say "I don't know." Boundary — authorized data only. Ownership — every base has an owner. KPI — task coverage and sourced-answer speed, not prompt count.

06工作方式与讲法Workstyle & Narrative

别喊"组织变革"，先让会议短下来。

Skip the org-change memo. Shorten the meeting first.

AI 会改变对齐方式、会议形态和知识流动。但在成果出现之前，别把它包装成"组织架构调整"——那只会触发恐惧。正确的讲法是：降低同步成本，让人把时间花在判断上。用场景说话，不用概念。

AI will reshape alignment, meetings, and how knowledge flows. But before the wins are visible, don't frame it as org redesign — that only triggers fear. Frame it as lower sync cost and more time for judgment. Lead with scenarios, not concepts.

人的价值：AI 传递信息，建立不了信任。 客户愿意合作，不只因为这次数据好，而是相信下次数据不好时你也会全力以赴。AI 处理信息，人承担判断、关系与责任。
The human moat: AI moves information; it can't build trust. Customers stay not because this run looks good, but because they trust you'll fight to make it right when it doesn't. AI handles information; people carry judgment, relationships, and accountability.

你的直觉成立：模型更强、价格更低 → 今天的账是"下限"。 今天 7 分钟回本，几年后可能 3–4 分钟；今天做不到的，明年同价就能做。所以不该"等降价再上"——早期真正的成本是学习曲线和采用习惯，不是 license。投入买的是组织的 AI 熟练度（会升值），不是软件（会贬值）。但正式汇报一律用今天价格做基线，不写降价预测。
Your instinct holds: stronger models, lower prices → today's numbers are a floor. Seven-minute break-even today may be three or four in a few years; what's impossible now becomes possible at the same price next year. So don't "wait for prices to drop" — the real early cost is the learning curve and adoption habit, not the license. The investment buys organizational AI fluency (appreciates), not software (depreciates). But in formal reporting, always baseline on today's price; don't forecast discounts.
