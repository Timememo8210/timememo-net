gm（跨导）gm (transconductance)

基础指标Core metrics

栅压变一点，漏电流跟着变多少：ΔID/ΔVGS，单位 S。How much drain current changes per small change in gate voltage: ΔID/ΔVGS, in siemens.

实际含义What it is
gm = ∂ID/∂VGS，在固定 VDS 的偏置点上取。它是晶体管把输入电压信号转换成输出电流信号的“放大倍率”。gm = ∂ID/∂VGS at a fixed VDS bias point. It is the factor by which the transistor converts an input voltage signal into an output current signal.
表征什么What it tells you
器件对输入信号的响应强度，决定放大器的增益、带宽和输入参考噪声。How strongly the device responds to an input signal; it sets amplifier gain, bandwidth and input-referred noise.
数值越大 / 越小意味着什么Bigger vs. smaller
越大越好：同样电流下 gm 越大，增益越高、速度越快、折算到输入的噪声越小。但 gm 本身随电流上升，所以单看 gm 没有意义，要看 gm/ID。Larger is better: at the same current, higher gm means more gain, more speed and less input-referred noise. But gm rises with current, so gm alone means little; look at gm/ID.
打个比方Analogy
像水龙头的灵敏度：把手转一点点，水流变化多少。灵敏的龙头 gm 大。Like the sensitivity of a faucet: turn the handle a little, how much does the flow change? A sensitive faucet has high gm.
怎么测How it is measured
扫 ID–VGS 曲线后对 VGS 求导；高频下用 S 参数转 Y 参数，取 Re(Y21)。求导会放大噪声，要平滑或用细步长、多次平均。Sweep ID–VGS and differentiate; at high frequency convert S- to Y-parameters and take Re(Y21). Differentiation amplifies noise, so smooth or use fine steps and averaging.

gm/ID（跨导效率）gm/ID (transconductance efficiency)

基础指标Core metrics

每 1 A 电流能换来多少 gm，单位 S/A 或 1/V。模拟设计的“油耗”指标。How much gm each ampere of current buys, in S/A (1/V). Analog design's fuel-efficiency number.

实际含义What it is
gm 除以漏电流 ID。它只取决于器件处于哪个反型程度，和 W 基本无关，所以能把不同尺寸的器件放在同一张图上比较。gm divided by drain current. It depends mainly on the inversion level, not on W, so devices of different sizes can be compared on one chart.
表征什么What it tells you
偏置点的位置：弱反型、中等反型还是强反型；也就是功耗效率和速度之间的取舍。Where the bias point sits — weak, moderate or strong inversion — and therefore the trade-off between power efficiency and speed.
数值越大 / 越小意味着什么Bigger vs. smaller
越大越省电：约 25–35 S/A 时在弱反型区，效率最高但速度慢；约 5 S/A 以下在强反型区，速度快但费电。常用的折中在 10–15 S/A。理论上限约为 1/(n·kT/q)，室温下大约 30–40 S/A。Higher means more power-efficient: around 25–35 S/A is weak inversion, most efficient but slow; below about 5 S/A is strong inversion, fast but power-hungry. The usual compromise is 10–15 S/A. The theoretical ceiling is about 1/(n·kT/q), roughly 30–40 S/A at room temperature.
打个比方Analogy
像汽车的油耗：同样一升油（电流）跑多远（gm）。低速档省油但慢，高速档快但费油。Like fuel economy: how far (gm) each liter of fuel (current) takes you. Low gear saves fuel but is slow; high gear is fast but thirsty.
怎么测How it is measured
从 ID–VGS 扫描计算 gm 再除以 ID，画 gm/ID 对 ID/(W/L) 的曲线；每个 L 一条。Murmann 的 gm/ID 工具包可直接在开源 PDK 上生成。Compute gm from an ID–VGS sweep and divide by ID; plot gm/ID against ID/(W/L), one curve per L. Murmann's gm/ID kit generates these on open PDKs.
典型数值Typical numbers
【常识】弱反型上限约 1/(n·UT)，n≈1.2–1.5，UT≈26 mV。[Textbook] Weak-inversion ceiling ≈ 1/(n·UT), with n ≈ 1.2–1.5 and UT ≈ 26 mV.

反型系数 IC 与弱/中等/强反型Inversion coefficient (IC) and weak/moderate/strong inversion

基础指标Core metrics

把偏置点归一化的刻度：IC<0.1 弱反型，0.1–10 中等反型，>10 强反型。A normalized scale for the bias point: IC < 0.1 weak, 0.1–10 moderate, > 10 strong inversion.

实际含义What it is
IC = ID / (I_spec·W/L)，I_spec 是工艺相关的特征电流（EKV 模型）。它衡量沟道里反型电荷有多“满”。IC = ID / (I_spec·W/L), where I_spec is a process-dependent specific current (EKV model). It measures how ‘full’ the channel inversion charge is.
表征什么What it tells you
器件工作在哪种导电机制：弱反型以扩散电流为主（指数关系），强反型以漂移电流为主（平方律或速度饱和）。Which conduction regime the device is in: diffusion-dominated in weak inversion (exponential), drift-dominated in strong inversion (square law or velocity saturation).
数值越大 / 越小意味着什么Bigger vs. smaller
IC 越小，gm/ID 越高但 fT 越低、器件越大；IC 越大则相反。最佳折中通常在中等反型。Lower IC gives higher gm/ID but lower fT and bigger devices; higher IC does the opposite. The best compromise is usually moderate inversion.
打个比方Analogy
像水管的开度：开一条缝（弱反型）水流细但每份压力都用得很省；全开（强反型）流量大但浪费也大。Like how far a valve is open: a crack (weak inversion) gives a thin but efficient flow; wide open (strong inversion) gives big flow with more waste.
怎么测How it is measured
从 gm/ID–ID 曲线拟合出 I_spec 和斜率因子 n，再换算 IC。Fit I_spec and slope factor n from the gm/ID–ID curve, then convert to IC.

fT（特征频率）fT (transition frequency)

基础指标Core metrics

电流增益降到 1 时的频率，≈ gm/(2π·Cgg)；衡量器件本征速度。The frequency at which current gain falls to 1, ≈ gm/(2π·Cgg); the device's intrinsic speed.

实际含义What it is
短路电流增益 |h21| 等于 1 时的频率。近似为 gm 除以 2π 倍的总栅电容 Cgg。The frequency where short-circuit current gain |h21| reaches 1. Approximately gm divided by 2π times total gate capacitance Cgg.
表征什么What it tells you
器件“推得动”自己栅电容的能力，即本征带宽。How well the device can drive its own gate capacitance — its intrinsic bandwidth.
数值越大 / 越小意味着什么Bigger vs. smaller
越大越快。但 fT 随 gm/ID 下降而上升，所以要说“在某个 gm/ID 下的 fT”才有可比性。电路实际可用带宽通常只有 fT 的十分之一左右。Higher is faster. fT rises as gm/ID falls, so compare ‘fT at a given gm/ID’. Usable circuit bandwidth is typically about a tenth of fT.
打个比方Analogy
像一个人能多快把自己的行李搬上车：力气（gm）大、行李（电容）轻，就快。Like how fast someone can load their own luggage: more strength (gm) and lighter bags (capacitance) mean faster.
怎么测How it is measured
片上 S 参数测量（去嵌入焊盘和走线后）转 h21，外推到 0 dB；或用 DC 的 gm 加 CV 测的 Cgg 估算。On-wafer S-parameters, de-embedded, converted to h21 and extrapolated to 0 dB; or estimate from DC gm and CV-measured Cgg.
典型数值Typical numbers
14 nm FinFET 报道的 fT 约 314/285 GHz（n/p），见指南 §3。14 nm FinFET reports fT ≈ 314/285 GHz (n/p); see guide §3.

fmax（最高振荡频率）fmax (maximum oscillation frequency)

基础指标Core metrics

功率增益降到 1 的频率；比 fT 更受栅电阻和 Cgd 影响。Frequency where power gain falls to 1; more sensitive to gate resistance and Cgd than fT.

实际含义What it is
单向功率增益（Mason U）等于 1 时的频率，是器件还能做功率放大的最高频率。The frequency where unilateral power gain (Mason's U) equals 1 — the highest frequency at which the device can still amplify power.
表征什么What it tells you
射频能力，同时反映栅电阻 Rg、Cgd 和输出电导这些寄生。RF capability, reflecting parasitics such as gate resistance Rg, Cgd and output conductance.
数值越大 / 越小意味着什么Bigger vs. smaller
越大越适合射频。fmax 远低于 fT 通常说明栅电阻太大，GAA 的多层金属栅要特别注意这一点。Higher suits RF better. fmax far below fT usually means gate resistance is too high — a watch item for GAA's multi-layer metal gates.
打个比方Analogy
fT 是发动机转速，fmax 是车真正跑出来的速度；传动（栅电阻）漏得多，再高转速也白搭。fT is engine RPM; fmax is the speed the car actually reaches. If the drivetrain (gate resistance) leaks, high RPM is wasted.
怎么测How it is measured
S 参数计算 Mason U 或 MAG，外推到 0 dB。版图上双侧栅接触能明显改善。Compute Mason's U or MAG from S-parameters and extrapolate to 0 dB. Double-sided gate contacts improve it markedly.

Cgg / Cgd（栅电容）Cgg / Cgd (gate capacitances)

基础指标Core metrics

Cgg 是栅极看进去的总电容；Cgd 是栅到漏的跨接电容，会被 Miller 效应放大。Cgg is total capacitance seen at the gate; Cgd is gate-to-drain, multiplied by the Miller effect.

实际含义What it is
Cgg = Cgs + Cgd + Cgb（含本征和寄生部分）。Cgd 跨接在输入和输出之间。Cgg = Cgs + Cgd + Cgb, intrinsic plus parasitic. Cgd bridges input and output.
表征什么What it tells you
驱动这个器件要付出的“负载”，决定速度和功耗；Cgd 还决定反馈和稳定性。The load you pay to drive the device, setting speed and power; Cgd also sets feedback and stability.
数值越大 / 越小意味着什么Bigger vs. smaller
越小越好。Cgd/Cgg 比值高说明寄生占比大，fT 和 fmax 都会受损；GAA 的内侧墙和栅到源漏电容是重点。Smaller is better. A high Cgd/Cgg ratio means parasitics dominate and hurt both fT and fmax; GAA inner spacers and gate-to-S/D capacitance are the focus.
打个比方Analogy
像推车上的货：Cgg 是总重量，Cgd 是一根把你和后面车厢连起来的绳子，你一动它反拽你。Like cargo on a cart: Cgg is the total weight; Cgd is a rope tying you to the car behind, pulling back whenever you move.
怎么测How it is measured
CV 测量（分离 Cgs/Cgd 需要分端口连接），或从 S 参数转 Y 参数取虚部。CV measurements (split terminals to separate Cgs/Cgd), or from Y-parameter imaginary parts.

VT（阈值电压）VT (threshold voltage)

基础指标Core metrics

器件开始导通的栅压；模拟里更关心它的失配和随温度、应力的漂移。The gate voltage where the device turns on; analog cares more about its mismatch and drift with temperature and stress.

实际含义What it is
沟道表面形成反型层所需的栅压，常用恒流法或最大跨导外推法定义。The gate voltage needed to form the inversion layer, defined by constant-current or max-gm extrapolation.
表征什么What it tells you
开关点和偏置点的位置，以及多 VT 选项带来的设计自由度。The switching point and bias position, and the design freedom of multi-VT options.
数值越大 / 越小意味着什么Bigger vs. smaller
对模拟而言绝对值不是关键，关键是两个“相同”器件之间的 ΔVT（失配）越小越好，以及 VT 随温度变化可预测。低 VT 给更多电压裕量但漏电大。For analog the absolute value matters less than ΔVT between two ‘identical’ devices (smaller is better) and a predictable temperature drift. Low VT gives more headroom but more leakage.
打个比方Analogy
像门槛高度：门槛高低本身好商量，但一对双胞胎门槛高度不一样，就是麻烦。Like a doorstep height: the height itself is negotiable, but two ‘twin’ doors with different steps are a problem.
怎么测How it is measured
ID–VGS 扫描后用恒流法或 gm 最大点线性外推法提取；失配需要成对器件统计。Extract from ID–VGS by constant current or max-gm linear extrapolation; mismatch needs paired-device statistics.

SS（亚阈值摆幅）SS (subthreshold swing)

基础指标Core metrics

亚阈值区电流增大 10 倍需要的栅压，室温极限约 60 mV/dec。Gate voltage needed for a 10× current increase below threshold; ~60 mV/dec limit at room temperature.

实际含义What it is
SS = dVGS/d(log10 ID)，在弱反型区量。SS = dVGS/d(log10 ID), measured in weak inversion.
表征什么What it tells you
栅极静电控制的好坏，也直接对应弱反型 gm/ID 的上限。Quality of gate electrostatic control; it directly sets the weak-inversion gm/ID ceiling.
数值越大 / 越小意味着什么Bigger vs. smaller
越小越好，越接近 60 mV/dec 越理想；SS 小 → 弱反型 gm/ID 高 → 低功耗模拟更有利。GAA 报道约 65 mV/dec。Smaller is better, ideally near 60 mV/dec; low SS means higher weak-inversion gm/ID, good for low-power analog. GAA reports ≈ 65 mV/dec.
打个比方Analogy
像音量旋钮的灵敏度：转一格就让声音大十倍，转的角度越小越灵。Like a volume knob: the less you turn it for a 10× louder sound, the more sensitive it is.
怎么测How it is measured
ID–VGS 半对数图上取亚阈值段斜率的倒数。Inverse slope of the subthreshold region on a semilog ID–VGS plot.

ZTC（零温度系数点）ZTC (zero-temperature-coefficient point)

基础指标Core metrics

某个栅压下，电流几乎不随温度变化；可用作温度稳定的偏置点。A gate voltage where current barely changes with temperature; a temperature-stable bias point.

实际含义What it is
温度升高时 VT 下降（电流增加）而迁移率下降（电流减少），两者抵消的那个 VGS。As temperature rises, VT falls (more current) while mobility falls (less current); ZTC is the VGS where they cancel.
表征什么What it tells you
器件温度敏感性的平衡点。The balance point of the device's temperature sensitivity.
数值越大 / 越小意味着什么Bigger vs. smaller
偏置在 ZTC 附近，电流随温度变化最小；远离它则温漂大。imec 的 nanosheet 数据显示 25–200 °C 存在清晰的 ZTC。Biasing near ZTC minimizes current drift with temperature; far from it, drift grows. imec nanosheet data show a clear ZTC from 25 to 200 °C.
打个比方Analogy
像一杯冷热各半的水：一边升温一边降温，刚好温度不变。Like mixing hot and cold water so the temperature stays the same.
怎么测How it is measured
多个温度下测 ID–VGS，找各曲线的交点。Measure ID–VGS at several temperatures and find where the curves cross.

Ion/Ioff 与 CV/I（逻辑指标）Ion/Ioff and CV/I (logic metrics)

基础指标Core metrics

逻辑器件的核心指标：开态电流、关态漏电和门延迟；模拟器件不以它们为主。Core logic metrics: on-current, off-leakage and gate delay; analog does not lead with them.

实际含义What it is
Ion 是 VGS=VDS=VDD 时的电流，Ioff 是 VGS=0 时的漏电，CV/I 近似一级门延迟。Ion is current at VGS = VDS = VDD; Ioff is leakage at VGS = 0; CV/I approximates one gate delay.
表征什么What it tells you
大信号开关的速度和静态功耗。Large-signal switching speed and static power.
数值越大 / 越小意味着什么Bigger vs. smaller
Ion 越大越快，Ioff 越小越省电。但模拟器件工作在偏置点的小信号区，这两个数很少直接决定模拟性能。Higher Ion is faster, lower Ioff saves power. But analog works in small-signal mode at a bias point, so these rarely decide analog performance directly.
打个比方Analogy
逻辑器件是电灯开关，只关心开得快、关得严；模拟器件是调光旋钮，关心中间每一档有多稳。A logic device is a light switch — fast on, fully off; an analog device is a dimmer — every position in between must be stable.
怎么测How it is measured
在 VDD 下测 ID–VGS 端点；CV/I 用环形振荡器或 Ieff 估算。ID–VGS endpoints at VDD; CV/I from ring oscillators or Ieff.

四个问题的直接回答Four questions, answered directly

Q1“Drive 越大越好、Cdyn 越小越好，延迟就会降低”对吗？Is it right that more drive and less Cdyn lower delay?

方向对，三点要补上。

  • 比 Drive 要控制条件：同一 Ioff、同一面积（栅距、鳍/片数）下比。否则降 VT 或加宽都能“买”到电流。
  • 速度看的是 I/C 比，不是单独的 I 或 C。靠加宽得来的电流会带来同比例的自身电容；只有导线和扇出负载占大头时，加宽才明显变快。所以工艺开发里常看的 “I-drive 对 C” 图，本质上在看 CV/I 这条斜率。
  • 延迟里的 C 和 Cdyn 不是同一个数。延迟看的是单条路径、单次翻转的负载电容 C_load；Cdyn 是整颗芯片按翻转概率加权的电容，还和所跑程序有关。降寄生电容两边都受益，但幅度不同。

The direction is right; three qualifications.

  • Compare drive under controlled conditions: at the same Ioff and the same footprint (gate pitch, fin or sheet count). Otherwise lower VT or more width simply buys current.
  • Speed is about the I/C ratio, not I or C alone. Current bought with width brings proportional self-capacitance; widening pays only when wire and fan-out load dominate. The familiar “I-drive versus C” plot is really reading the CV/I slope.
  • The C in delay is not Cdyn. Delay uses the load capacitance of one path and one transition, C_load; Cdyn is the whole chip's capacitance weighted by switching probability, and it depends on the workload. Cutting parasitics helps both, by different amounts.
Q2Cdyn 和频率（effective frequency）是直接相连的吗？Is Cdyn directly tied to (effective) frequency?

定义上不相连，实际上通过三条路连在一起。Cdyn = (P − P_漏电)/(V²·f_eff) 已经把频率除掉了，理想情况下它不随频率变化。

  • 共享电容（直接路径）：如果降掉的电容在关键路径上（比如工艺上的低 k、空气侧墙），同电压下延迟直接变小、频率升高，而功耗基本不变（C 降、f 升相互抵消）。
  • 功耗预算（间接路径）：功耗受限的产品里 P 固定。Cdyn 降低后可以抬电压换频率，但要付 V² 的代价，所以频率收益只有 Cdyn 降幅的一部分：
    Δf/f ≈ −(ΔCdyn/Cdyn) / (1 + 2/s)，其中 s = d ln f / d ln V 是 V–F 曲线的对数斜率。s≈1 时约为 1/3，s≈2 时约为 1/2（忽略漏电；漏电随电压上升会让收益更小）。【推断】
  • 测量效应：Cdyn 要用 f_eff 计算。内存受限的程序在高频下有更多周期在等待，每周期的 Cdyn 反而下降；短路电流和毛刺也会让测得的 Cdyn 随电压、频率漂移。

公开例子：3 nm GAA 路径探索 PDK 上，空气侧墙使环振在等速度下有功功耗降 30%，在等功耗下频率只升 9%（Lee 等，IEEE Access 2022）。同一个改进，用功耗报和用频率报，数字差三倍左右。

Not by definition, but in practice through three routes. Cdyn = (P − P_leak)/(V²·f_eff) already divides out frequency, so ideally it does not change with f.

  • Shared capacitance (direct route): if the capacitance removed sits on the critical path (process-level low-k, air spacers), delay drops at the same voltage and frequency rises while power stays roughly flat (less C, more f).
  • Power budget (indirect route): in a power-limited product P is fixed. Lower Cdyn lets you raise voltage for frequency, but you pay V², so the frequency gain is a fraction of the Cdyn cut:
    Δf/f ≈ −(ΔCdyn/Cdyn) / (1 + 2/s), where s = d ln f / d ln V is the log slope of the V–F curve. About 1/3 for s≈1, about 1/2 for s≈2 (ignoring leakage; leakage rising with voltage shrinks the gain further). [Inference]
  • Measurement effects: compute Cdyn with f_eff. Memory-bound programs stall more cycles at high frequency, so Cdyn per cycle falls; short-circuit current and glitches also make measured Cdyn drift with V and f.

A public example: on a 3 nm GAA path-finding PDK, an air spacer cut ring-oscillator active power by 30% at iso-speed but raised frequency by only 9% at iso-power (Lee et al., IEEE Access 2022). The same improvement reads about three times larger when quoted as power.

Q3Cdyn 越小越好、降电阻电容能提升 AC 性能，对吗？Is smaller Cdyn always better, and does cutting R and C improve AC performance?

对一半：Cdyn 里只有 C，没有 R。

  • 降 C（不伤 Drive）：既提速又省电，是双赢。
  • 降 R（接触电阻、源漏电阻、导线电阻、IR 压降）：提升 Drive 和速度，但不降 Cdyn。
  • “越小越好”有前提：本征沟道电容 Cinv 是换来电流的“好电容”，减它会同时减电流；要减的是寄生部分（交叠/边缘、栅–接触、导线）。
  • R 和 C 常常互换：加厚侧墙 C 降 R 升；接触做小 C 降 R 升。判断标准是 Ieff/C_load 或环振的“延迟对 Cdyn”是否净改善。

Half right: Cdyn contains C only, not R.

  • Cutting C (without hurting drive): faster and lower power — a double win.
  • Cutting R (contact, source/drain, wire resistance, IR drop): more drive and speed, but no change in Cdyn.
  • “Smaller is better” has a condition: the intrinsic channel capacitance Cinv is the “good” capacitance that buys current; cutting it cuts current. Cut the parasitic parts (overlap/fringe, gate-to-contact, wires).
  • R and C often trade: thicker spacers cut C but raise R; smaller contacts cut C but raise R. The test is a net gain in Ieff/C_load, or in ring-oscillator delay versus Cdyn.
Q4Cdyn 是不是不光包含器件，后段所有部分都在里面？Does Cdyn cover more than the device — all of the back end too?

是的。产品层的 Cdyn 是全芯片口径：凡是每个周期被充放电的电容都在里面，再按各自的翻转率加权——器件本征栅电容、交叠/边缘、栅–接触和栅–外延（MOL）、结电容、所有金属层的导线（含线间耦合）、时钟网络、SRAM 位线等。

不计入的：去耦电容、MIM 电容、封装寄生——它们稳态不翻转（但会影响供电噪声）。测出来的 Cdyn 还会混进短路电流和毛刺。

注意口径：工艺层用环振测的“每级 Cdyn”只含器件、MOL 和局部导线，不含时钟网络和长线。拿工艺的 Cdyn 改进去推算产品功耗时，要按各部分占比折算。

Yes. Product-level Cdyn is a whole-chip quantity: every capacitance charged each cycle counts, weighted by its toggle rate — intrinsic gate capacitance, overlap and fringe, gate-to-contact and gate-to-epi (MOL), junctions, wires on every metal layer (including coupling), the clock network, SRAM bit lines and more.

Not counted: decoupling capacitors, MIM capacitors and package parasitics — they do not toggle in steady state (though they affect supply noise). Measured Cdyn also picks up short-circuit current and glitches.

Mind the scope: the process-level “Cdyn per stage” from ring oscillators covers only device, MOL and local wire, not the clock network or long wires. To project a process Cdyn gain onto product power, weight it by each part's share.

Drive / 驱动电流（Idsat、Ion）Drive current (Idsat, Ion)

速度与功耗：Drive 与 CdynSpeed & power: drive and Cdyn

晶体管全开时能推出多大电流，通常按每 µm 宽度归一化；越大，充放电越快。How much current a fully-on transistor delivers, usually per µm of width; more drive charges and discharges loads faster.

实际含义What it is
Idsat（或 Ion）是 VGS = VDS = VDD 时的漏电流，单位 µA/µm。比较工艺时必须在同一关态漏电 Ioff 下比，否则调低 VT 就能“作弊”换来更大电流。Idsat (or Ion) is the drain current at VGS = VDS = VDD, in µA/µm. Processes must be compared at the same off-state leakage Ioff; otherwise lowering VT buys current dishonestly.
表征什么What it tells you
晶体管给负载电容充放电的能力，是速度公式 τ ∝ C·V/I 里的分母。The transistor's ability to charge and discharge load capacitance — the denominator of τ ∝ C·V/I.
数值越大 / 越小意味着什么Bigger vs. smaller
在同 Ioff、同面积（同栅距）下越大越好。但靠加宽（多鳍、多片、更宽片）得来的电流会带来同比例的自身电容，只有负载主要来自导线和扇出时才划算；靠迁移率、应力、降低源漏电阻得来的电流才是“白赚”。Larger is better at the same Ioff and footprint (gate pitch). Current bought with width (more fins, more or wider sheets) brings proportional self-capacitance and only pays when wires and fan-out dominate the load; current from mobility, strain or lower source/drain resistance comes for free.
打个比方Analogy
像水泵的出水量：泵越猛，水桶（电容）灌得越快。但换更大的泵，泵自己的管道也更粗，要先灌满。Like a pump's flow rate: a stronger pump fills the bucket (capacitance) faster. But a bigger pump has bigger pipes of its own to fill first.
怎么测How it is measured
DC 参数测试：VDS = VDD 下扫 ID–VGS，读 VGS = VDD 时的电流，用 Kelvin 连接扣除接触压降；画 Ion–Ioff 散点，在固定 Ioff（如 1 或 10 nA/µm）处比较。DC parametric test: sweep ID–VGS at VDS = VDD, read the current at VGS = VDD, using Kelvin connections to remove contact drops; plot Ion versus Ioff and compare at a fixed Ioff (e.g., 1 or 10 nA/µm).

Ieff（有效驱动电流）Ieff (effective drive current)

速度与功耗：Drive 与 CdynSpeed & power: drive and Cdyn

反相器翻转过程中的“平均”电流：(IH + IL)/2；比 Idsat 更能预测门延迟。The average current during an inverter transition, (IH + IL)/2; predicts gate delay better than Idsat.

实际含义What it is
Na 等人（IEDM 2002）的定义：IH 取 VGS = VDD、VDS = VDD/2；IL 取 VGS = VDD/2、VDS = VDD；Ieff 是两者平均。原因是翻转过程中晶体管从不停在 Idsat 那一个偏置点上。Defined by Na et al. (IEDM 2002): IH at VGS = VDD, VDS = VDD/2; IL at VGS = VDD/2, VDS = VDD; Ieff is their average. During a transition the transistor never sits at the single Idsat bias point.
表征什么What it tells you
真实开关过程中的驱动能力；对中等栅压和线性区特性（DIBL、源漏电阻、迁移率退化）敏感。Drive under real switching conditions; sensitive to mid-gate-voltage and linear-region behavior (DIBL, source/drain resistance, mobility degradation).
数值越大 / 越小意味着什么Bigger vs. smaller
越大越好。Ieff/Idsat 的比值也有信息：DIBL 大或亚阈值摆幅差的器件 IL 掉得多，Ieff 会比 Idsat 看起来更差。Larger is better. The Ieff/Idsat ratio is informative too: devices with high DIBL or poor subthreshold swing lose IL, so Ieff looks worse than Idsat suggests.
打个比方Analogy
像看一辆车的“平均车速”而不是“最高车速”：真上路时大部分时间不在极速档。Like a car's average speed rather than its top speed: on a real trip you rarely sit at top speed.
怎么测How it is measured
在同一 DC 扫描里取 IH、IL 两个偏置点直接计算；再与环形振荡器测得的延迟做相关，检查 Ieff 能否解释延迟变化。Compute IH and IL from the same DC sweep, then correlate with ring-oscillator delay to check that Ieff explains the delay shifts.

门延迟 CV/IGate delay CV/I

速度与功耗：Drive 与 CdynSpeed & power: drive and Cdyn

一级门的延迟 ≈ 负载电容 × 电压 ÷ 驱动电流；速度链的核心公式。One gate's delay ≈ load capacitance × voltage ÷ drive current; the core formula of the speed chain.

实际含义What it is
把负载电容 C 充（或放）到约一半 VDD 所需的时间，τ ∝ C·VDD/Ieff。这里的 C 是这一级看到的全部负载：自身漏端电容、导线、下一级的栅电容。The time to charge (or discharge) load C to about half of VDD: τ ∝ C·VDD/Ieff. C is everything the stage sees: its own drain-side capacitance, the wire and the next gate.
表征什么What it tells you
工艺的“AC 性能”：同一电压下电路能跑多快。A process's AC performance: how fast circuits run at a given voltage.
数值越大 / 越小意味着什么Bigger vs. smaller
越小越快。三个旋钮：I 提高、C 降低、V 降低（但 V 降低时 I 掉得更多，通常反而更慢）。所以“I 越大、C 越小越好”成立，前提是比较时控制住 Ioff 和面积。Smaller is faster. Three knobs: raise I, cut C, lower V (but I falls faster than V, so lower V is usually slower). So “more I, less C” holds — provided Ioff and area are held fixed.
打个比方Analogy
用水管灌桶：时间 = 桶的容量（C）× 要灌的高度（V）÷ 水流量（I）。Filling a bucket with a hose: time = bucket size (C) × fill height (V) ÷ flow (I).
怎么测How it is measured
环形振荡器：测振荡频率 f，N 级环的单级延迟 τ = 1/(2N·f)；用不同扇出或金属负载的环振组拆出 C 和等效开关电阻。Ring oscillators: measure frequency f; an N-stage ring's per-stage delay is τ = 1/(2N·f); families with different fan-out or metal loads separate C from effective switching resistance.

负载电容 C_load / Ceff 与自负载Load capacitance (C_load / Ceff) and self-loading

速度与功耗：Drive 与 CdynSpeed & power: drive and Cdyn

一级门翻转时真正要充放电的电容：自身漏端 + 导线 + 下一级输入。The capacitance a gate actually charges when it switches: its own drain side, the wire and the next stage's input.

实际含义What it is
C_load = C_self（自身漏端的交叠、边缘、结电容）+ C_wire（导线）+ C_fanout（后级栅电容）。速度公式里的 C 就是它，针对的是单条路径、单次翻转。Ceff 常指翻转过程中按 ΔQ/ΔV 积分得到的等效值。C_load = C_self (own drain-side overlap, fringe and junction) + C_wire + C_fanout (next stages' gates). This is the C in the speed formula — one path, one transition. Ceff usually means the equivalent value integrated as ΔQ/ΔV over the transition.
表征什么What it tells you
这一级“背着多重的包”在跑；也决定加大器件尺寸值不值。How heavy a load the stage carries — and whether upsizing the device is worth it.
数值越大 / 越小意味着什么Bigger vs. smaller
越小越快。自负载占比越高，加宽器件越没用：电流和自身电容同比例增加，延迟几乎不变，功耗却上升；导线和扇出占比高时，加宽才有效。Smaller is faster. The higher the self-loading share, the less upsizing helps: current and self-capacitance grow together, delay barely moves and power rises; upsizing helps only when wire and fan-out load dominate.
打个比方Analogy
像搬家：箱子（外部负载）重时，换个更壮的人有用；如果累的主要是自己的体重（自负载），再壮也快不了多少。Like moving house: when the boxes (external load) are heavy, a stronger mover helps; when the burden is mostly the mover's own weight (self-loading), strength buys little.
怎么测How it is measured
对比 FO1/FO3/FO4 环振，或同一驱动后接不同长度的金属线，用频率和电流差值拆分；寄生提取（PEX）给出版图上每条线的电容。Compare FO1/FO3/FO4 ring oscillators, or one driver with different metal loads, and difference frequency and current; parasitic extraction (PEX) gives per-net capacitance from layout.

Cdyn（动态电容）Cdyn (dynamic capacitance)

速度与功耗：Drive 与 CdynSpeed & power: drive and Cdyn

平均每个时钟周期被充放电的电容：Cdyn = 动态功耗 ÷ (V²·f)；功耗链的核心量。The capacitance charged and discharged per clock cycle on average: Cdyn = dynamic power ÷ (V²·f); the core of the power chain.

实际含义What it is
Cdyn = Σ αᵢ·Cᵢ：芯片上每个节点的电容乘以它每周期翻转的概率 α 再求和。实际中常反过来算：Cdyn = (P_总 − P_漏电)/(V²·f_eff)。它是“芯片 + 所跑程序”的属性，同一颗芯片跑不同程序，Cdyn 不同。Cdyn = Σ αᵢ·Cᵢ: every node's capacitance times its per-cycle switching probability α, summed. In practice it is backed out as Cdyn = (P_total − P_leak)/(V²·f_eff). It belongs to chip plus workload: the same chip has a different Cdyn on each program.
表征什么What it tells you
每做一个周期的活要搬多少电荷。它把功耗里与电压、频率无关的部分单独拿出来，方便跨电压、跨频率比较工艺和设计。How much charge each cycle of work moves. It strips voltage and frequency out of power so processes and designs can be compared across V and f.
数值越大 / 越小意味着什么Bigger vs. smaller
完成同样工作量时越小越好：同频同压下功耗按比例下降，或者在同功耗下换来更高的电压和频率。但 Cdyn 小也可能只是芯片没在干活（α 低、IPC 低），所以要按每条指令或每单位工作量来看。Smaller is better for the same work: power falls proportionally at the same V and f, or the budget buys higher voltage and frequency. But a low Cdyn can also mean the chip is idling (low α, low IPC), so normalize per instruction or per unit of work.
打个比方Analogy
像一栋楼里每小时开关灯的总瓦数：灯多（C 大）、开关勤（α 高）都会让电表转得快；它和电价（V²）、营业时长（f）分开记账。Like the wattage of lights switched per hour in a building: more lamps (C) or more frequent switching (α) spin the meter faster; it is booked separately from the tariff (V²) and opening hours (f).
怎么测How it is measured
产品层：固定 V 和 f 跑代表性负载，测电源电流，扣除同温度下的漏电（可在停钟时测），再除以 V²·f_eff。工艺层：环振的 (IDDA − IDDQ)/(VDD·f) 给出每级 Cdyn。设计阶段用 PEX 加门级仿真的翻转率估算。Product level: run representative workloads at fixed V and f, measure supply current, subtract leakage at the same temperature (e.g., with clocks stopped), then divide by V²·f_eff. Process level: (IDDA − IDDQ)/(VDD·f) on a ring oscillator gives Cdyn per stage. During design, estimate from PEX plus gate-level toggle rates.

活动因子 α（翻转率）Activity factor α (toggle rate)

速度与功耗：Drive 与 CdynSpeed & power: drive and Cdyn

一个节点每个时钟周期完成一次充放电的概率；时钟线 α = 1，普通逻辑常在 0.1 量级。The probability a node completes a charge–discharge each clock cycle; α = 1 for clocks, on the order of 0.1 for ordinary logic.

实际含义What it is
Cdyn = Σ α·C 里的 α。常见两种口径（只数 0→1 翻转，或数所有翻转），比较时要统一。它由电路结构、数据和程序共同决定。The α in Cdyn = Σ α·C. Two conventions exist (counting 0→1 transitions only, or all transitions), so compare like with like. It depends on circuit structure, data and the program.
表征什么What it tells you
电容里有多少“真的在动”；也是设计侧降 Cdyn 的主要抓手（时钟门控、操作数隔离、减少毛刺）。How much of the capacitance actually moves — and the main design-side lever on Cdyn (clock gating, operand isolation, fewer glitches).
数值越大 / 越小意味着什么Bigger vs. smaller
完成同样工作量时越低越省电；但如果 α 低是因为核心在等内存，那是空转，不是高效。Lower saves power for the same work; but a low α because the core is waiting on memory is idling, not efficiency.
打个比方Analogy
一栋楼里灯很多（C），但每小时真正开关的比例（α）才决定电费。A building may have many lamps (C), but the fraction actually switched each hour (α) sets the bill.
怎么测How it is measured
仿真：门级或 RTL 仿真记录每个网络的翻转次数（SAIF/VCD 文件）。硅片：测得的 Cdyn 除以总电容得到平均 α；片上活动计数器可实时估算。Simulation: count toggles per net in gate-level or RTL simulation (SAIF/VCD files). Silicon: divide measured Cdyn by total capacitance for an average α; on-chip activity counters estimate it in real time.

动态功耗 P = Cdyn·V²·f（含短路功耗）Dynamic power P = Cdyn·V²·f (incl. short-circuit power)

翻转一次耗能 C·V²，每秒 f 次：P = Cdyn·V²·f；再加上上下管同时导通时的短路电流。Each switch costs C·V², f times a second: P = Cdyn·V²·f, plus short-circuit current while pull-up and pull-down conduct together.

实际含义What it is
给电容充电时从电源取能 C·V²：一半存进电容，一半耗在上拉管上；放电时存的那一半耗在下拉管上。总功耗 = 动态 + 短路 + 漏电。短路和毛刺不是电容，但测出来会被算进 Cdyn。Charging a capacitor draws C·V² from the supply: half is stored, half burned in the pull-up; discharging burns the stored half in the pull-down. Total power = dynamic + short-circuit + leakage. Short-circuit and glitch power are not capacitance, but they land in measured Cdyn.
表征什么What it tells you
功耗随电压平方涨、随频率线性涨。所以降电压最省电；提频率最费电，因为提频率通常也要提电压。Power grows with V² and linearly with f. Lowering voltage saves the most; raising frequency costs the most, because it usually needs more voltage too.
数值越大 / 越小意味着什么Bigger vs. smaller
越低越好。沿 V–F 曲线升频时电压也要升；如果频率和电压近似成正比，功耗大约按 f³ 上涨。Lower is better. Climbing the V–F curve raises voltage too; if frequency is roughly proportional to voltage, power grows roughly like f³.
打个比方Analogy
像反复给气球打气再放掉：每次打进去的气量 ∝ C·V，打气的压力 ∝ V，所以每次耗能 ∝ C·V²；每秒打 f 次。Like inflating and releasing a balloon over and over: each fill holds air ∝ C·V at a pressure ∝ V, so each cycle costs ∝ C·V², f times a second.
怎么测How it is measured
在若干 (V, f) 点测总电流；停钟测漏电并扣除；剩余功耗对 V²·f 作图，斜率就是 Cdyn。短路功耗可用改变输入边沿速率的环振估计。Measure total current at several (V, f) points; stop the clocks to measure and subtract leakage; plot the remaining power against V²·f — the slope is Cdyn. Short-circuit power can be estimated with ring oscillators that vary input slew.

现代 CPU 的频率随负载、温度和功耗上限实时变化（睿频、降频、睡眠态）。MPERF 在 C0 态按基准频率计数，APERF 按实际频率计数；两者增量之比乘以基准频率，就是这段时间的有效频率。Modern CPU clocks shift constantly with load, temperature and power limits (turbo, throttling, sleep states). MPERF counts at the reference frequency in C0, APERF at the actual frequency; the ratio of their increments times the reference frequency is the interval's effective frequency.
表征什么What it tells you
把测到的功耗换算成 Cdyn 时要除的那个 f；也是“产品实际跑多快”的度量。The f to divide by when turning measured power into Cdyn — and a measure of how fast the product actually ran.
数值越大 / 越小意味着什么Bigger vs. smaller
越高说明越多时间跑在高频。算 Cdyn 时要用 f_eff：用标称频率而实际在降频，Cdyn 会被算小；把睡眠时间也算进去，会被算得更小。Higher means more time at high clocks. Use f_eff when computing Cdyn: dividing by the nominal frequency while the part throttles understates Cdyn, and folding in sleep time understates it further.

像车的平均车速：路牌写的是限速，实际要看里程 ÷ 行驶时间（停车时间不算）。Like a car's average speed: the sign shows the limit, but the truth is distance ÷ driving time (parked time excluded).

芯片在每个电压下能稳定跑到的最高频率；它是 Drive 链和 Cdyn 链的连接点。The highest stable frequency at each voltage; the hinge between the drive chain and the Cdyn chain.

实际含义What it is
电压越高，Ieff 越大、门延迟越小，能跑的频率越高；Vmin 是保证功能正确的最低电压。DVFS 就是沿这条曲线上下移动工作点。Higher voltage gives more Ieff, shorter gate delay and higher achievable frequency; Vmin is the lowest functional voltage. DVFS moves the operating point along this curve.
表征什么What it tells you

数值越大 / 越小意味着什么Bigger vs. smaller

打个比方Analogy

等功耗 / 等频率比较（iso-power、iso-frequency）Iso-power vs iso-frequency comparisons

实际含义What it is
同一组晶体管和电容改进，可以兑现成频率，也可以兑现成功耗。功耗随 V² 变化：同频率下降电压能省很多电；同功耗下提频率要付 V² 的代价，所以频率收益的百分比小于功耗收益。The same transistor and capacitance gains can be cashed in as frequency or as power. Since power scales with V², lowering V at iso-frequency saves a lot, while raising frequency at iso-power pays the V² toll, so the frequency gain in percent is smaller.
表征什么What it tells you
把 Drive 和 Cdyn 的改进折算成产品语言；也是判断一项改动值不值的口径。It translates drive and Cdyn improvements into product terms — and is the yardstick for whether a change is worth it.
数值越大 / 越小意味着什么Bigger vs. smaller

打个比方Analogy
像换了一辆更轻的车：可以油耗不变跑得快一点，也可以速度不变省很多油；省油的百分比看起来总是更大。Like a lighter car: same fuel, a bit faster; or same speed, a lot less fuel — the fuel saving always looks bigger.
怎么测How it is measured

环形振荡器（RO）Ring oscillator (RO)

速度与功耗：Drive 与 CdynSpeed & power: drive and Cdyn

奇数级反相器首尾相接自激振荡；同时测频率和电流，就能得到工艺的门延迟和每级 Cdyn。An odd chain of inverters looped into self-oscillation; measuring frequency and current together yields gate delay and Cdyn per stage.

实际含义What it is
N 级环振的周期是 2N 个门延迟：τ = 1/(2N·f)。振荡时的电源电流 IDDA 减去停振电流 IDDQ，就是充放电电流，于是每级电容 C = (IDDA − IDDQ)/(N·VDD·f)。An N-stage ring's period is 2N gate delays: τ = 1/(2N·f). Oscillating supply current IDDA minus the stopped current IDDQ is the switching current, so per-stage capacitance is C = (IDDA − IDDQ)/(N·VDD·f).
表征什么What it tells you
工艺 AC 性能的标尺：在同一结构上同时看到速度（τ）、电容（C）以及两者的取舍。The yardstick for a process's AC performance: speed (τ), capacitance (C) and their trade-off on one structure.
数值越大 / 越小意味着什么Bigger vs. smaller
τ 越小越快，C 越小越省。常画“延迟对每级 Cdyn”或“延迟对功耗”的图，越靠左下越好。只降 τ 却让 C 上升（如加宽器件），未必是好改动。Smaller τ is faster, smaller C is thriftier; plots of delay versus Cdyn per stage, or delay versus power, are better toward the lower left. A τ cut bought with more C (e.g., wider devices) is not necessarily a win.
打个比方Analogy
像一圈人传话：一圈传完的时间告诉你每人反应多快，大家耗的力气告诉你每人要喊多大声。Like a whisper passed around a circle: the round time tells you each person's reaction speed; the total effort tells you how loudly each had to shout.
怎么测How it is measured

Families with different fan-out, metal loads and stacks split C into gate, diffusion and wire parts (Bhushan et al., IEEE TSM 2006).

实际含义What it is

表征什么What it tells you

数值越大 / 越小意味着什么Bigger vs. smaller

表征什么What it tells you
FEOL 结构里“白交的电费”；也加在漏端的自负载上。The FEOL's wasted charge; it also adds to the self-load at the drain.
数值越大 / 越小意味着什么Bigger vs. smaller

打个比方Analogy

像城市道路：路网越密，车流（信号）越互相干扰；隔离带（低 k、气隙）让相邻车道互不影响。Like a city's roads: the denser the grid, the more traffic (signals) interferes; medians (low-k, air gaps) keep adjacent lanes apart.
怎么测How it is measured
梳状–蛇形线结构测线间电容；带金属负载的环振；BEOL 统计模型和寄生提取（PEX）与硅片对标。Comb–serpentine structures for line-to-line capacitance; ring oscillators with metal loads; BEOL statistical models and PEX correlated to silicon.
典型数值Typical numbers

时钟网络（clock tree）Clock network (clock tree)

速度与功耗：Drive 与 CdynSpeed & power: drive and Cdyn

把时钟送到每个触发器的网络；每周期必翻转（α = 1），是 Cdyn 的大户。The network that delivers the clock to every flip-flop; it switches every cycle (α = 1) and is a major Cdyn consumer.

实际含义What it is
由全局时钟网格或时钟树、局部缓冲器和触发器的时钟输入组成。和数据线不同，它的活动因子固定为 1，而且线长、缓冲器多。A global grid or tree, local buffers and flip-flop clock pins. Unlike data nets, its activity factor is fixed at 1, with long wires and many buffers.
表征什么What it tells you
设计侧的“底噪” Cdyn：哪怕没做有用的计算，时钟也在耗电。Design-side baseline Cdyn: the clock burns power even when no useful work happens.
数值越大 / 越小意味着什么Bigger vs. smaller
越小越好。时钟门控把空闲模块的时钟关掉，是最有效的降 Cdyn 手段之一。Smaller is better. Clock gating stops the clock to idle blocks — one of the most effective Cdyn reductions.
打个比方Analogy
像全楼的广播：不管有没有人听，每层都在响；关掉没人的楼层就省电了。Like a building-wide PA system: every floor plays whether or not anyone listens; switch off the empty floors and you save power.
怎么测How it is measured
空闲负载（数据活动很少）下测得的 Cdyn 近似时钟加常开逻辑；设计中由时钟树综合报告给出时钟电容。Cdyn measured on an idle workload (little data activity) approximates the clock plus always-on logic; in design, clock-tree synthesis reports the clock capacitance.
典型数值Typical numbers
【硅片·研究】0.13 µm 处理器中时钟网约占动态功耗 40%（局部约 29%、全局约 13%），而时钟网只占约 1% 的网络、4% 的布线长度（Magen 等，SLIP 2004）。[Silicon · research] In a 0.13 µm processor clock nets took ~40% of dynamic power (local ~29%, global ~13%) while being ~1% of nets and ~4% of routing length (Magen et al., SLIP 2004).

互连电阻与 RC 延迟（含电阻屏蔽）Interconnect resistance and RC delay (incl. resistive shielding)

速度与功耗：Drive 与 CdynSpeed & power: drive and Cdyn

导线电阻让信号沿线变慢；它不属于 Cdyn，但和电容一起决定线延迟。Wire resistance slows signals along the line; it is not part of Cdyn, but with capacitance it sets wire delay.

实际含义What it is
长线延迟 ≈ 0.4·R_wire·C_wire（分布式 RC）+ 驱动电阻 × 总电容。电阻还会“屏蔽”远端电容：驱动器看到的有效电容小于导线总电容，但总延迟仍随电阻增加。供电网络的电阻造成 IR 压降，降低晶体管实际拿到的电压和驱动。Long-wire delay ≈ 0.4·R_wire·C_wire (distributed RC) + driver resistance × total capacitance. Resistance also shields far-end capacitance: the driver sees less than the total, yet total delay still grows with resistance. Power-grid resistance causes IR drop, reducing the voltage and drive the transistor actually gets.
表征什么What it tells you
金属线越细越长电阻越大；先进节点里电阻上升得比电容快。Thinner, longer wires mean more resistance; at advanced nodes resistance rises faster than capacitance.
数值越大 / 越小意味着什么Bigger vs. smaller
越小越好，但降电阻不会降 Cdyn。所以“降 RC”提升 AC 性能，“降 Cdyn”要靠降 C。背面供电（BSPDN）减小 IR 压降、腾出正面布线，两方面同时帮忙。Smaller is better, but lower R does not lower Cdyn. Cutting RC helps AC performance; cutting Cdyn is about C. Backside power (BSPDN) cuts IR drop and frees front-side routing, helping on both counts.
打个比方Analogy
像细长的水管：管子越细越长，水越慢流到另一头；桶（电容）没变，但灌满要更久。Like a long, thin hose: the thinner and longer it is, the slower water reaches the far end; the bucket (capacitance) is unchanged, but filling takes longer.
怎么测How it is measured
Kelvin 四端法测线电阻和通孔链电阻；长线环振或延迟链测线延迟；片上电压监测电路测 IR 压降。Four-terminal Kelvin measurement of line and via-chain resistance; long-wire ring oscillators or delay chains for wire delay; on-die voltage monitors for IR drop.
典型数值Typical numbers
【常识】分布式 RC 线的 50% 延迟约 0.38·R·C（Elmore 近似给 0.5·R·C）。【厂商】据报道 Intel 18A 的 PowerVia 背面供电使最坏情况电压跌落比 Intel 3 降低至多 10 倍。[Textbook] A distributed RC line's 50% delay is ≈ 0.38·R·C (Elmore gives 0.5·R·C). [Vendor] Intel 18A's PowerVia backside power is reported to cut worst-case voltage droop by up to 10× versus Intel 3.
