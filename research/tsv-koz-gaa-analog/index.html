3D IC 的 TSV KOZ
与 GAA Analog 器件TSV keep-out zones
& GAA analog devices

KOZ 不是一个固定的禁区半径。它由热机械应力、衬底噪声耦合、TSV 对走线的耦合和 Cu pumping 四种机制分别决定，前段器件和后段金属的规则不同；analog 的判据比 digital 严一个量级，KOZ 通常是 digital 的 3 倍以上。A keep-out zone is not one fixed radius. Thermo-mechanical stress, substrate noise coupling, TSV-to-wire coupling and Cu pumping each set their own limits, with different rules for front-end devices and back-end metal. Analog criteria are ten times tighter than digital, so analog KOZs are usually at least three times larger.

KOZ 由什么决定

KOZ 的本意是 TSV 周围不放有源器件的区域，针对的是前段；后段金属并不整体禁用，只是 TSV 正上方和紧邻处有单独的 via 与走线规则。KOZ 的大小也不是工艺常数，而是由机制、器件类型和允许的漂移量共同决定。

机制 物理来源 影响对象 作用范围 常用对策
热机械应力 Cu 与 Si 的 CTE 相差约 7 倍（16.7 对 2.3 ppm/°C），退火后冷却留下残余应力 前段：迁移率、Id、gm；在匹配对上表现为系统失配 单根 TSV 近似按 1/r² 衰减，数 µm 到十几 µm；阵列叠加后更远 缩小 TSV 直径；阵列 pitch/直径大于 5；器件相对 TSV 方位对称
衬底噪声耦合 TSV、liner、Si 构成 MOS 电容，TSV 上的信号经衬底调制器件的 body 和 drain 前段：analog 的噪声和电流调制 衰减比应力慢；planar 器件按 2% 判据约 28 µm 就近接地的 substrate contact（28 µm 降到约 8 µm）；guard ring（100 MHz 下约 −17 dB）；厚 liner（100 MHz 下约 −20 dB）
TSV 对走线的耦合 TSV 与邻近金属线之间的电容 后段：敏感走线的噪声和延时 主要在 10 µm 内，20 µm 外可忽略 布线层单独设 KOZ；接地的 guard ring 走线

来源：imec IEDM 2010 报道、Guo 等 IEDM 2013、Lu 等 TDMR 2012、Rack 等 ECTC 2015、Cho 等 CPMT 2011、Peng 等 DAC 2014、GlobalFoundries 专利 US10199315。

Analog 的 KOZ 为什么更大

  • 判据严一个量级。 imec 对 digital 用 5% 的 Id 变化，对 analog 用 0.5%。按 1/r² 衰减，判据收紧 10 倍，KOZ 半径约为原来的 3 倍，面积约为 10 倍。
  • 长沟道器件更敏感。 短沟道器件受接入电阻和短沟道效应限制，电流对迁移率变化不敏感；analog 常用的长 L 器件正好相反。
  • 匹配看的是梯度而不是绝对值。 TSV 周围径向是拉应力、切向是压应力，大小相近、符号相反。同一对器件相对 TSV 的方位不同，电流会一个增大、一个减小。

KOZ 不是定值的另外两个原因

  • 阵列叠加。 pitch/直径小于 5 时相邻 TSV 的应力场相互作用。imec 在 TSV 矩阵旁得到的 analog KOZ 为 20–200 µm，取决于矩阵里 TSV 的数量。
  • 随寿命和个体变化。 NIST 用同步辐射 X 射线测得：1000 次热循环后，Cu TSV 内的主应力和剪应力约为初始的 5 倍；相邻两根 TSV 的静水应力也因微结构不同而相差约 50%（99±30 对 151±37 MPa）。两篇都建议 KOZ 规则把这些变化算进去。来源：Okoro 等 ECTC 2014、Okoro 等 TED 2014。

TSV 剖面上的影响区

前段的应力 KOZ 按判据分两档，analog 一档更宽，digital 器件可以放进 analog 的禁区。后段只在 TSV 正上方和紧邻的走线处受限。噪声耦合走衬底，靠就近接地的 substrate contact 截断。

公开数据里的 KOZ 量级

公开数据里 analog 的 KOZ 从约 6 µm（FinFET 配 3 µm TSV）到 200 µm（planar 配 5 µm TSV 矩阵），跨两个量级。差别主要来自 TSV 直径、沟道长度、阵列规模和判据。

判据收紧时 KOZ 怎么变

Guo 等给出一个简化关系：单根 TSV 的 KOZ 半径等于 K/δId 的平方根，其中 K 是由 TSV 工艺和器件应力响应决定的品质因子。他们在 5 µm TSV、L = 900 nm 的长沟道 FinFET 上得到 K 约为 110（n 型）和 130（p 型）%·µm²。下表是用这两个 K 值算出的半径，距离指 TSV 中心到器件中心；5% 和 0.5% 是论文用的判据，1% 和 0.1% 是补充的示例。

允许的 Id 变化 n 型 KOZ 半径（µm） p 型 KOZ 半径（µm）
5%（digital） 4.7 5.1
1% 10.5 11.4
0.5%（analog） 14.8 16.1
0.1%（精密匹配） 33.2 36.1

这组数只对孤立的单根 TSV 成立，阵列旁会更大。同一模型下 KOZ 面积与 TSV 直径的平方成正比，5 µm 缩到 3 µm 面积约减小到三分之一。

GAA 器件的情况

针对 nanosheet 的公开结果目前是 TCAD，没有找到硅片数据。Jeong 等 IEEE Access 2021（只读到摘要）对比了 sub-5 nm 的 NSFET 与 FinFET，结论有三条：

  • TSV 应力引起的 Ion 变化，NSFET 比 FinFET 大，主要来自沟道长度方向的应力分量。
  • 器件放在 KOZ 之外时，应力影响可忽略。
  • 噪声耦合按 analog 判据（耦合电流小于 Ion 的 0.5%）衡量时，常规结构不达标；作者建议在 S/D 下方加 bottom oxide 并用不掺杂的 punch-through stopper。

不同 3D 结构下的差别

TSV 从微米级缩到纳米级后，局部应力 KOZ 随直径平方缩小，但减薄、背面金属和失去衬底带来的全局效应变成主要问题。所以在 GAA 加背面供电的工艺上，只看 TSV 周围的禁区是不够的。

减薄本身带来的两个全局代价

  • 散热变差。 Si 的热导率约 140 W/(m·K)，SiO₂ 约 1.4。衬底被减薄或去掉后横向散热变差，imec 仿真得到背面供电的峰值温度比正面供电高约 14 °C；另一组仿真显示 Si 薄于 300 nm 后自热明显加重。来源：Semiconductor Engineering。
  • 依赖衬底的器件变弱。 imec 把 Si 从 700 µm 减薄到 300 nm 后，ESD 器件的失效电流 It2 下降 20–40%，其中 STI 二极管在 100 ns TLP 下下降约 36%。来源：imec，IEDM 2023 结果。

GAA 下 analog 器件要关注的特性

从公开数据看，GAA 本身对 analog 是利好：增益更高，1/f 噪声更低，对衬底噪声更不敏感。要重点盯的是四件事：应力响应与 FinFET 不同、自热更重、依赖衬底的器件在减薄后变弱、版图相关效应更强。

量测方法

定 KOZ 最可靠的依据是器件电学响应，不是应力图。应力计量用来校准仿真，器件和电路级的测试结构用来定规则。

建议的做法

以下是基于上面文献给出的建议，不是某一篇文献的结论。核心思路是：用器件自己的应力响应加距离阶梯数据拟合出 K，再按每类电路允许的漂移量反推 KOZ。

  1. 把 KOZ 拆成三条规则分别定。 前段应力 KOZ 按器件类别给（digital、一般 analog、精密匹配）；噪声 KOZ 按 TSV 类型给，快沿信号 TSV 比电源和地 TSV 严；后段的 via 与走线规则单列。
  2. 先测 GAA analog 器件自己的压阻系数。 用四点弯曲，覆盖 n 和 p、长短 L、不同 sheet 宽度。导电晶面和 FinFET 不同，系数不能沿用。
  3. 用距离阶梯结构拟合 K。 TSV 排布覆盖单根、成排和产品实际 pitch 的阵列；器件方位覆盖沟道指向 TSV 和垂直于 TSV 两种；参考器件放在距阵列 200 µm 以外。
  4. 匹配对单独验证。 把匹配对做成相对 TSV 等距同方位和不对称两种摆法，比较平均偏移。样本量要足够把系统偏移从随机失配里分出来。
  5. 条件覆盖温度、寿命和封装。 应力大小取决于与无应力参考温度的温差，所以要测高低温；热循环后复测；堆叠和封装后再测一次，因为 microbump 的影响可以比 TSV 还大。
  6. 噪声耦合用 S 参数结构定。 分组对比 substrate contact 距离、有无 guard ring、有无 bottom dielectric isolation。
  7. 依赖衬底的器件列为单独的验证项。 ESD 二极管和 GGNMOS 用 TLP 对比减薄前后；bandgap 用的二极管或 BJT、well 电阻也做同样的对比。
  8. 关键 analog 器件加四端栅结构测自热。 对比减薄前后和堆叠前后的热阻。

KOZ 判据怎么定

从电路指标倒推。先确定某类电路能分给 TSV 的系统失配预算，比如电流镜允许 0.5% 的电流偏差；再用实测的 K 算出单根 TSV 的半径；阵列旁按叠加效应放大，并留出热循环和个体差异的余量。对精密匹配电路，对称摆放比单纯拉大距离更省面积。

What sets the KOZ

A keep-out zone is the area around a TSV where no active devices are placed, so it applies to the front end. Back-end metal is not banned wholesale; only the area directly above the TSV and its immediate neighborhood carry separate via and routing rules. The size of a KOZ is not a process constant either: it depends on the mechanism, the device type and how much drift the circuit can tolerate.

Mechanism Physical origin What it affects Range Common mitigations
Thermo-mechanical stress Cu and Si CTE differ by about 7× (16.7 vs 2.3 ppm/°C); cooling after anneal leaves residual stress Front end: mobility, Id, gm; shows up as systematic mismatch in matched pairs Roughly 1/r² decay for a single TSV, a few µm to over 10 µm; farther once arrays superpose Smaller TSV diameter; array pitch/diameter above 5; place devices symmetrically relative to the TSV
Substrate noise coupling TSV, liner and Si form a MOS capacitor; signals on the TSV modulate device body and drain through the substrate Front end: analog noise and current modulation Decays more slowly than stress; about 28 µm for planar devices at a 2% criterion Nearby grounded substrate contact (28 µm down to about 8 µm); guard ring (about −17 dB at 100 MHz); thick liner (about −20 dB at 100 MHz)
TSV-to-wire coupling Capacitance between the TSV and nearby metal lines Back end: noise and delay on sensitive routes Mostly within 10 µm; negligible beyond 20 µm Routing-layer KOZ; grounded guard-ring wires
Cu pumping At high temperature Cu extrudes axially, domes the TSV top and pushes up the layers above Back end: V0 stress voiding, M1 dishing, dielectric cracking and delamination Directly above the TSV and near its top corners No vias in several layers directly above the TSV; smaller diameter

Why the analog KOZ is larger

  • The criterion is ten times tighter. imec uses a 5% Id change for digital and 0.5% for analog. With 1/r² decay, a 10× tighter criterion gives a radius about 3× larger and an area about 10× larger.
  • Long-channel devices are more sensitive. In short-channel devices, access resistance and short-channel effects make current less sensitive to mobility; the long-L devices common in analog behave the opposite way.
  • Matching depends on the gradient, not the absolute value. Around a TSV the radial stress is tensile and the tangential stress is compressive, similar in magnitude and opposite in sign. If the two devices of a pair sit at different orientations to the TSV, one current goes up while the other goes down.

Two more reasons the KOZ is not a fixed number

  • Array superposition. Below a pitch/diameter ratio of 5, stress fields of neighboring TSVs interact. Next to TSV arrays, imec found analog KOZs of 20–200 µm depending on how many TSVs the array held.
  • Change over lifetime and from via to via. NIST synchrotron X-ray measurements show principal and shear stress in Cu TSVs about 5× higher after 1,000 thermal cycles, and hydrostatic stress differing by about 50% between two neighboring TSVs (99±30 vs 151±37 MPa) because of microstructure. Both papers recommend building these variations into KOZ rules. Sources: Okoro et al. ECTC 2014, Okoro et al. TED 2014.

Zones in a TSV cross-section

The front-end stress KOZ comes in two tiers set by the criterion; the analog tier is wider, and digital devices may sit inside the analog zone. The back end is restricted only directly above the TSV and on nearby routes. Noise couples through the substrate and is cut off by a nearby grounded substrate contact.

KOZ sizes in published data

Published analog KOZs range from about 6 µm (FinFET with a 3 µm TSV) to 200 µm (planar devices next to a 5 µm TSV array), two orders of magnitude. TSV diameter, channel length, array size and the criterion explain most of the spread.

How the KOZ grows as the criterion tightens

Guo et al. give a simplified relation: the KOZ radius of a single TSV is the square root of K/δId, where K is a figure of merit set by the TSV process and the device's stress response. On a 5 µm TSV and L = 900 nm long-channel FinFETs, they measured K of about 110 (n-type) and 130 (p-type) %·µm². The table below applies those K values; distances run from TSV center to device center. 5% and 0.5% are the paper's criteria; 1% and 0.1% are added examples.

Allowed Id change n-type KOZ radius (µm) p-type KOZ radius (µm)
5% (digital) 4.7 5.1
1% 10.5 11.4
0.5% (analog) 14.8 16.1
0.1% (precision matching) 33.2 36.1

These numbers hold only for an isolated TSV; next to arrays the zone is larger. In the same model, KOZ area scales with the square of TSV diameter, so going from 5 µm to 3 µm cuts the area to about a third.

GAA devices

Published nanosheet results are TCAD so far; no silicon data was found. Jeong et al. IEEE Access 2021 (abstract only) compared sub-5 nm NSFETs with FinFETs and reached three conclusions:

  • TSV stress shifts Ion more in NSFETs than in FinFETs, mainly through the stress component along the channel length.
  • Outside the KOZ, the stress impact is negligible.
  • Judged by an analog criterion (coupling current below 0.5% of Ion), conventional structures fail on noise coupling; the authors recommend a bottom oxide under source/drain and an undoped punch-through stopper.

How 3D structures differ

As TSVs shrink from micrometers to nanometers, the local stress KOZ shrinks with the square of the diameter, but global effects from thinning, backside metal and the loss of the substrate take over. On a GAA process with backside power, looking only at zones around TSVs is not enough.

Two global costs of thinning

  • Worse heat spreading. Si conducts about 140 W/(m·K), SiO₂ about 1.4. Thinning or removing the substrate reduces lateral spreading; imec simulations put backside-power peak temperature about 14 °C above frontside power, and another simulation shows self-heating rising sharply below 300 nm of Si. Source: Semiconductor Engineering.
  • Weaker substrate-dependent devices. When imec thinned Si from 700 µm to 300 nm, ESD failure current It2 dropped 20–40%, with STI diodes down about 36% under 100 ns TLP. Source: imec, IEDM 2023 results.

Measurement methods

The most reliable basis for a KOZ is the device's electrical response, not a stress map. Stress metrology calibrates simulation; device- and circuit-level test structures set the rules.

One easily missed problem with Raman: it reports only the sum of radial and tangential stress, and these two components are similar in size and opposite in sign, so they largely cancel. A quiet Raman map does not mean the individual stress components seen by devices are small (a judgment based on the measurement principle).

Recommended approach

These recommendations build on the literature above; they are not the conclusion of any single paper. The core idea: fit K from the device's own stress response plus distance-ladder data, then derive the KOZ from the drift each circuit class can tolerate.

  1. Split the KOZ into three rules. Set the front-end stress KOZ by device class (digital, general analog, precision matching); set the noise KOZ by TSV type, stricter for fast-edge signal TSVs than for power and ground; keep back-end via and routing rules separate.
  2. Measure piezoresistive coefficients on GAA analog devices first. Use four-point bending across n and p, short and long L, and several sheet widths. Conduction surfaces differ from FinFET, so FinFET coefficients do not carry over.
  3. Fit K with distance-ladder structures. Cover single TSVs, rows and arrays at the product pitch; cover channels pointing toward and perpendicular to the TSV; put reference devices more than 200 µm from any array.
  4. Verify matched pairs separately. Lay out pairs both equidistant with the same orientation to the TSV and asymmetric, and compare mean offsets. Use enough samples to separate systematic offset from random mismatch.
  5. Cover temperature, lifetime and packaging. Stress scales with the difference from the stress-free reference temperature, so measure hot and cold; re-measure after thermal cycling; measure again after stacking and packaging, since microbump effects can exceed those of TSVs.
  6. Set noise rules with S-parameter structures. Split on substrate-contact distance, with and without guard rings, and with and without bottom dielectric isolation.
  7. Treat substrate-dependent devices as a separate check. Compare ESD diodes and GGNMOS with TLP before and after thinning; do the same for bandgap diodes or BJTs and well resistors.
  8. Add four-terminal gate structures to key analog devices to measure self-heating. Compare thermal resistance before and after thinning and stacking.

Setting the KOZ criterion

Work back from circuit specs. First decide how much systematic mismatch a circuit class can give to TSVs, for example 0.5% current error in a current mirror; then compute the single-TSV radius from measured K; enlarge it for array superposition and add margin for thermal cycling and via-to-via variation. For precision matching, symmetric placement saves more area than simply moving farther away.

  • Sizes and KOZ values for pass-through and die-to-die TSVs. Public process pages do not give them.
